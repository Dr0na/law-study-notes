# Constitutional Law Subject Template

## Purpose

This template defines the mandatory structure for all Constitutional Law study material generated by the system.

Applicable subjects include:

* Constitutional Law I
* Constitutional Law II
* Indian Constitution
* Constitutional Governance
* Fundamental Rights
* Union-State Relations
* Constitutional Amendments
* Constitutional Doctrines

This template is optimized for:

* SPPU LLB examinations
* 5 Marks questions
* 10 Marks questions
* 15 Marks questions
* Revision preparation
* NotebookLM visual generation

---

# Constitutional Law Generation Principles

Constitutional Law answers must emphasize:

* Constitutional provisions
* Articles
* Amendments
* Doctrines
* Landmark judgments
* Constitutional philosophy
* Judicial interpretation

Unlike Contract Law, Constitutional Law answers must always be article-centric and doctrine-centric.
