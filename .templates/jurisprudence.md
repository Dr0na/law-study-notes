# Jurisprudence Generation Principles

Jurisprudence is fundamentally different from statute-centric subjects.

The focus must be on:

* Legal theories
* Schools of thought
* Jurists
* Philosophical foundations
* Comparative viewpoints
* Criticism
* Evolution of ideas

Unlike Constitutional Law or Contract Law, jurisprudence answers must emphasize:

```text
Thinkers

↓

Theories

↓

Comparisons

↓

Criticism

↓

Modern Relevance
```
