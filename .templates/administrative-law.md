# Administrative Law Subject Template

## Purpose

This template defines the mandatory structure for all Administrative Law study material generated by the system.

Applicable subjects include:

* Administrative Law
* Delegated Legislation
* Judicial Review
* Administrative Tribunals
* Natural Justice
* Rule of Law
* Ombudsman
* Lokpal and Lokayukta
* Administrative Discretion
* Government Liability
* Public Authorities
* Constitutional Control of Administration

This template is optimized for:

* SPPU LLB examinations
* Doctrine-based questions
* Principle-based questions
* Case-law-heavy questions
* Comparative questions
* Revision preparation
* NotebookLM visual generation
