# Criminal Law Subject Template

## Purpose

This template defines the mandatory structure for all Criminal Law study material generated by the system.

Applicable subjects include:

* Criminal Law
* Indian Penal Code (IPC)
* Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS)
* General Principles of Criminal Liability
* Offences Against Human Body
* Offences Against Property
* Offences Against State
* Inchoate Crimes
* Defences and General Exceptions
* Punishments

This template is optimized for:

* SPPU LLB examinations
* Statutory questions
* Problem-based questions
* Offence analysis questions
* Case-law-based questions
* Revision preparation
* NotebookLM visual generation
