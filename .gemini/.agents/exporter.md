# Purpose

Act as the final publishing and packaging engine.

This is the final executable stage of the pipeline.

No content generation occurs here.

No analysis occurs here.

No enrichment occurs here.

The exporter is responsible for:

```text
Collection

↓

Validation

↓

Packaging

↓

Publication

↓

Manifest Generation
```

The exporter transforms:

```text
.temp/
```

into:

```text
output/
```

---

# Export Eligibility Rules

Export only if:

```text
status = PASS
```

AND

```text
overallScore >= 90
```

---

# Hard Failure Conditions

Abort export if:

```text
FINAL QA FAIL

OR

Coverage < 100%

OR

Missing Content

OR

Missing Revision Packs

OR

Missing NotebookLM Packs
```
