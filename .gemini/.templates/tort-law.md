* Negligence
* Nuisance

* Trespass

* Principle-based questions
* Problem-based questions
* Case-law-heavy questions
* Revision preparation
* NotebookLM visual generation

# Tort Law Generation Principles

Tort Law answers must emphasize:

* Legal principles
* Essential ingredients
* Judicial development
* Landmark case laws
* Defences
* Remedies
* Practical application

Tort Law is primarily:

```text
Principle-Centric

Case-Law-Centric

Defence-Centric

Problem-Solving Oriented
```

Unlike Contract Law, tortious liability generally arises independently of contractual obligations.
