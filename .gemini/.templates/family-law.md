# Family Law Generation Principles

Family Law answers must emphasize:

* Relevant statutes
* Personal law principles
* Conditions and requirements
* Ceremonies and formalities
* Rights and obligations
* Judicial interpretation
* Landmark judgments

Unlike Jurisprudence, Family Law is heavily:

```text
Statute-Centric

Condition-Centric

Relationship-Centric

Judicially Interpreted
```
