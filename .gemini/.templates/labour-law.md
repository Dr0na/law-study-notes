# Labour Law Subject Template

## Purpose

This template defines the mandatory structure for all Labour Law study material generated by the system.

Applicable subjects include:

* Labour Law
* Industrial Law
* Industrial Relations
* Trade Unions
* Industrial Disputes
* Social Security Laws
* Occupational Safety
* Wages and Bonus Laws
* Factories Law
* Standing Orders
* Labour Welfare Legislation
* Labour Codes

This template is optimized for:

* SPPU LLB examinations
* Statutory questions
* Problem-based questions
* Industrial relations questions
* Revision preparation
* NotebookLM visual generation

---

# Labour Law Generation Principles

Labour Law answers must emphasize:

* Statutory framework
* Employee rights
* Employer obligations
* Industrial relations
* Welfare measures
* Dispute resolution mechanisms
* Judicial interpretation

Labour Law is primarily:

```text
Statute-Centric

Rights-Centric

Industrial-Relations-Centric

Welfare-Oriented
```

Every answer must identify:

* Relevant legislation
* Relevant provisions
* Rights and obligations
* Enforcement mechanisms
