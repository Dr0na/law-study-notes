Property Law answers must emphasize:

* Statutory provisions
* Transfer mechanisms
* Ownership rights
* Conditions and requirements
* Legal consequences
* Judicial interpretation
* Landmark judgments

Property Law is primarily:

```text
Transfer-Centric

Ownership-Centric

Section-Centric

Rights-Centric
```

Every answer must identify:

* Relevant statute
* Relevant section
* Nature of transfer
* Legal effect
