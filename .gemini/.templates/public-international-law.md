# Public International Law Generation Principles

Public International Law answers must emphasize:

* International legal principles
* Sources of law
* Treaties and conventions
* International institutions
* State practice
* Judicial decisions
* International customs

Public International Law is primarily:

```text
Principle-Centric

Treaty-Centric

Institution-Centric

State-Practice-Centric
```

Every answer must identify:

* Relevant international source
* Applicable treaty or convention
* Governing principle
* International judicial position
