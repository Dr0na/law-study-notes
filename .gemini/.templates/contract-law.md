# Contract Law Subject Template

## Purpose

This template defines the mandatory structure for all Contract Law study material generated by the system.

Applicable subjects include:

* Law of Contract I
* Law of Contract II
* Special Contracts
* Indemnity
* Guarantee
* Bailment
* Pledge
* Agency
* Quasi Contract
* Sale of Goods (where applicable)

This template is optimized for:

* SPPU LLB examinations
* Statutory answer writing
* Section-based questions
* Problem-based questions
* Revision preparation
* NotebookLM visual generation

---

# Contract Law Generation Principles

Contract Law answers must emphasize:

* Statutory provisions
* Sections
* Essentials
* Elements
* Conditions
* Exceptions
* Rights and Duties
* Landmark judgments

Unlike Constitutional Law, Contract Law is primarily:

```text
Section-Centric

Essentials-Centric

Problem-Oriented
```

Every answer must clearly identify the relevant statutory provision.
