# 📊 Hohfeldian Jural Relations — skeleton

| ⚖️ Right-side position | 🤝 Correlative | ❌ Opposite |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Claim (right)** | Duty | No-right |
| **Privilege (liberty)** | No-right | Duty |
| **Power** | Liability | Disability |
| **Immunity** | Disability | Liability |

## Quick worked examples

- **Claim ⇄ Duty:** A’s contractual right to delivery; B’s duty to deliver.
- **Privilege ⇄ No-right:** Walking on one’s own land; the world has no right to stop.
- **Power ⇄ Liability:** Parliament’s power to tax; taxpayers’ liability to be taxed.
- **Immunity ⇄ Disability:** Diplomatic immunity; prosecuting state’s disability.
