# 📊 Comparison Tables - Jurisprudence

## 1. Law vs Morality

| Basis | ⚖️ Law | 🧠 Morality |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Source** | Created by the State/Legislature. | Derived from conscience, religion, or society. |
| **Enforcement** | Enforced by external sanctions (Fines, Prison). | Enforced by internal sanctions (Guilt, Social shame). |
| **Nature** | Objective and certain. | Subjective and varies by individual. |
| **Scope** | Regulates external conduct only. | Regulates both internal thoughts and external acts. |
| **Goal** | Maintains social order and justice. | Aims for individual perfection. |

💡 **Exam Tip**: Use this table for short notes on "Law and Morality." Mention that modern law is often "morality with a tooth."

## 2. Ownership vs Possession

| Basis | 🏠 Ownership | 📦 Possession |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Legal Status** | *De Jure* (By Right). | *De Facto* (In Fact). |
| **Nature** | Absolute and ultimate right. | Physical control and intention to exclude. |
| **Duration** | Permanent (unless transferred). | Temporary (can be lost easily). |
| **Proof** | Title deeds and legal documents. | Physical presence and control. |
| **Protection** | Protected by proprietary remedies. | Protected by possessory remedies (to prevent violence). |

💡 **Exam Tip**: Remember the maxim "Possession is nine-tenths of the law."

## 3. Natural Law vs Legal Positivism

| Basis | 🌿 Natural Law | 📜 Legal Positivism |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Focus** | Law as it *ought* to be (Moral). | Law as it *is* (Fact). |
| **Validity** | Depends on conformity with morality/reason. | Depends on the source/authority (Sovereign). |
| **Key Maxim** | *Lex iniusta non est lex* (Unjust law is no law). | *Dura lex sed lex* (The law is hard, but it is the law). |
| **Source** | Divine, Nature, or Reason. | Human will and state authority. |
| **Thinkers** | Aquinas, Locke, Fuller. | Austin, Hart, Kelsen. |

💡 **Exam Tip**: This is a classic 15-mark question. Use this table to structure your comparison.
