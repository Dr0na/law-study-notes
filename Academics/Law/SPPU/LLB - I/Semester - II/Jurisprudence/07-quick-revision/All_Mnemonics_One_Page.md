# 🧠 All Mnemonics Cheat Sheet - Jurisprudence

Use these mnemonics for rapid recall during the exam!

## 📘 Module 01: Introduction
- **USA-H**: **U**lpian, **S**almond, **A**ustin, **H**olland (Key jurists).

## 📘 Module 02: Sources of Law
- **A-C-R-C-C**: **A**ntiquity, **C**ontinuity, **R**easonableness, **C**ertainty, **C**ompulsory (Essentials of Custom).
- **R-O**: **R**atio (Binding) vs **O**biter (Persuasive).

## 📘 Module 03: Natural Law
- **H-L-R**: **H**obbes (Security), **L**ocke (Rights), **R**ousseau (General Will).
- **L-L-P**: **L**ife, **L**iberty, **P**roperty (Locke's Natural Rights).

## 📘 Module 04: Legal Positivism
- **C-S-S-D**: **C**ommand, **S**overeign, **S**anction, **D**uty (Austin's Law).
- **P-S**: **P**rimary + **S**econdary Rules (Hart).

## 📘 Module 05: Other Schools
- **S-E**: **S**ocial **E**ngineering (Roscoe Pound).
- **S-C**: **S**tatus to **C**ontract (Sir Henry Maine).
- **V-G**: **V**olk**g**eist (Savigny).

## 📘 Module 06: Person, Rights & Duties
- **F-R-B**: **F**iction, **R**ealist, **B**racket (Theories of Personality).
- **R-P-P-I**: **R**ight, **P**rivilege, **P**ower, **I**mmunity (Hohfeld).

## 📘 Module 07: Ownership & Possession
- **C-A**: **C**orpus (Body) + **A**nimus (Mind) = Possession.
- **V-C**: **V**ested (Certain) vs **C**ontingent (Conditional).

## 📘 Module 08: Liability
- **S-A-V**: **S**trict, **A**bsolute, **V**icarious (Special Liabilities).
- **D-S-I vs I-S-D**: **D**amnum **S**ine **I**njuria vs **I**njuria **S**ine **D**amnum.
