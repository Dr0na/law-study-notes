# ⚡ Doctrines and Maxims - Jurisprudence

## 📜 Key Legal Maxims

| Maxim | Meaning | Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Ubi Jus Ibi Remedium* | Where there is a right, there is a remedy. | Rights and Duties |
| *Stare Decisis* | To stand by things decided. | Precedent |
| *Ratio Decidendi* | Reason for the decision. | Precedent |
| *Obiter Dicta* | Things said by the way. | Precedent |
| *Lex Iniusta Non Est Lex* | An unjust law is not a law. | Natural Law |
| *Salus Populi Est Suprema Lex* | The welfare of the people is the supreme law. | Sociological School |
| *Respondeat Superior* | Let the superior answer. | Vicarious Liability |
| *Qui Facit Per Alium Facit Per Se* | He who acts through another acts himself. | Vicarious Liability |
| *Damnum Sine Injuria* | Damage without legal injury. | Liability |
| *Injuria Sine Damnum* | Legal injury without damage. | Liability |
| *Nemo Dat Quod Non Habet* | No one can give what they do not have. | Ownership/Title |

## 🏛️ Key Legal Doctrines

1.  **Doctrine of Stare Decisis**: Courts must follow previous decisions.
2.  **Doctrine of Prospective Overruling**: New rules apply only to future cases.
3.  **Doctrine of Separate Legal Entity**: A company is a separate person (*Salomon*).
4.  **Doctrine of Strict Liability**: Liability without fault for dangerous escapes.
5.  **Doctrine of Absolute Liability**: No-exception liability for hazardous industries.
6.  **Doctrine of Volkgeist**: Law reflects the spirit of the people.
7.  **Doctrine of Social Engineering**: Law balances competing interests.
