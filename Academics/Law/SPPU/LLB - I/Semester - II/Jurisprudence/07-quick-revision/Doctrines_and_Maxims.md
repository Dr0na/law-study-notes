## Doctrines

- **Separation thesis** — *is* and *ought* are distinct enquiries.
- **Overlap thesis** — law and morality are conceptually linked.
- **Inner morality of law (Fuller)** — eight desiderata.
- **Minimum content of natural law (Hart)** — five truisms.
- **Volksgeist** — Savigny — law as spirit of the people.
- **Bad-man theory (Holmes)** — law is a prediction of what courts do.
- **Social engineering (Pound)** — law balances interests.
- **Stare decisis** — vertical (Art. 141) and horizontal (Bengal Immunity).
- **Doctrine of prospective overruling** — *Golak Nath* (1967).
- **Doctrine of basic structure** — *Kesavananda Bharati* (1973) — Indian natural-law equivalent.
- **Enterprise liability (M.C. Mehta)** — absolute and non-delegable for hazardous activity.
