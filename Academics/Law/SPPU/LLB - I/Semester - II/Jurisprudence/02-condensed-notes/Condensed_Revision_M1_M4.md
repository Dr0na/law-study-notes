# 📄 Condensed Revision: Modules 01 - 04

## 📘 Module 01: Introduction
- **Jurisprudence**: "Eye of Law", "Formal Science" (Holland), "First Principles of Civil Law" (Salmond).
- **USA-H**: Ulpian, Salmond, Austin, Holland.
- **Law vs Morality**: Law = External/State; Morality = Internal/Conscience.
- **Classification**: Public/Private, Substantive/Procedural, Municipal/International.

## 📘 Module 02: Sources of Law
- **Custom**: Antiquity, Continuity, Reasonableness, Certainty, Compulsory (**A-C-R-C-C**).
- **Legislation**: Supreme (Parliament) vs Subordinate (Delegated).
- **Precedent**: Stare Decisis, Art 141 (SC binding), Ratio Decidendi (Reason), Obiter Dicta (By the way).
- **Volkgeist**: Spirit of the people (Savigny).

## 📘 Module 03: Natural Law
- **Classical**: Aquinas (Eternal, Natural, Divine, Human).
- **Social Contract**:
  - **Hobbes**: Leviathan, Security, "Nasty, brutish, short".
  - **Locke**: Life, Liberty, Property (**L-L-P**), Right to revolt.
  - **Rousseau**: General Will.
- **Revival**:
  - **Lon Fuller**: 8 Principles of Internal Morality.
  - **John Finnis**: 7 Basic Goods.

## 📘 Module 04: Legal Positivism
- **Bentham**: Utilitarianism (Greatest Happiness).
- **Austin**: Command + Sovereign + Sanction + Duty (**C-S-S-D**).
- **Hart**: Primary Rules (Duties) + Secondary Rules (Change/Recognition).
- **Kelsen**: Pure Theory, Grundnorm (Basic Norm), Hierarchy of Norms.
