# ⚡ Condensed — Modules 04–05 (Positivism, Schools, CLS)

## Module 04 — Legal Positivism

- **Order — BAHK:** Bentham → Austin → Hart → Kelsen.
- **Bentham:** Expositorial (descriptive) + Censorial (utilitarian critique). *“Natural rights — nonsense upon stilts.”*
- **Austin — CSSD:** Command + Sovereign + Sanction + Duty. Sovereign = habitual obedience, not habitually obedient.
- **Hart:** Primary (duty) + Secondary (power) rules; **RCA** = Recognition, Change, Adjudication. Rule of recognition tested by **internal point of view**. Open texture. Minimum content of natural law.
- **Kelsen:** Pure Theory; **Stufenbau** pyramid; **Grundnorm** presupposed; *dynamic* over static. Used in *Dosso* (Pakistan) for revolutions.
- **Comparison cue:** Austin = command; Hart = rules + recognition; Kelsen = norms + Grundnorm.

## Module 05 — Sociological / Realist / Historical / CLS

### Sociological — Roscoe Pound

- **Social engineering** of competing interests.
- **Interests (3) — IPS:** Individual, Public, Social.
- **5 jural postulates** of civilised society.

### American Realism — HLF

- **Holmes** — *“life of the law is experience”*, bad-man theory.
- **Llewellyn** — rule-skepticism; UCC drafting.
- **Frank** — fact-skepticism; *Law and the Modern Mind* (1930).

### Historical school

- **Savigny** — **Volksgeist**; opposed codification.
- **Maine** — *"from status to contract"*; *Ancient Law* (1861).
