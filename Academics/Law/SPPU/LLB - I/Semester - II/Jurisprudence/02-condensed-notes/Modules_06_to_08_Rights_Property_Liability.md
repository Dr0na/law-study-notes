# ⚡ Condensed — Modules 06–08 (Rights, Property, Liability)

## Module 06 — Person, Rights, Duties

- **Person** = entity capable of rights/duties (natural + legal).
- **Difficult cases:** animals (not persons), dead (limited statutory protection), unborn (contingent rights — *S.13 TPA*).
- **Legal persons (3) — CIF:** Corporations, Institutions/idols, Funds/estates.
- **Theories of personality (5) — FCRBP:** Fiction, Concession, Realist, Bracket, Purpose.
- **Right characteristics (5) — SAOPT:** Subject, Act, Object, Person of incidence, Title.
- **Theories of rights (3):** Will (Kant/Hegel/Savigny) → Interest (Ihering/Salmond) → Composite.
- **Hohfeld — CPPI:** Claim ⇄ Duty; Privilege ⇄ No-right; Power ⇄ Liability; Immunity ⇄ Disability.

## Module 07 — Ownership / Possession / Title

- **Ownership characteristics (5) — UUPRI:** Indefinite User, Unrestricted disposition, Possess, Residuary, Inheritable.
- **Modes of acquisition (4) — ODIP:** Original, Derivative, Inheritance, Prescription.
- **Possession — CA:** Corpus + Animus possidendi.
- **Kinds of possession:** mediate/immediate, de facto/de jure, adverse, constructive.
- **Possessory remedies:** *Sections 5 & 6, Specific Relief Act, 1963*; trespass.
- **Ownership vs Possession:** *de jure* vs *de facto*; title-based vs possessory remedies.
- **Title (4) — OD-VD:** Original, Derivative, Vestitive, Divestitive facts.

## Module 08 — Obligations & Liability

- **Sources of obligation (4) — CTQI:** Contract, Tort, Quasi-contract, Innominate.
- **Conditions of liability (4) — AMCD:** Act/omission, Mens rea, Cause, Damage.
- **Civil vs Penal:** compensation vs punishment; preponderance vs beyond reasonable doubt.
- **Strict liability** — *Rylands v Fletcher* (1868); 5 classical defences.
- **Absolute liability** — *M.C. Mehta v Union of India* (1987); **no defences**.
- **Vicarious (4):** Master–servant, Principal–agent, Partners, State/employer.
- **Corporate criminal liability** — *Standard Chartered* (2005); *Iridium v Motorola* (2011); punishable by fine; mens rea via directing minds.
