# 📖 START HERE - Jurisprudence Study Path

Jurisprudence is the "eye of the law." To master it, you need to understand the *why* behind the *what*. Follow this guided path to prepare for your SPPU exams.

## 🏁 Phase 1: The Foundation (Days 1-2)
- Read **Module 01** and **Module 02** in `01-complete-notes/`.
- Understand the basic meaning of law and where it comes from (Sources).
- **Key Task**: Memorize the kinds of Precedents and the conditions for a valid Custom.

## 🏛️ Phase 2: The Schools of Thought (Days 3-5)
- Study **Modules 03, 04, and 05**. This is the heart of Jurisprudence.
- Compare Natural Law with Positivism.
- Use the **Comparison Tables** in `03-visual-aids/` to keep the thinkers and their theories clear.
- **Key Task**: Master Austin's Command Theory and Kelsen's Grundnorm.

## 🧩 Phase 3: Legal Concepts (Days 6-8)
- Dive into **Modules 06, 07, and 08**. These are the building blocks of legal practice.
- Focus on Rights (Hohfeld), Ownership vs. Possession, and Liability.
- **Key Task**: Draw the Hohfeldian table of jural correlatives and opposites.

## 📝 Phase 4: Exam Drill (Days 9-10)
- Go through the **Most Probable Questions** in `05-question-bank/`.
- Practice writing at least two 15-mark answers using the **Answer Writing Template**.
- Review the **Case Law Digest** to ensure you have 2-3 cases for every major topic.

## ⚡ Phase 5: Final Blitz (Last 24 Hours)
- Scan the **Condensed Notes**.
- Review the **All Mnemonics Cheat Sheet**.
- Check the **Doctrines and Maxims** list.

Good luck with your studies!
