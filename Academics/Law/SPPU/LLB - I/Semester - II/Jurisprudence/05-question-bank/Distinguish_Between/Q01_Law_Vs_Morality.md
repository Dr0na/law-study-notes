# Q01: Law vs Morality

## 📝 Question
Distinguish between Law and Morality. (5 Marks)

---

## 🧾 MODEL ANSWER

### 1. 🧾 Answer Synopsis
- **Definition**: Law (State-enforced) vs Morality (Conscience-enforced).
- **Key Differences**: Sanction, Scope, Objectivity.
- **Conclusion**: Interdependence.

### 2. 🧠 Introduction
Law and Morality are both systems of social control. While they often overlap (e.g., both forbid theft), they differ in their origin, enforcement, and scope.

### 3. 📖 Body (Comparison Table)

| Basis | ⚖️ Law | 🧠 Morality |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Enforcement** | Enforced by the State. | Enforced by individual conscience. |
| **Sanction** | External (Fine, Prison). | Internal (Guilt, Shame). |
| **Scope** | Regulates external conduct. | Regulates both thoughts and acts. |
| **Certainty** | Objective and written. | Subjective and varies. |
| **Change** | Can be changed overnight by legislation. | Evolves slowly over generations. |

### 4. ⚖️ Case Laws
- **Queen v. Dudley and Stephens**: Highlighted that while an act might be "morally" understandable (survival), it remains "legally" a crime (murder).

### 5. 🧾 Conclusion
Law and Morality are like two circles that intersect. Law provides the "minimum" standard of conduct for order, while Morality provides the "ideal" standard for human perfection.
