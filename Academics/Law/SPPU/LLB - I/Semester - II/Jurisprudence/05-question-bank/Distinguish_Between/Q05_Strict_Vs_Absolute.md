# Q05: Strict vs Absolute Liability

## 📝 Question
Distinguish between Strict Liability and Absolute Liability. (5 Marks)

---

## 🧾 MODEL ANSWER

### 1. 🧾 Answer Synopsis
- **Strict**: English law (*Rylands*), has exceptions.
- **Absolute**: Indian law (*M.C. Mehta*), no exceptions.
- **Key Differences**: Exceptions, Type of Industry.

### 2. 🧠 Introduction
While both theories impose liability without fault, Absolute Liability is a more stringent version developed in India.

### 3. 📖 Body (Comparison Table)

| Basis | ⚠️ Strict Liability | 🔥 Absolute Liability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Origin** | *Rylands v. Fletcher* (1868). | *M.C. Mehta v. UOI* (1987). |
| **Exceptions** | Has exceptions (Act of God, etc.). | **No exceptions** whatsoever. |
| **Scope** | Applies to "non-natural" use of land. | Applies to "hazardous/inherently dangerous" industries. |
| **Damages** | Compensatory. | Exemplary (higher based on the size of the company). |

### 4. 🧾 Conclusion
Absolute Liability was created because the exceptions in Strict Liability allowed large corporations to escape responsibility for major industrial disasters.
