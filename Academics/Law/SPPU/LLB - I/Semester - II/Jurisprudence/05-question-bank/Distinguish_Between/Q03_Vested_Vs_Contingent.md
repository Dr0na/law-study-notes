# Q03: Vested vs Contingent Ownership

## 📝 Question
Distinguish between Vested and Contingent Ownership. (5 Marks)

---

## 🧾 MODEL ANSWER

### 1. 🧾 Answer Synopsis
- **Vested**: Immediate and certain.
- **Contingent**: Conditional and uncertain.
- **Key Differences**: Possession, Inheritability, Conditions.

### 2. 🧠 Introduction
Ownership can be classified based on when the right becomes absolute.

### 3. 📖 Body (Comparison Table)

| Basis | 🏠 Vested Ownership | 🎲 Contingent Ownership |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Certainty** | The right is certain and immediate. | The right depends on an uncertain event. |
| **Condition** | No condition or a condition that *will* happen (e.g., death). | Depends on a condition that *may* happen (e.g., marriage). |
| **Inheritability** | It is heritable (passes to heirs). | It is not heritable until it becomes vested. |
| **Example** | A gift of property to X immediately. | A gift to X "if he graduates from law school." |

### 4. 🧾 Conclusion
Vested ownership is a present right, whereas contingent ownership is a mere "hope" or "expectancy" until the condition is fulfilled.
