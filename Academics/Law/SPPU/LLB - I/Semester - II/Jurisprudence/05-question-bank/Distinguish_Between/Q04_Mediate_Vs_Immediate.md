# Q04: Mediate vs Immediate Possession

## 📝 Question
Distinguish between Mediate and Immediate Possession. (5 Marks)

---

## 🧾 MODEL ANSWER

### 1. 🧾 Answer Synopsis
- **Immediate**: Direct physical control.
- **Mediate**: Control through an intermediary.
- **Examples**: Owner/Tenant, Master/Servant.

### 2. 🧠 Introduction
Possession can be held directly or through another person.

### 3. 📖 Body (Comparison Table)

| Basis | 📦 Immediate Possession | 🤝 Mediate Possession |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Control** | Direct physical control by the person. | Control through another person (agent/tenant). |
| **Relationship** | The person himself holds the object. | The person holds the object through an intermediary. |
| **Example** | A tenant living in a house. | The landlord who owns the house. |
| **Nature** | Primary possession. | Secondary/Derivative possession. |

### 4. 🧾 Conclusion
In many cases, two people can have possession of the same object at the same time—one mediate and one immediate.
