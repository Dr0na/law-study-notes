## 👔 Q8. Discuss the rights and duties of an agent towards his principal.

### 1. Introduction
The relationship between an agent and a principal is fiduciary in nature, built on trust and confidence. The Indian Contract Act, 1872 (Sections 211-223) lays down a detailed framework of duties the agent owes to the principal, and corresponding rights the agent can claim.

### 2. Duties of an Agent Towards the Principal

#### I. Duty to Follow Directions (Sec 211)
An agent is bound to conduct the business of the agency according to the directions given by the principal. In the **absence of any directions**, the agent must follow the custom prevailing in the same kind of business at the place where the agent conducts business.
- 🛑 **Consequence of disobedience:** The agent is liable for any loss to the principal.

#### II. Duty to Work with Reasonable Skill and Diligence (Sec 212)
The agent must act with as much skill as is generally possessed by persons engaged in similar business, unless the principal has notice of the agent's lack of skill.

#### III. Duty to Render Proper Accounts (Sec 213)
An agent must render proper accounts to the principal on demand.

#### IV. Duty to Communicate in Difficulty (Sec 214)
It is the duty of an agent, in cases of difficulty, to use all reasonable diligence in communicating with the principal and in seeking to obtain instructions.

#### V. Duty Not to Deal on Own Account (Sec 215)
If an agent, without the knowledge of the principal, deals in the business of the agency on their own account, the principal may:
- Repudiate the transaction if the case shows the agent dealt on their own account.
- Claim from the agent any benefit that may have resulted from the transaction.

#### VI. Duty Not to Make Secret Profits (Sec 216)
An agent must pay the principal all sums received on their account. If the agent makes any **secret profit** beyond the agreed remuneration, the principal is entitled to claim it.
- The principal can also dismiss the agent for breach of fiduciary duty.

#### VII. Duty to Pay Sums Received (Sec 218)
All monies received by the agent on behalf of the principal must be duly handed over.

### 3. Rights of an Agent

#### I. Right of Retainer (Sec 217)
An agent may retain, out of any sums received on account of the principal, all monies due to the agent in respect of:
- Advances made to the principal.
- Expenses properly incurred in conducting the business.

#### II. Right to Remuneration (Sec 219)
Unless there is a contract to the contrary, an agent is entitled to receive **agreed remuneration** or, in the absence of an agreement, reasonable remuneration for services rendered. No remuneration is payable for misconduct (Sec 220).

#### III. Right of Lien (Sec 221)
In the absence of a contract to the contrary, an agent has the right to retain goods, papers, and other property of the principal, until the amount due to the agent for commission, disbursements, and services is paid.

#### IV. Right to be Indemnified Against Lawful Acts (Sec 222)
The employer (principal) is bound to indemnify the agent against the consequences of all **lawful acts** done by the agent in exercise of the authority conferred.

#### V. Right to be Indemnified Against Acts Done in Good Faith (Sec 223)
The principal must indemnify the agent against the consequences of acts done in **good faith**, even if those acts cause loss to third parties.
- ⚠️ No indemnity for acts done that are **criminal in nature** (Sec 224).

### 4. Conclusion
The duties of an agent are essentially fiduciary — demanding loyalty, transparency, and accountability. In return, the agent's rights of retainer, lien, remuneration, and indemnification ensure that they are fairly compensated and protected for the risks undertaken on behalf of the principal.
