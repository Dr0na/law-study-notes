### ⚖️ Q3. Distinguish between Sale and Agreement to Sell.
**(Refer to Section 4 of Sale of Goods Act, 1930)**

| 📊 Basis | 🛒 Sale | ⏳ Agreement to Sell |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Transfer of Property**| Ownership passes **immediately**. | Ownership passes at a **future time**. |
| **Nature** | Executed Contract. | Executory Contract. |
| **Risk of Loss** | Risk falls on the **Buyer**. | Risk falls on the **Seller**. |
| **Breach by Buyer** | Seller can sue for the **price**. | Seller can only sue for **damages**. |
| **Breach by Seller**| Buyer can sue third party for goods. | Buyer can only sue seller for **damages**. |
| **Seller's Insolvency**| Buyer can recover goods from receiver. | Buyer cannot recover goods, only dividend. |
