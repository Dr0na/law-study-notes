### ⚖️ Q4. Distinguish between Condition and Warranty.
**(Refer to Section 12 of Sale of Goods Act, 1930)**

| 📊 Basis | ❗ Condition | ⚠️ Warranty |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Meaning** | A stipulation essential to the main purpose of the contract. | A stipulation collateral to the main purpose of the contract. |
| **Effect of Breach** | Buyer can repudiate (cancel) the contract and claim damages. | Buyer can only claim damages, cannot reject goods. |
| **Treatment** | A breach of condition can be treated as a breach of warranty. | A breach of warranty cannot be treated as a breach of condition. |
