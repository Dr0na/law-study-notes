### ⚖️ Q2. Distinguish between Bailment and Pledge.
**(Refer to Sections 148 & 172 of ICA 1872)**

| 📊 Basis | 📦 Bailment | 💍 Pledge |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Meaning** | Delivery of goods for a specific purpose, to be returned later. | Delivery of goods strictly as security for a debt/promise. |
| **Scope** | Wider term (all pledges are bailments). | Narrower term (not all bailments are pledges). |
| **Purpose** | Repair, custody, use, carriage. | Strictly as collateral/security. |
| **Right of Sale** | Bailee cannot sell (only Right of Lien). | Pawnee can sell on default (after notice). |
| **Use of Goods** | Bailee may use if authorized. | Pawnee cannot use the pledged goods. |
