### ⚖️ Q1. Distinguish between a Contract of Indemnity and a Contract of Guarantee.
**(Refer to Sections 124 & 126 of ICA 1872)**

| 📊 Basis | 🛡️ Contract of Indemnity | 🤝 Contract of Guarantee |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Meaning** | A contract to save the other from loss caused by promisor or third party. | A contract to perform the promise or discharge liability of a third person on default. |
| **Parties** | **Two:** Indemnifier, Indemnity Holder. | **Three:** Creditor, Principal Debtor, Surety. |
| **No. of Contracts**| **One:** Between Indemnifier & Holder. | **Three:** C-PD, C-Surety, Surety-PD. |
| **Nature of Liability**| Primary and independent. | Secondary and conditional. |
| **Purpose** | Reimbursement of loss. | Security for a debt. |
| **Right to Sue** | Indemnifier cannot sue 3rd party in own name. | Surety can sue PD in own name (Subrogation). |
