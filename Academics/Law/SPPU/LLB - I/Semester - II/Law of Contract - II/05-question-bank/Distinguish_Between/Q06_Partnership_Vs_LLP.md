### ⚖️ Q6. Distinguish between a Partnership Firm and an LLP.

| 📊 Basis | 🤝 Ordinary Partnership (1932 Act) | 🏢 Limited Liability Partnership (2008 Act) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Legal Status** | Not a separate legal entity. | Separate legal entity (Body Corporate). |
| **Liability** | Unlimited, joint and several. | Limited to agreed contribution (except fraud). |
| **Registration** | Optional. | Mandatory. |
| **Mutual Agency** | Every partner is agent of firm AND other partners. | Partner is agent of LLP, but NOT of other partners. |
| **Succession** | Death/insolvency may dissolve firm. | Perpetual succession (existence unaffected). |
| **Maximum Limit** | 50 partners. | No maximum limit. |
