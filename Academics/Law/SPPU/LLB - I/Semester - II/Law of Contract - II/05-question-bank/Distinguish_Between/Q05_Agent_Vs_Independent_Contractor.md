### ⚖️ Q5. Distinguish between an Agent and an Independent Contractor.

| 📊 Basis | 👔 Agent | 🛠️ Independent Contractor |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Control** | Principal directs *what* to do, but agent decides *how*. | Employer only cares about the final result, not the method. |
| **Legal Relations** | Creates legal relations between Principal and 3rd parties. | Does not bind the employer with 3rd parties. |
| **Liability** | Principal is liable for authorized acts. | Employer is generally not liable for contractor's torts. |
