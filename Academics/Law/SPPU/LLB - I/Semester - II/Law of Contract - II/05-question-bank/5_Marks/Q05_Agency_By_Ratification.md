### Q5. Write a short note on: Agency by Ratification.
**📖 Statutory Provision:** Sections 196-200 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872.
**1. Meaning:** When an act is done by a person on behalf of another, but without their knowledge or authority, the person on whose behalf the act is done may elect to ratify (approve) or disown the act.
**2. Effect:** If ratified, the same effects follow as if the act had been performed with prior authority. It relates back to the date of the act.
**3. Essential Conditions for Valid Ratification:**
- The agent must have expressly acted on behalf of the principal. (Case: *Keighley, Maxsted & Co. vs. Durant*).
- The principal must be competent and in existence at the time of the act.
- The principal must have full knowledge of the material facts (Sec 198).
- It must be for the whole transaction, not part of it (Sec 199).
**🎯 Conclusion:** Ratification cures the defect of lack of authority.
