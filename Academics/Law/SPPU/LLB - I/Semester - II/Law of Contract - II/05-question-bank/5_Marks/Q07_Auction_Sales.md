### Q7. Explain 'Auction Sales' under the Sale of Goods Act.
**📖 Statutory Provision:** Section 64 of the Sale of Goods Act, 1930.
**1. Meaning:** An auction is a public sale where goods are offered to be taken by bidders.
**2. Key Rules:**
- 📦 **Lots:** Each lot put up is deemed to be a separate contract of sale.
- 🔨 **Completion:** The sale is complete upon the fall of the hammer or other customary announcement. Bids can be retracted before this.
- 🛡️ **Reserved Price:** The seller may notify a right to bid or set a reserve price.
- 🛑 **Pretended Bidding:** If the seller secretly uses people to bid up the price, the sale is voidable at the buyer's option.
**🎯 Conclusion:** Auction sales follow strict rules to ensure fairness and prevent artificial price inflation.
