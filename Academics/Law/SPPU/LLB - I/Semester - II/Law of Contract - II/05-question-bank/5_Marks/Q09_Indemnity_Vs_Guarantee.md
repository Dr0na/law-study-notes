### Q9. Distinguish between Contract of Indemnity and Contract of Guarantee.

**📖 Key Sections:** Section 124 (Indemnity) and Section 126 (Guarantee) of the Indian Contract Act, 1872.

| 📊 Basis | 🛡️ Indemnity (Sec 124) | 🤝 Guarantee (Sec 126) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Meaning** | Promise to save from loss caused by promisor or third party. | Promise to perform/discharge liability of a third person on default. |
| **Parties** | **Two:** Indemnifier, Indemnity Holder. | **Three:** Creditor, Principal Debtor, Surety. |
| **Contracts** | **One** contract. | **Three** contracts (C-PD, C-S, S-PD). |
| **Liability** | Primary and independent. | Secondary and conditional. |
| **Purpose** | Reimbursement of loss. | Security for a debt. |
| **Right to Sue** | Cannot sue third party in own name. | Surety can sue PD via subrogation. |

**🎯 Conclusion:** While both involve protective promises, a contract of indemnity is a standalone two-party agreement, whereas a guarantee is a tripartite arrangement where the surety's liability arises only upon default of the principal debtor.
