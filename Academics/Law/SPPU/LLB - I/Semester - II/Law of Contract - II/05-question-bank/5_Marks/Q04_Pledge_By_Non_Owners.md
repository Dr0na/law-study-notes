### Q4. Explain 'Pledge by non-owners' (Exceptions to Nemo dat).
**📖 Statutory Provision:** Sections 178, 178A, 179 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872.
**1. General Rule:** Only the true owner can pledge goods.
**2. Exceptions (Valid Pledge by Non-Owners):**
- 🤝 **Mercantile Agent (Sec 178):** An agent in possession of goods with owner's consent can make a valid pledge in the ordinary course of business, provided the pawnee acts in good faith.
- 🎭 **Possessor under Voidable Contract (Sec 178A):** If someone obtains goods via fraud/undue influence and pledges them *before* the contract is rescinded, the pawnee gets a good title (if acting in good faith).
- 🍰 **Person with Limited Interest (Sec 179):** A person can pledge goods to the extent of their interest in them (e.g., a finder pledging goods for the expenses incurred).
**🎯 Conclusion:** These exceptions protect innocent third-party pawnees in commercial transactions.
