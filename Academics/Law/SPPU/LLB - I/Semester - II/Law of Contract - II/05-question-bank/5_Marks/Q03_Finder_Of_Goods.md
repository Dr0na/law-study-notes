### Q3. Explain the concept of 'Finder of Goods' as a bailee.
**📖 Statutory Provision:** Section 71, 168, and 169 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872.
**1. Meaning:** A person who finds goods belonging to another and takes them into his custody is subject to the same responsibility as a bailee.
**2. Duties (Like a Bailee):**
- To take reasonable care of the goods.
- To try to find the true owner.
- Not to mix the goods or use them for personal benefit.
**3. Rights:**
- 💰 **Right of Lien (Sec 168):** Can retain the goods against the owner until compensated for trouble and expenses incurred in finding the owner and preserving the goods.
- 🏷️ **Right to Sell (Sec 169):** Can sell if: (a) goods are perishable, or (b) lawful charges amount to two-thirds (2/3rds) of the value of the goods.
**🎯 Conclusion:** The law imposes a quasi-contractual obligation on the finder to protect the property and the rights of the true owner.
