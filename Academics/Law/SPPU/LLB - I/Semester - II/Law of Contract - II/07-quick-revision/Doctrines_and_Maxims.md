# Quick Revision: Doctrines, Maxims & Checklists
*(Last-Hour Review Sheet)*

## 1. Important Legal Maxims

| Maxim | Meaning | Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Qui facit per alium facit per se** | He who acts through another acts himself. | Foundational principle of Agency. |
| **Delegatus non potest delegare** | A delegated power cannot be further delegated. | Rule against appointment of sub-agents (Sec 190). |
| **Caveat Emptor** | Let the buyer beware. | Sale of Goods (Sec 16). Buyer must choose carefully. |
| **Nemo dat quod non habet** | No one can give what he has not got. | Transfer of title by non-owners (Sale of Goods & Pledge). |
