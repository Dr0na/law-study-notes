# Condensed Notes: Modules 5 to 7
*(For Quick Revision Before Exams)*

## Module 5: Contracts of Agency (Sec 182-238)
- **Definition:** Agent is a person employed to do any act for another or represent another in dealings with 3rd persons. The other is the Principal.
- **Key Maxims:** *Qui facit per alium facit per se* (He who acts through another acts himself), *Delegatus non potest delegare* (Delegate cannot further delegate).
- **Essentials:** Principal must be competent, Agent need not be competent, no consideration required.
- **Creation:** Express, Implied, Estoppel, Holding Out, Necessity, Ratification.
- **Authority:** Express, Implied, Apparent/Ostensible, in Emergency.
- **Sub-agent:** Employed by and under control of original agent. (Properly appointed = binds principal).
- **Substituted Agent:** Named by agent to act directly for the principal.
- **Duties of Agent:** Follow directions, diligence, render accounts, no secret profits, communicate in difficulty.
- **Rights of Agent:** Remuneration, Retainer, Lien, Indemnification.
- **Personal Liability of Agent:** Generally not liable, EXCEPT: foreign principal, undisclosed principal, incompetent principal, pretended agent.
- **Termination:** Revocation, renunciation, business completion, death/insanity. *Irrevocable* if coupled with interest (Sec 202).

## Module 6: Contracts of Sale of Goods (Sale of Goods Act 1930)
- **Definition:** Contract whereby seller transfers/agrees to transfer property in goods to buyer for a price.
- **Essentials (6):** Two parties, movable goods, price (money), transfer of property, absolute/conditional, valid contract essentials.
- **Sale vs Agreement to Sell:** Sale = property transfers immediately, risk passes, executed. Agreement to sell = future transfer, risk with seller, executory.
- **Goods:** Existing (specific, ascertained, unascertained), Future, Contingent.
- **Condition vs Warranty:** Condition = essential to main purpose, breach allows rejection. Warranty = collateral, breach allows damages only.
- **Implied Conditions:** Title, Description, Sample, Merchantable quality.
- **Caveat Emptor:** "Let the buyer beware." Exceptions: relying on seller's skill for specific purpose, fraud.
- **Transfer of Title:** *Nemo dat quod non habet* (Nobody can give what he doesn't have). Exceptions: Mercantile agent, joint owner, voidable title possessor, unpaid seller.
- **Unpaid Seller Rights:** 
  1. *Against Goods:* Lien (retain possession), Stoppage in transit (if buyer insolvent), Resale.
  2. *Against Buyer:* Suit for price, damages, interest.

## Module 7: Contracts of Partnership (Partnership Act 1932 & LLP Act 2008)
- **Definition (Sec 4):** Relation between persons who agreed to share profits of a business carried on by all or any of them acting for all.
- **Essentials (5):** Association of 2+ persons, Agreement, Business, Sharing profits, **Mutual Agency** (true test).
- **Minor:** Cannot be partner, but can be admitted to benefits (no personal liability). Has 6 months upon majority to decide.
- **Partner's Implied Authority:** Can bind firm for acts in usual course of business. Cannot: acquire immovable property, submit to arbitration, compromise claims.
- **Liability:** Partners are jointly and severally liable for firm's acts.
- **Dissolution of Firm:** By agreement, compulsory (all insolvent/unlawful), contingent (death/term expiry), notice (at will), by Court (misconduct, continuous loss).
- **Registration:** Optional, but non-registration means firm/partners cannot sue third parties or each other in civil court.
- **LLP (2008 Act):** Body corporate, separate legal entity, limited liability, perpetual succession, mandatory registration, partner is agent of LLP but NOT of other partners.
