## 📚 MODULE 4: LEGISLATURE

### Lok Sabha Formula: **543-5-25-SP-MP**
**543** = Elected members  
**5** = Years (term)  
**25** = Minimum age  
**SP** = Speaker  
**MP** = More Powerful  

### Rajya Sabha Formula: **245-P-30-CH-LP**
**245** = Total members  
**P** = Permanent (never dissolves)  
**30** = Minimum age  
**CH** = Chairman (VP)  
**LP** = Less Powerful  

### Anti-Defection 4 Grounds: **RVNI**
**R** = Resignation from party

**I** = Independent joins party  

### Anti-Defection 2 Exceptions: **MS**
