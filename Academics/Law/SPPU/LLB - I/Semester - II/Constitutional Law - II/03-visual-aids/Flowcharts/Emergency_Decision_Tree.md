## 🏴 PRESIDENT'S RULE (Article 356) - Post-Bommai Flow

```
    Constitutional machinery fails in State?
                     |
         Governor assesses situation
                     ↓
         ┌───────────┴───────────┐
         │                       │
    Clear failure          Doubt about
    (no govt possible)     majority
         │                       │
         ↓                       ↓
    Governor reports       ORDER FLOOR TEST
         ↓                  (Bommai principle)
    President                   ↓
    examines              ┌─────┴─────┐
    material              │           │
         ↓            Has        Doesn't have
    Applies mind   majority     majority
    independently      │           │
         ↓              ↓           ↓
    Proclamation    Govt      Governor
    issued         continues   reports
         ↓                        ↓
    Parliament             President's
    approves               Rule
    (2 months,            proclaimed
    simple majority)
         ↓
    Valid 6 months
         ↓
    Renewable
    (max 3 years)
```

**Key Change After Bommai:**
- Floor test MUST be ordered first
- Cannot dismiss without testing majority
- Protects democracy

## 🔀 Emergency Type Selection Chart

```
YOUR SITUATION                    → EMERGENCY TYPE

Country attacked by China         → Article 352 (War)
Pakistan threatens invasion       → Article 352 (External Aggression)
Armed militant uprising          → Article 352 (Armed Rebellion)
State govt loses majority        → Article 356 (President's Rule)
No party can form govt           → Article 356 (President's Rule)
Economic collapse imminent       → Article 360 (Financial)
Cannot repay foreign debt        → Article 360 (Financial)
```
