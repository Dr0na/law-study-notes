### 🔹 3 Grounds - WEA (COUNT = 3)

```
        GROUNDS (WEA)
             |
    ┌────────┼────────┐
    │        │        │
    W        E        A
   WAR    EXTERNAL  ARMED
         AGGRESSION REBELLION
    │        │        │
 Actual  Threat of  Internal
  war    invasion   violent
         imminent    revolt
```

1️⃣ **W** = War (Actual armed conflict)  
2️⃣ **E** = External Aggression (Threat of invasion)  
3️⃣ **A** = Armed Rebellion (Internal armed revolt)  

### 🔹 Procedure - CNP-1-6 (COUNT = 3)

```
       PROCEDURE
            |
    ┌───────┼───────┐
    │       │       │
    C       N       P
 Cabinet  Notice  Parliament
recommend within  approves
(written) 1 month (special)
    ↓       ↓       ↓
 44th    Must     2/3+
  Amd   approve  majority
```

**C** = Cabinet recommendation (mandatory)  
**N** = Notice to Parliament (1 month)  
**P** = Parliament approval (special majority)  

**Duration:** 6 months, renewable indefinitely

### 🔹 Effects - 3 Main (COUNT = 3)

#### Effect 1: On Relations 🔀
**Union becomes supreme:** Federal structure suspended, Union can direct States on anything.

- Article 353: Union supreme
- Can give directions on any matter
- States lose autonomy

#### Effect 2: On Fundamental Rights 🚫
**Rights can be suspended:**

**Article 358** (Automatic):
- **Only for war/external aggression**
- Article 19 automatically suspended
- No separate order needed

**Article 359** (By Order):
- President can suspend other FRs (except 20-21)
- **Articles 20 & 21 protected** (44th Amendment)
- Needs separate order

#### Effect 3: On Legislature 🏛️
**Parliament becomes super-legislature:**

- Article 250: Can legislate on State List
- Laws valid for 6 months after emergency
- Lok Sabha term can extend (max 1 year at time)

### 🔢 5 Major Safeguards (COUNT = 5)

1️⃣ Changed to **"Armed Rebellion"** (from "internal disturbance")  
2️⃣ **Cabinet recommendation** mandatory (written)  
3️⃣ **Special majority** in Parliament needed  
4️⃣ **Articles 20 & 21** cannot be suspended  
5️⃣ **Lok Sabha** can revoke by simple majority  

🧠 **Mnemonic**: **"G-P-R-L"** = "Good Protection Rules Learned"  
**G**round changed, **P**rocedure stricter, **R**ights protected (20-21), **L**ok Sabha can revoke

### ⚖️ Bommai Guidelines - 5 Key Rules (COUNT = 5)

**Context:** S.R. Bommai v. Union of India (1994) transformed Article 356 from misused provision to judicially controlled power.

1️⃣ **Judicial review possible** - Courts can examine  
2️⃣ **Governor's report not final** - President must apply mind  
3️⃣ **Floor test preferred** - Test majority in House first  
4️⃣ **Federalism is basic** - Cannot be misused politically  
5️⃣ **Can restore government** - If misused, courts can undo  

🧠 **Mnemonic**: **"J-G-F-F-C"** = "Judges Guard Federal Features Carefully"

✅ S.R. Bommai case detailed (10 marks)

## ⚡ 30-SECOND MASTER RECALL

- June 25, 1975
- Internal disturbance ground

- 44th Amendment reforms

**44th Amendment (GPRL):**
- Ground: Armed rebellion
- Procedure: Cabinet + special majority
- Rights: 20-21 protected

- Floor test preferred
- Judicial review possible
- Federalism protected
- Cannot be misused
