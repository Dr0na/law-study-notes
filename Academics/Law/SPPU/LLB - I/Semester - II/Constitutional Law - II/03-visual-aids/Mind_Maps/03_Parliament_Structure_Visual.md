### 🧠 Lok Sabha Formula: **"543-5-25-SP-MP"**

1️⃣ **543** = Current strength (elected members)  
2️⃣ **5** = Years (term duration)  
3️⃣ **25** = Minimum age  
4️⃣ **SP** = Speaker (presiding officer)  
5️⃣ **MP** = More Powerful (than Rajya Sabha)  

### ⚡ Story Trick:
"**5**43 members serve **5** years if over **25** with **S**peaker **P**residing, being **M**ore **P**owerful"

### 🧠 Rajya Sabha Formula: **"245-P-30-CH-LP"**

1️⃣ **245** = Current strength  
2️⃣ **P** = Permanent (never dissolves)  
3️⃣ **30** = Minimum age  
4️⃣ **CH** = Chairman (Vice-President)  
5️⃣ **LP** = Less Powerful (than Lok Sabha)  

### ⚡ Story Trick:
"**2**45 **P**ermanent members over **30** with **CH**airman, being **L**ess **P**owerful"

## ⚡ 30-Second Master Recall

**Parliament = 3 Parts:**
- President + Lok Sabha + Rajya Sabha

**Lok Sabha:**
- 543-5-25-SP-MP (formula)
- Money Bills supreme
- Can pass no-confidence

**Rajya Sabha:**
- 245-P-30-CH-LP (formula)
- Cannot originate money bills
- Cannot move no-confidence

**Anti-Defection:**
- 4 grounds: RVNI
- 2 exceptions: MS (Merger, Speaker)
- Speaker decides
- Kihoto Hollohan case

**Money Bills:**
- Only Lok Sabha
- Rajya Sabha: 14 days, recommend
- Article 110, Speaker certifies
