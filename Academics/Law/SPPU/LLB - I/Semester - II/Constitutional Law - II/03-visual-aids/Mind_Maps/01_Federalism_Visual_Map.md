### 🔢 COUNT = 7 (WD-SCIBR)

1️⃣ **W** = Written Constitution  
2️⃣ **D** = Division of Powers (3 Lists)  
3️⃣ **S** = Supremacy of Constitution  
4️⃣ **C** = Courts (Independent Judiciary)  
5️⃣ **I** = Indirect (Bicameral Legislature)  
6️⃣ **B** = Both levels (Dual Government)  
7️⃣ **R** = Rigid-Flexible (Amendment)  

### ⚡ 30-Second Recall:
Point at your 7 fingers and recite: "WD-SCIBR" - Written, Division, Supremacy, Courts, Indirect, Both, Rigid
