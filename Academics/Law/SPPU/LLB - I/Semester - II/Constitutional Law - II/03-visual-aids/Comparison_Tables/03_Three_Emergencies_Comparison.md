# 📊 Comparison Table: Three Types of Emergencies

## 🎯 Articles 352, 356, 360 - Complete Comparison

**Context:** Constitution provides for 3 types of emergencies to handle different crisis situations. Understanding differences is crucial.

---

## 📋 Complete Comparison (COUNT = 15 features)

| # | Feature | National Emergency (352) | President's Rule (356) | Financial Emergency (360) |
|---|---------|------------------------|----------------------|------------------------|
| 1️⃣ | **Ground** | War, External Aggression, Armed Rebellion (WEA) | Constitutional machinery failure in State | Financial stability/credit of India threatened |
| 2️⃣ | **Proclaimed By** | President | President | President |
| 3️⃣ | **Approval By** | Both Houses of Parliament | Both Houses of Parliament | Both Houses of Parliament |
| 4️⃣ | **Majority Needed** | **Special majority** (2/3 + majority of total) | **Simple majority** | **Simple majority** |
| 5️⃣ | **Time for Approval** | Within **1 month** | Within **2 months** | Within **2 months** |
| 6️⃣ | **Initial Duration** | **6 months** | **6 months** | Not specified |
| 7️⃣ | **Maximum Duration** | **Indefinite** (renewable every 6 months) | **3 years** (under special conditions) | **Indefinite** |
| 8️⃣ | **Scope** | **Entire India** or part thereof | **Affected State only** | **Entire India** |
| 9️⃣ | **Effect on FRs** | Article 19 suspended (war/aggression), Others by order (except 20-21) | **No automatic effect** | **No effect** |
| 🔟 | **Effect on Federalism** | Federal → Unitary temporarily | State under Union control | Financial control to Union |
| 1️⃣1️⃣ | **Effect on Legislature** | Parliament can legislate on State List (Art 250) | Parliament legislates for that State | State money bills reserved |
| 1️⃣2️⃣ | **Lok Sabha Term** | Can be **extended** (max 1 year at time) | **Not affected** | **Not affected** |
| 1️⃣3️⃣ | **Revocation** | Lok Sabha simple majority can revoke | President can revoke anytime | President can revoke |
| 1️⃣4️⃣ | **Times Used** | **3 times** (1962, 1971, 1975) | **100+ times** (frequently misused) | **NEVER used** |
| 1️⃣5️⃣ | **Judicial Review** | Yes (limited) | **Yes** (after Bommai 1994) | Yes (theoretical) |

## 📊 Effect Comparison

### On Fundamental Rights:

| Emergency | Effect on FRs |
|-----------|---------------|
| **National (352)** | ✅ Article 358: Art 19 suspended automatically (war/aggression)<br>✅ Article 359: Others can be suspended by order (except 20-21) |
| **State (356)** | ❌ No effect on Fundamental Rights |
| **Financial (360)** | ❌ No effect on Fundamental Rights |

### On Federal Structure:

| Emergency | Effect on Federalism |
|-----------|---------------------|
| **National** | Federal → Unitary (Union supreme everywhere) |
| **State** | That State under Union control (other States unaffected) |
| **Financial** | States' financial autonomy lost (Union controls finances) |

## 📌 Safeguards - 44th Amendment

**For National Emergency (352):**
- "Armed rebellion" (not "internal disturbance")
- Cabinet recommendation mandatory
- Special majority needed
- Articles 20-21 cannot be suspended
- Lok Sabha can revoke by simple majority

**For President's Rule (356):**
- Bommai case made it judicially reviewable
- Floor test preferred
- Cannot be misused politically
