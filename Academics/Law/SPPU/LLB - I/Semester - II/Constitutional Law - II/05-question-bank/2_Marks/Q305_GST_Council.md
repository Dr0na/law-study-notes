# 📝 Question ID: CON2-2M-Q305

## Question
**What is GST Council? How are decisions taken in it?**

**Marks**: 2  
**Pattern**: ⚖️ Doctrinal  
**Topic**: Module 3 - GST  
**Exam Frequency**: 🔥🔥🔥 (Common - current affairs)  
**Time**: 2-3 minutes  

---

## 📋 MODEL ANSWER

**GST Council:**
Under **Article 279A** (added by 101st Amendment, 2016), GST Council is a constitutional body for making recommendations on GST matters. It's chaired by the **Union Finance Minister** and includes Finance Ministers of all States and UTs.

**Composition:** Union FM (Chair) + Union Minister of State (Finance) + All State Finance Ministers

**Decision-Making:**
Decisions taken by **3/4th (three-fourth) majority** of weighted votes, where **Union has 1/3rd voting power** and **States collectively have 2/3rd voting power**. This ensures States have collective veto and represents cooperative federalism.

**Functions:** Recommends GST rates, exemptions, threshold limits, and resolves disputes between Centre and States on GST matters.

---

## 🎯 Key Points

✅ **Article 279A** mentioned  
✅ **101st Amendment** (2016)  
✅ **Chairman** = Union FM  
✅ **Voting**: 1/3 Union, 2/3 States  
✅ **Decision**: 3/4 majority  
✅ **Cooperative federalism** phrase
