# 📝 Question ID: CON2-2M-Q309

## Question
**Distinguish between National Emergency and President's Rule.**

**Marks**: 2  
**Pattern**: 🔁 Comparative  
**Topic**: Module 8 - Emergency  
**Exam Frequency**: 🔥🔥🔥 (Common)  
**Time**: 2-3 minutes  

---

## 📋 MODEL ANSWER

**Two Types of Emergency:**
National Emergency (Article 352) and President's Rule (Article 356) differ in grounds, scope, and effects.

### Quick Comparison

| Feature | National Emergency (352) | President's Rule (356) |
|---------|-------------------------|----------------------|
| **Ground** | War, External Aggression, Armed Rebellion | Constitutional machinery failure in State |
| **Scope** | Entire India or part | Only that State |
| **Approval** | Special majority | Simple majority |
| **Effect on FRs** | Can suspend (358, 359) | No effect on FRs |
| **Effect** | Union supreme everywhere | State under Union control |
| **Used** | 3 times (1962, 1971, 1975) | 100+ times |

**Key Difference:** National Emergency affects entire nation with suspension of FRs, while President's Rule affects only one State without impacting Fundamental Rights.

---

## 🎯 Key Points

✅ **Articles 352 vs 356**  
✅ **Ground difference** (security vs governance)  
✅ **Scope** (nation vs state)  
✅ **FRs impact** (yes vs no)  
✅ **Usage frequency** (3 vs 100+)
