## Question
**What is Tenth Schedule? Mention any two grounds for disqualification under it.**

**Marks**: 2  
**Pattern**: ⚖️ Doctrinal  
**Topic**: Module 4 - Anti-Defection  
**Exam Frequency**: 🔥🔥🔥 (Common)  
**Time**: 2-3 minutes  

---

## 📋 MODEL ANSWER

**Tenth Schedule - Anti-Defection Law:**
The Tenth Schedule, added by the **52nd Constitutional Amendment (1985)**, contains provisions for **disqualification of members on ground of defection** from political parties. It applies to both Parliament and State Legislatures and aims to curb political defections that were causing instability.

**Two Main Grounds for Disqualification:**

**Ground 1: Voluntarily Giving Up Party Membership 🚪**
- If a member voluntarily resigns from the political party on whose ticket elected
- Or by conduct shows has given up membership (joining another party)

**Ground 2: Voting Against Party Whip ❌**
- If member votes or abstains contrary to party's direction (whip)
- Without obtaining prior permission from party
- And not condoned by party within 15 days

**Decision:** Speaker/Chairman decides on disqualification; decision subject to judicial review (**Kihoto Hollohan case, 1992**).
