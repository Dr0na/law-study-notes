**Name the three All India Services.**

## 📋 MODEL ANSWER

**All India Services (Article 312):**
The three All India Services created to ensure uniform administrative standards across India are:

### 3 Services (COUNT = 3)

1️⃣ **Indian Administrative Service (IAS)** - For civil administration  
2️⃣ **Indian Police Service (IPS)** - For police administration  
3️⃣ **Indian Forest Service (IFS)** - For forest management  

**Creation:** IAS and IPS created by Constitution itself; IFS created by Parliament law.

**Recruitment:** All three recruited through UPSC Civil Services Examination.

**Feature:** Members serve in States but cadre controlled by Union, representing dual control in federal structure.

**Article 312:** Rajya Sabha can pass resolution by 2/3rd majority to create new All India Services.
