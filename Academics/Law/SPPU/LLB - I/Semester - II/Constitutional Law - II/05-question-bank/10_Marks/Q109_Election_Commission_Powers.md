### Introduction (1 mark)

**Election Commission - Guardian of Democracy:**
Under **Article 324**, the Election Commission of India is entrusted with the **"superintendence, direction and control"** of elections to Parliament, State Legislatures, and offices of President and Vice-President. As the guardian of free and fair elections - the foundation of democracy - the Election Commission's independence is crucial. The Commission has evolved from a single-member body (1950-1989) to a three-member body since 1993.

---

### ⚖️ Composition (2 marks)

### 3 Members (COUNT = 3)

#### 1️⃣ Chief Election Commissioner (CEC) 👨‍⚖️
- Heads the Commission
- Appointed by President
- **6 years** term or till age of **65** (whichever earlier)

#### 2️⃣ Two Election Commissioners (ECs) 👥
- Support CEC
- Appointed by President
- Same term: 6 years or 65 age

#### 3️⃣ Multi-Member Since 1993 🔢
- Originally only CEC (1950-1989)
- Made multi-member in 1993
- Currently 3 members
- Decisions by majority

**Qualifications:** No specific qualifications prescribed in Constitution

---

### 🛡️ Removal Provisions - Most Important for Independence (2 marks)

**Why Removal Provisions Matter:**
The removal mechanism determines independence - difficult removal = more independence.

### Removal Rules (COUNT = 2)

#### CEC Removal - Like Supreme Court Judge ⚖️
**How removed:** Same manner as Supreme Court judge (impeachment process)

**Grounds:** Proved misbehavior or incapacity

**Procedure:**
- Motion in Parliament
- 2/3rd special majority in both Houses
- Very difficult to remove

**Result:** **Never removed** in Indian history - ensures complete independence

#### Other ECs Removal - BUT Protected ✅
**How removed:** Can be removed by President

**Critical Protection:** Only on **recommendation of CEC**

**Why Important:**
- President cannot remove ECs unilaterally
- Must have CEC's approval
- Protects ECs from political pressure
- CEC shields other commissioners

**Effect:** All 3 commissioners effectively independent

---

### 💪 Powers Under Article 324 (4-5 marks)

### 6 Main Powers (COUNT = 6)

#### 1️⃣ Conduct of Elections 🗳️
**Complete control:** EC decides all aspects of election conduct.

- Announces election dates and schedule
- Decides phases of polling
- Notifies constituencies
- Appoints polling officers

#### 2️⃣ Model Code of Conduct 📋
**Enforcement power:** Enforces Model Code once elections announced.

**Covers:**
- No misuse of official machinery
- No hate speech
- No bribery/inducement
- No government announcements of schemes
- Campaign conduct rules

**Not Statutory:** Convention-based but strictly enforced

**T.N. Seshan Era (1990-96):** Transformed EC into powerful, feared body

#### 3️⃣ Political Parties 🏛️
**Registration and recognition:** EC registers and recognizes political parties.

- Registers new parties
- Recognizes national and State parties
- Allots election symbols
- Decides symbol disputes
- De-recognizes parties (if criteria not met)

#### 4️⃣ Electoral Rolls 📝
**Voter lists:** Prepares and revises electoral rolls.

- Maintains voter lists
- Issues voter ID cards
- Adds new voters
- Removes dead/shifted voters

#### 5️⃣ Enforcement Powers ⚖️
**Can take action:** EC has quasi-judicial powers.

- Postpone/cancel elections
- Order re-poll
- Remove polling officers
- Disqualify candidates
- Recommend action against violators
- Seize cash/gifts meant for voters

#### 6️⃣ Dispute Resolution 🤝
**Decides disputes:** Symbol disputes, party splits/mergers.

- Disputes over party symbols
- Recognition of party factions
- Merger/split decisions

**Cannot Decide:** Election disputes after results (go to courts)

---

### 🛡️ Independence of Election Commission (1-2 marks)

### 3 Key Safeguards (COUNT = 3)

#### 1️⃣ Removal Protection 🔒
- CEC: Like SC judge (almost impossible)
- ECs: Only on CEC's recommendation
- Most important safeguard

#### 2️⃣ Fixed Tenure ⏰
- 6 years or 65 age (whichever earlier)
- Cannot be removed at government's will
- Completes full term

#### 3️⃣ Financial Independence 💰
- Salaries charged on Consolidated Fund
- Not subject to parliamentary vote
- Cannot be reduced

**Result:** EC can take tough decisions without fear of government

---

### Conclusion (1 mark)

The Election Commission's independence, ensured primarily through the CEC's Supreme Court judge-like removal protection, has made it a powerful constitutional body capable of conducting free and fair elections. The transformation under T.N. Seshan and subsequent CECs has established EC as the guardian of electoral democracy, with powers to enforce Model Code, register parties, and take action against violations, making it truly the protector of India's democratic foundation.
