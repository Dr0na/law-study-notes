# 📝 Question ID: CON2-5M-Q202

## Question
**Explain Article 74 of the Constitution. Discuss the position of President in light of this Article.**

**Marks**: 5  
**Pattern**: ⚖️ Doctrinal + 🧠 Conceptual  
**Topic**: Module 6 - Executive (President)  
**Exam Frequency**: 🔥🔥🔥 (Regular)  
**Time**: 6-7 minutes  

---

## 📋 MODEL ANSWER

### ⚖️ Article 74 - Council of Ministers to Aid and Advise President (3 marks)

**Article 74(1):**
"There shall be a Council of Ministers with the Prime Minister at the head to **aid and advise** the President who **shall**, in the exercise of his functions, **act in accordance with such advice**."

### 3 Key Points (COUNT = 3)

#### 1️⃣ Constitutional Provision 📜
**What it says:** President must act on the aid and advice of Council of Ministers headed by Prime Minister.

- **"Shall act"** makes it mandatory (not discretionary)
- Advice is **binding** on President
- President cannot act independently (generally)

#### 2️⃣ 42nd Amendment (1976) 🔒
**What changed:** Word **"shall"** inserted, making advice absolutely binding.

- Before: "may act" on advice (discretionary)
- After: **"shall act"** on advice (mandatory)
- Removed President's discretion completely
- Made binding nature explicit

#### 3️⃣ 44th Amendment (1978) ⚖️
**What changed:** Inserted Clause (2) giving President power to ask Council to **reconsider** advice once.

**Article 74(2):**
- President can ask Council to **reconsider** the advice
- Council reconsiders
- If Council sends same or modified advice again
- President **must accept** and act accordingly

**Effect:**
- One chance to reconsider
- But ultimately binding
- Balances completely mechanical acceptance

---

### 👤 Position of President (2 marks)

### President = Nominal Executive (COUNT = 3 aspects)

#### 1️⃣ Constitutional Head 📜
**What it means:** President is head of State (like British monarch) but not head of government (PM is).

- **De jure executive** (by law)
- **Nominal/Titular head**
- Real power with PM and Cabinet

#### 2️⃣ Bound by Advice ⛓️
**Article 74 makes clear:** President must act on Cabinet's advice in almost all matters.

- Cannot refuse advice (after reconsideration)
- Cannot act independently
- PM and Cabinet make decisions

#### 3️⃣ Very Limited Discretion ⚠️
**When President can act independently:** Only in very exceptional situations.

**Rare Discretionary Powers:**
- Appointing PM when no clear majority
- Dissolving Lok Sabha (extreme cases)
- Seeking information from PM
- Returning non-money bill for reconsideration (once)

### 📌 Samsher Singh v. State of Punjab (1974)
**Principle**: President has no independent powers; must act on aid and advice of Council of Ministers.
