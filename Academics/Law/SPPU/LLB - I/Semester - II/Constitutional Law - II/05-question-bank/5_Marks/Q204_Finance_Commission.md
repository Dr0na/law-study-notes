## Question
**Explain the composition and functions of Finance Commission.**

**Marks**: 5  
**Pattern**: ⚖️ Doctrinal + 🧠 Conceptual  
**Topic**: Module 3 - Finance Commission  
**Exam Frequency**: 🔥🔥🔥 (Regular)  
**Time**: 6-7 minutes  

---

## 📋 MODEL ANSWER

### ⚖️ Finance Commission (Article 280) (3 marks)

**What it is:** Finance Commission is a constitutional body established by the President **every 5 years** (or earlier if needed) to recommend distribution of financial resources between the Union and States.

### Composition (COUNT = 2 categories)

#### Chairman 👤
**Who:** Person with experience in public affairs  
**Usually:** Former judges, economists, civil servants  

#### 4 Members 👥
**Who:** Appointed by President  
**Expertise:** Finance, economics, administration  
**Current**: 15th Finance Commission (2020-2025), N.K. Singh - Chairman

---

### Functions of Finance Commission (2 marks)

### 4 Main Functions (COUNT = 4)

#### 1️⃣ Distribution of Union Taxes 💰
**What it recommends:** How Union's tax revenue should be shared between Union and States.

**Current Formula (15th FC):**
- **41% of divisible pool** to States
- Distributed based on: Population (15%), Income distance (45%), Area (15%), Forest & Ecology (10%), Demographic performance (12.5%), Tax effort (2.5%)

#### 2️⃣ Distribution Among States 📊
**What it recommends:** How the States' share should be divided among different States based on needs and performance.

#### 3️⃣ Grants-in-Aid (Article 275) 🎁
**What it recommends:** Which States need grants and how much.

- For States in need of assistance
- Special grants for tribal welfare
- Based on revenue gaps

#### 4️⃣ Other Financial Matters 📋
**What it reviews:** Any matter referred by President regarding Centre-State finances.

- State finances health
- Debt sustainability
- Fiscal discipline measures

---

### Conclusion (Brief)

Finance Commission ensures objective, non-political distribution of financial resources between Union and States, promoting fiscal federalism. By being reconstituted every 5 years, it adapts recommendations to changing economic realities, ensuring both vertical (Centre-State) and horizontal (among States) equity in resource distribution.
