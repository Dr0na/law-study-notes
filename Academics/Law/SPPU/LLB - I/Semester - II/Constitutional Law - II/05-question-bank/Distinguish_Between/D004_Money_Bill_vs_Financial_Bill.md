# 📝 Question ID: CON2-DIST-D004

## Question
**Distinguish between Money Bill and Financial Bill.**

**Marks**: 5  
**Pattern**: 🔁 Comparative  
**Topic**: Module 4 - Types of Bills  
**Exam Frequency**: 🔥🔥🔥 (Frequent)  
**Time**: 6-7 minutes  

---

## 📋 MODEL ANSWER

### Introduction (0.5 mark)

**Two Types of Financial Bills:**
Both Money Bills and Financial Bills deal with financial matters, but they differ significantly in definition, procedure, and powers of the two Houses. Understanding this distinction is crucial as the procedure and supremacy of Lok Sabha applies only to true Money Bills under Article 110.

---

### 🔁 Key Distinctions (4 marks)

### 8 Major Differences (COUNT = 8)

| # | Feature | Money Bill (Art 110) | Financial Bill (Art 117) |
|---|---------|---------------------|------------------------|
| 1️⃣ | **Definition** | Contains **ONLY** matters under Art 110 | Contains Art 110 matters **+ other matters** |
| 2️⃣ | **Introduction** | **Only in Lok Sabha** | Can be introduced in **either House** |
| 3️⃣ | **Rajya Sabha Role** | Recommend only (**14 days max**) | **Equal power** with Lok Sabha |
| 4️⃣ | **Rajya Sabha Power** | Cannot reject or amend | Can reject, amend, delay |
| 5️⃣ | **Final Say** | **Lok Sabha alone** | Both Houses must agree |
| 6️⃣ | **Certification** | **Speaker certifies** (final) | No certification needed |
| 7️⃣ | **Joint Sitting** | Not possible (LS supreme) | Possible (if deadlock) |
| 8️⃣ | **Example** | Annual Finance Bill (Budget), Tax bills | Bills with financial + other provisions |

---

### 📋 Article 110 - What Qualifies as Money Bill

**9 Matters (ONLY these):**
1. Taxation (impose, abolish, alter)
2. Borrowing by Government
3. Consolidated Fund matters
4. Appropriation of money
5. Government accounts audit
6-9. Related financial matters

**Key Word:** **"ONLY"** - If bill has even one provision outside these 9 matters, it's Financial Bill (not Money Bill)

---

### 📝 Examples

**Money Bill:**
- Annual Finance Bill (Budget) - only taxes and appropriation
- Bill to impose new income tax
- Bill to abolish certain tax

**Financial Bill:**
- Bill involving government expenditure + administrative provisions
- Bill with taxation + policy matters
- Most bills involving money (but not purely Article 110)

---

### Conclusion (0.5 mark)

The distinction is crucial because Lok Sabha has supremacy only on true Money Bills (Article 110), while Financial Bills are treated as Ordinary Bills with both Houses having equal powers. The **Speaker's certificate** determining whether a bill is Money Bill is final and cannot be questioned in courts, protecting parliamentary autonomy.
