# Comparison: Will vs Gift (under Muslim Law)

| 📊 Basis | 📜 Will (Wasiyat) | 🎁 Gift (Hiba) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Operative When** | After death of testator. | **During lifetime** (inter vivos). |
| **Limit** | Max **1/3rd** of estate. | No limit (entire property can be gifted). |
| **To Heirs** | Cannot bequeath to an heir (without other heirs' consent). | Can gift to an heir. |
| **Consideration** | No consideration. | No consideration (Hiba); with consideration (Hiba-bil-Iwaz). |
| **Revocability** | Freely revocable during lifetime. | Generally **irrevocable** once completed. |
| **Delivery of Possession** | Not required during lifetime. | **Essential** for validity. |
| **Form** | Oral or written. | Oral or written. |
