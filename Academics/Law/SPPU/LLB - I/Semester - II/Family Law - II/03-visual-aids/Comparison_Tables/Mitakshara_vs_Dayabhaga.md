# Comparison: Mitakshara vs Dayabhaga Coparcenary

| 📊 Basis | 🏛️ Mitakshara | 📜 Dayabhaga |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Prevalence** | All of India except Bengal. | Bengal and Assam. |
| **Right in Property** | By **birth** (son acquires interest the moment he is born). | By **succession** (only on death of the last owner). |
| **Share** | Fluctuating — changes with births and deaths. | Defined and ascertainable. |
| **Alienation** | Coparcener cannot alienate his undivided interest (except for legal necessity). | Coparcener can alienate his share freely. |
| **Survivorship** | Applies (share of deceased merges with survivors). | Does NOT apply (share passes by succession). |
| **Female Coparcener** | Daughters are coparceners after 2005 Amendment. | No concept of female coparcener (women inherit by succession). |
| **Partition** | Any coparcener can demand partition at any time. | Partition is primarily division by metes and bounds. |
