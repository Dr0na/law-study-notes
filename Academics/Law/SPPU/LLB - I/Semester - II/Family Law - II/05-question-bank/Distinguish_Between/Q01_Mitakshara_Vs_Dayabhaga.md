### ⚖️ Q1. Distinguish between Mitakshara and Dayabhaga Coparcenary.

| 📊 Basis | 🏛️ Mitakshara | 📜 Dayabhaga |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Prevalence** | All India except Bengal. | Bengal and Assam. |
| **Right in Property** | By **birth**. | By **succession** on death. |
| **Share** | Fluctuating. | Defined and ascertainable. |
| **Alienation** | Cannot alienate undivided interest freely. | Can alienate freely. |
| **Survivorship** | Applies. | Does not apply. |
| **Female Coparcener** | Yes (after 2005 Amendment). | No concept (women inherit by succession). |
