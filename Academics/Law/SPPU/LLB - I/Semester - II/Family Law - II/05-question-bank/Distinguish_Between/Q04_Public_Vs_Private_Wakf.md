### ⚖️ Q4. Distinguish between Public Wakf and Private Wakf (Wakf-alal-aulad).

| 📊 Basis | 🕌 Public Wakf | 👨‍👩‍👧 Private Wakf (Wakf-alal-aulad) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Beneficiaries** | General public at large. | Wakif's own family/descendants. |
| **Purpose** | Mosques, schools, graveyards, hospitals. | Family benefit; ultimate purpose must be charitable. |
| **Validity** | Always valid if object is lawful. | Valid only if the ultimate benefit is charitable/religious. |
| **Example** | Dedicating land for a mosque. | Income of property to go to children, then to charity. |
| **Supervision** | Wakf Board. | Wakf Board (under Wakf Act 1995). |
