### ⚖️ Q5. Distinguish between Privileged and Unprivileged Wills under the Indian Succession Act, 1925.

| 📊 Basis | 🎖️ Privileged Will (Sec 65-66) | 📝 Unprivileged Will (Sec 63) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Who Can Make** | Soldiers in warfare, mariners at sea. | Any person of sound mind, not a minor. |
| **Form** | Oral or written. | Must be in **writing**. |
| **Attestation** | **Not required.** | Must be attested by **2 or more witnesses**. |
| **Signature** | Not essential. | Must be signed by the testator. |
| **Revocation** | Same rules apply. | Same rules apply. |
| **Rationale** | Emergency circumstances of war/sea. | Standard civilian formalities. |
