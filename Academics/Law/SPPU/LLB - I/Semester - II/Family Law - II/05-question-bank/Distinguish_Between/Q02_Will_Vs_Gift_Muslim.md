### ⚖️ Q2. Distinguish between Will and Gift under Muslim Law.

| 📊 Basis | 📜 Will (Wasiyat) | 🎁 Gift (Hiba) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Operative** | After death. | During lifetime (inter vivos). |
| **Limit** | Max 1/3rd of estate. | No limit. |
| **To Heirs** | Cannot bequeath to heir (without consent). | Can gift to an heir. |
| **Revocability** | Freely revocable. | Generally irrevocable once completed. |
| **Delivery of Possession** | Not required. | Essential for validity. |
| **Form** | Oral or written. | Oral or written. |
