### ⚖️ Q3. Distinguish between Hiba and Hiba-bil-Iwaz.

| 📊 Basis | 🎁 Hiba (Pure Gift) | 💰 Hiba-bil-Iwaz |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Consideration** | Without consideration. | With return consideration. |
| **Nature** | Gratuitous transfer. | Operates as a **sale**. |
| **Delivery of Possession** | Essential. | Not essential (consideration substitutes). |
| **Mushaa** | Invalid for divisible property (Sunni). | Valid even for undivided share. |
| **Revocability** | Revocable in certain cases. | **Absolutely irrevocable**. |
| **Registration** | Not required. | Required (as it operates like a sale). |
