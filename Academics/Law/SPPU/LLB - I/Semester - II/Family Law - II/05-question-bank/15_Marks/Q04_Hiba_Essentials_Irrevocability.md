## 🎁 Q4. Define Hiba (Gift) under Muslim Law. Discuss its essentials, types, and the circumstances under which a gift is irrevocable.

### 1. Definition
**Hiba** is a transfer of property made immediately, without consideration, by one person (donor) to another (donee), accepted by or on behalf of the latter.

### 2. Three Essentials of a Valid Hiba
1. **Declaration (Ijab):** Clear intention by the donor to make a gift.
2. **Acceptance (Qubul):** Express or implied acceptance by the donee (guardian accepts for minors).
3. **Delivery of Possession (Qabza):** Physical transfer of possession — the most critical requirement. *(Mahboob Sahab vs. Syed Ismail, 1995)*.

### 3. Types of Hiba
1. **Hiba (Pure Gift):** Unconditional, no consideration. Irrevocable once completed.
2. **Hiba-bil-Iwaz:** Gift with return consideration. Operates as a sale. Irrevocable.
3. **Hiba-ba-Shart-ul-Iwaz:** Gift with a stipulation for return. Revocable until the return is made.
4. **Sadaqah:** Charitable gift for religious merit. Absolutely irrevocable.

### 4. Mushaa (Undivided Share)
- **Sunni Law:** Gift of undivided share in divisible property is **invalid** without partition.
- **Shia Law:** Valid even without partition.

### 5. Marz-ul-Maut (Death-bed Gift)
Subject to the **1/3rd rule** (like a will). Takes effect only after death. Cannot be made to an heir without consent of other heirs.

### 6. When a Gift is Irrevocable (8 Cases)
1. Gift to a person within **prohibited degrees** (spouse, child, parent).
2. **Sadaqah** (charitable gift).
3. **Hiba-bil-Iwaz** (consideration has been given).
4. Donee has **sold or consumed** the property.
5. **Donor has died.** 6. **Donee has died.**
7. Property has been **substantially altered** by donee.
8. **Relationship changed** between donor and donee (e.g., marriage).

### 7. Conclusion
The law of Hiba balances the donor's generosity with legal safeguards. Delivery of possession ensures seriousness of intent, while the irrevocability rules protect the donee's reliance on the completed gift.
