## 🕌 Q5. What is Wakf? Discuss its essentials, kinds, and the powers and duties of a Mutawalli.

### 1. Definition
**Wakf** is the permanent dedication of property by a Muslim for any purpose recognized by Muslim law as religious, pious, or charitable. Ownership vests in **God (Allah)**.

### 2. Essentials (4 Essentials)
1. **Wakif:** Must be a Muslim, of sound mind, major, and owner of the property.
2. **Declaration (Wakfnama):** Clear intention to dedicate (oral or written).
3. **Subject Matter:** Property owned by the wakif — movable or immovable.
4. **Lawful Object:** Must be recognized as religious, pious, or charitable.

### 3. Kinds
1. **Public Wakf:** For the benefit of the general public (mosques, schools, graveyards).
2. **Wakf-alal-aulad (Private/Family Wakf):** For the benefit of the wakif's family. The ultimate benefit must be for charity.

### 4. Mutawalli — The Manager
The Mutawalli is the person entrusted with the management of the wakf property. They are a **manager/superintendent, NOT the owner**.

### 5. Powers of the Mutawalli
1. Manage and administer the wakf property.
2. Spend income on the objects of the wakf.
3. Carry out necessary repairs and maintenance.
4. Lease property (short-term; long-term requires court sanction).
5. **Cannot alienate** wakf property except with court permission for legal necessity.
6. Defend the wakf property in legal proceedings.

### 6. Duties of the Mutawalli
1. Protect and preserve the wakf property.
2. Apply the income strictly to the specified purposes.
3. Maintain proper accounts and records.
4. Comply with directions of the Wakf Board.
5. Not to misappropriate or use the property for personal benefit.

### 7. Removal
A mutawalli can be removed by the **court or the Wakf Board** for mismanagement, breach of trust, misappropriation, or incompetence.

### 8. Conclusion
Wakf is a permanent and irrevocable institution that serves the dual purpose of religious devotion and public welfare. The mutawalli is a fiduciary whose powers are limited by the objects of the wakf and supervised by the Wakf Board.
