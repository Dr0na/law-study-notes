### Q3. Write a short note on: Will under Muslim Law (Wasiyat).
**📖 Context:** Module 3 — Testamentary Succession.
**1. Meaning:** A wasiyat is a disposition of property by a Muslim to take effect after death.
**2. Three Restrictions:**
- 📏 **One-Third Rule:** Can bequeath only up to 1/3rd of net estate.
- 🚫 **No Bequest to Heirs:** Cannot bequeath to an existing heir (unless other heirs consent after testator's death).
- ✅ **No Form Required:** Can be oral or written, no attestation needed.
**3. Revocability:** Freely revocable at any time during the testator's lifetime.
**🎯 Conclusion:** Muslim testamentary law restricts the testator's freedom to protect the rights of compulsory heirs.
