### Q1. Write a short note on: Joint Family Property vs Self-Acquired Property.
**📖 Context:** Hindu Joint Family System (Module 1).
**1. Joint Family Property:** Property inherited from the three immediate paternal male ancestors + property acquired with JF funds + accretions. All coparceners have a right by birth.
**2. Self-Acquired Property:** Acquired entirely with own funds/labour/skill **without** the aid of joint family property. The acquirer is the absolute owner.
**3. Key Distinction:** Joint family property cannot be willed away entirely (coparceners have birthright), whereas self-acquired property can be freely disposed of by will or otherwise.
**🎯 Conclusion:** The distinction determines whether coparceners can challenge an alienation or claim a share.
