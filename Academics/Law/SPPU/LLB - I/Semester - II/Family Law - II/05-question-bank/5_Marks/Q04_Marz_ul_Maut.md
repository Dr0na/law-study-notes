### Q4. Write a short note on: Marz-ul-Maut (Death-bed Gift).
**📖 Context:** Module 5 — Gifts under Muslim Law.
**1. Meaning:** A gift made by a person suffering from a disease that ultimately causes their death.
**2. Rules:**
- Subject to the **one-third rule** (like a will — cannot exceed 1/3rd of estate).
- Takes effect only **after death** (unlike a regular Hiba which is inter vivos).
- Cannot be made to an **heir** without consent of other heirs.
- The donor must die of the illness; if they recover, the gift becomes an ordinary Hiba.
**3. Rationale:** Prevents a dying person from unfairly distributing property to the detriment of legal heirs.
**🎯 Conclusion:** Marz-ul-Maut is a hybrid between a gift and a will — made during lifetime but subject to testamentary restrictions.
