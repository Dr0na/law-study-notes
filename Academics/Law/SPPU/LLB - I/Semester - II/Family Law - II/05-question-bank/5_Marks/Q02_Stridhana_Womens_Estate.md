### Q2. Write a short note on: Stridhana and Women's Estate under Hindu Law.
**📖 Statutory Provision:** Section 14 of the Hindu Succession Act, 1956.
**1. Stridhana:** Property over which a Hindu woman has absolute ownership — gifts from parents, husband, in-laws, property earned by her own skill.
**2. Women's Estate (Old Law):** Under old Hindu law, women held a "limited estate" (life interest only — could use but not alienate permanently).
**3. Sec 14 — Abolition:** The 1956 Act converted ALL limited estates into **absolute estates**. Any property possessed by a Hindu female is held as full owner.
**🎯 Conclusion:** Section 14 was a revolutionary provision that gave Hindu women full ownership rights, ending centuries of limited estate restrictions.
