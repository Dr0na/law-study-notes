### Q5. Write a short note on: Escheat under the Hindu Succession Act.
**📖 Statutory Provision:** Section 29 of the Hindu Succession Act, 1956.
**1. Meaning:** When a Hindu dies intestate and has **no heir** qualified to succeed under the Act, the property devolves upon the **Government**.
**2. Order of Succession:** Class I → Class II → Agnates → Cognates → **Government (Escheat)**.
**3. Principle:** The State is the ultimate heir. The doctrine is based on the principle of *bona vacantia* (ownerless property).
**4. Conditions:** All possible heirs in the prescribed order must be exhausted.
**🎯 Conclusion:** Escheat is a last-resort provision ensuring no property remains ownerless.
