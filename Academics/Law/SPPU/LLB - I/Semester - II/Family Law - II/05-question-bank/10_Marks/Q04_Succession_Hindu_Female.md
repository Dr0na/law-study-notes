## 👩 Q4. Discuss the rules of succession to the property of a Hindu female dying intestate under the Hindu Succession Act, 1956.

### 1. Section 15 — Order of Succession
Property of a Hindu female dying intestate devolves in the following order:
1. **Sons and daughters** (including children of predeceased son/daughter) and the **husband**.
2. **Heirs of the husband.**
3. **Mother and father.**
4. **Heirs of the father.**
5. **Heirs of the mother.**

### 2. Special Rule — Sec 15(2)
If the property was:
- Inherited from **father or mother** → goes to the heirs of the father.
- Inherited from **husband or father-in-law** → goes to the heirs of the husband.
This applies only if the female dies without leaving any son or daughter.

### 3. Section 14 — Absolute Ownership
Any property possessed by a Hindu female (whether acquired before or after the 1956 Act) is held by her as **full owner** (absolute estate), not as a limited owner. This abolished the old concept of "women's estate."

### 4. Conclusion
The Hindu Succession Act ensures that a Hindu female's property is distributed in a structured hierarchy. Section 15(2) traces the property back to its source, while Section 14 guarantees women absolute ownership rights.
