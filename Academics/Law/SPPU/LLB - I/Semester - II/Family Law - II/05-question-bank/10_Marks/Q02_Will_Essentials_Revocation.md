## 📜 Q2. What is a Will under the Indian Succession Act, 1925? Discuss the essentials of a valid unprivileged will and the modes of revocation.

### 1. Definition (Sec 2(h))
A **Will** is the legal declaration of the intention of a testator with respect to his property which he desires to be carried into effect after his death.

### 2. Essentials of an Unprivileged Will (Sec 63)
1. Must be **in writing** (no specific form required).
2. Must be **signed** by the testator (or by another person in testator's presence and by their direction).
3. The signature must show it was intended to give effect to the will.
4. Must be **attested by 2 or more witnesses**, each of whom saw the testator sign or received acknowledgment.
5. Each witness must sign in the **presence of the testator**.

### 3. Modes of Revocation (Sec 70)
1. **By making a new will or codicil** — the later will revokes the earlier to the extent of inconsistency.
2. **By a written declaration** executed like a will, declaring the intention to revoke.
3. **By destruction** — burning, tearing, or destroying with the intention of revoking.
- **By marriage** (Sec 69) — applicable to Christians and Parsis (marriage revokes a prior will).

### 4. Conclusion
The formality requirements ensure the testator's true intention is recorded and protected against fraud, while the revocation provisions give the testator full flexibility to change their mind.
