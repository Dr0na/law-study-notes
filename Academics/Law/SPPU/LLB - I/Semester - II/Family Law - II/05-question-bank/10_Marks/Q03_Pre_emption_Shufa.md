## 🎁 Q3. Discuss the right of pre-emption (Shufa) under Muslim law. Who can exercise it? What formalities must be observed?

### 1. Meaning
**Shufa** is the right to acquire immovable property in preference to other persons whenever it is sold. The pre-emptor substitutes themselves for the buyer.

### 2. Who Can Exercise? (3 Classes in Order of Priority)
1. **Shafi-i-Sharik (Co-sharer):** Co-owner in the property. Highest priority.
2. **Shafi-i-Khalit (Participator in appendages):** Shares common right of way or water.
3. **Shafi-i-Jar (Neighbor):** Owner of adjoining property.

### 3. Formalities — Three Demands (Talab)
1. **Talab-i-Mowasibat:** Immediate declaration of intention upon hearing of the sale.
2. **Talab-i-Ishhad:** Confirmation before witnesses, in presence of buyer/seller or at the property.
3. **Talab-i-Tamlik:** Filing a suit in court if the buyer refuses.

### 4. Loss of Right
Delay, acquiescence, death of pre-emptor (right is personal and non-heritable), express release, or property ceasing to exist.

### 5. Conclusion
Pre-emption protects co-owners and neighbors from the introduction of strangers. The three demands ensure the right is exercised promptly and seriously.
