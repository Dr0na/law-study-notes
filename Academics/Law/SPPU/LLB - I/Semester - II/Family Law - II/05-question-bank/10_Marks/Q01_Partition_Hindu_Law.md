## 🏛️ Q1. Explain the concept of Partition under Hindu law. Who can demand partition? Discuss reopening and reunion.

### 1. Meaning
Partition is the **severance of joint status** and division of joint family property among coparceners. It involves (i) expression of intention to separate, and (ii) actual division by metes and bounds.

### 2. Who Can Demand Partition?
1. Every coparcener (including daughters after 2005 Amendment).
2. A minor coparcener (through a guardian).
3. A son in the womb at the time of partition.
- A wife **cannot** demand partition but is entitled to a share equal to her husband.

### 3. Kinds
- **Total Partition:** Entire property divided among all coparceners.
- **Partial Partition:** Only some members separate, or only some property is divided.

### 4. Reopening of Partition
A completed partition may be reopened on:
1. Fraud, misrepresentation, or undue influence.
2. Son in the womb was not given a share.
3. An absent coparcener was excluded.
4. A minor was prejudiced.

### 5. Reunion
After partition, coparceners may reunite. Requires **mutual consent**. Only persons who were previously joint can reunite. A stranger cannot be admitted.

### 6. Conclusion
Partition is a fundamental right of every coparcener and cannot be denied. The provisions for reopening protect vulnerable members, while reunion allows flexibility in family arrangements.
