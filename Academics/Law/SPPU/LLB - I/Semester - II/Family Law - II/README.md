# Family Law II - Study Material

Welcome to the comprehensive, exam-oriented study material for **Family Law II** (LCC 0603) aligned with the SPPU syllabus.

## 📚 About the Subject
This course covers personal law as it affects property relations. It spans the Hindu Joint Family system, intestate and testamentary succession across Hindu, Muslim, Christian and Parsi laws, pre-emption, gifts under Muslim Law (Hiba), and Wakf. The course cuts across religious lines and exposes the student to comparative personal law systems.
