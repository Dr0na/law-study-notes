# Condensed Notes: Modules 1 to 3
*(Quick Revision Before Exams)*

## Module 1: Hindu Joint Family System
- **Joint Family:** Arises by birth/status, not contract. Common ancestor + male descendants + wives + unmarried daughters.
- **Joint Family Property:** Inherited from father/father's father/father's father's father + acquired with JF funds.
- **Self-acquired:** Own funds, no JF aid → absolute owner.
- **Coparcenary:** Narrower body. Mitakshara: right by birth (4 generations). Dayabhaga: right by succession on death.
- **2005 Amendment (Sec 6 HSA):** Daughters = coparceners by birth. *(Vineeta Sharma vs. Rakesh Sharma, 2020)*.
- **Karta:** Senior-most member. Powers: manage, alienate for legal necessity/benefit of estate, represent family.
- **Father's alienation:** Valid if for legal necessity. Burden on alienee to prove.
- **Partition:** Severance of joint status. Any coparcener can demand. Kinds: Total, Partial. Reopening: fraud, son in womb, absent coparcener.
- **Reunion:** Requires mutual consent.

## Module 2: Intestate Succession
### Hindu (HSA 1956)
- **Male (Sec 8):** Class I heirs (simultaneous, exclude others) → Class II → Agnates → Cognates → Escheat.
- **Female (Sec 15):** Sons/daughters/husband → Heirs of husband → Mother/Father → Heirs of father → Heirs of mother.
- **Sec 14:** Women's limited estate → absolute estate.
- **Disqualifications:** Murderer (Sec 25), Convert's children (Sec 26).

### Muslim
- **3 Classes:** Sharers (fixed fraction) → Residuaries (remainder) → Distant Kindred.
- **Sunni vs Shia:** Sunni: no representation. Shia: representation allowed.
- **Disqualifications:** Murder, difference of religion.

### Indian Succession Act 1925
- **Christians (Sec 31-49):** Widow 1/3 + children 2/3.
- **Parsis (Sec 50-56):** Widow and children take equal shares.

## Module 3: Testamentary Succession
### ISA 1925
- **Will (Sec 2(h)):** Legal declaration re property, effective after death.
- **Unprivileged (Sec 63):** Signed + attested by 2 witnesses.
- **Privileged (Sec 65-66):** Soldiers/mariners — oral/written, no attestation.
- **Revocation (Sec 70):** New will, written declaration, destruction.
- **Legacies:** Void, Onerous, Contingent, Conditional, Specific, Demonstrative.
- **Ademption:** Specific thing no longer in estate → legacy fails.
- **Lapse (Sec 105):** Legatee dies before testator → lapses.
- **Election (Sec 180-190):** Choose between will benefit and own property.

### Hindu Will — Sec 30 HSA: Any Hindu can dispose by will. Governed by ISA 1925.
### Muslim Will (Wasiyat) — 3 Restrictions: Max 1/3rd; No bequest to heir; Revocable.
