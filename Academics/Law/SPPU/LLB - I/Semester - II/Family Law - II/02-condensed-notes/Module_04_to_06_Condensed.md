# Condensed Notes: Modules 4 to 6
*(Quick Revision Before Exams)*

## Module 4: Right of Pre-emption (Shufa)
- **Meaning:** Right to purchase immovable property in preference to others when it is sold.
- **3 Classes (Priority):** Shafi-i-Sharik (co-sharer) → Shafi-i-Khalit (shares easements) → Shafi-i-Jar (neighbor).
- **3 Demands (Talab):** Mowasibat (immediate declaration) → Ishhad (confirmation before witnesses) → Tamlik (suit in court).
- **Loss:** Delay, acquiescence, death (right is personal, non-heritable), release, property ceases to exist.
- **Hindu Law:** Customary only, no specific statute. Punjab Pre-emption Act 1913.

## Module 5: Gifts under Muslim Law (Hiba)
- **Meaning:** Immediate transfer of property without consideration, accepted by donee.
- **3 Essentials:** Declaration (Ijab) + Acceptance (Qubul) + Delivery of Possession (Qabza).
- **Mushaa:** Undivided share — Invalid without partition (Sunni). Valid (Shia).
- **4 Types:** Hiba (pure) | Hiba-bil-Iwaz (with return = sale) | Hiba-ba-Shart-ul-Iwaz (conditional return) | Sadaqah (charitable).
- **Marz-ul-Maut:** Death-bed gift → 1/3rd rule applies, like a will.
- **Irrevocable (8 cases):** Prohibited degrees, sadaqah, consideration given, property consumed/sold, donor/donee dead, property altered, relationship changed.
- **Revocation:** Only by court decree (for non-irrevocable gifts).

## Module 6: Wakf
- **Meaning:** Permanent dedication of property for religious/pious/charitable purposes recognized by Muslim law.
- **Ownership:** Vests in God (Allah). Property detained for benefit of mankind.
- **4 Essentials:** Wakif (Muslim, major, sane, owner) + Declaration + Subject Matter + Lawful Object.
- **Kinds:** Wakf-alal-aulad (family/private) | Public Wakf (general public).
- **Mutawalli:** Manager, NOT owner. Appointed by wakif/court/Wakf Board.
- **Powers:** Manage, spend income, repairs, lease (short-term). **Cannot alienate** without court permission.
- **Duties:** Protect property, apply income, maintain accounts, comply with Wakf Board.
- **Removal:** Mismanagement, breach of trust, misappropriation.
- **Wakf Board (Wakf Act 1995):** Survey, register, supervise, settle disputes.
