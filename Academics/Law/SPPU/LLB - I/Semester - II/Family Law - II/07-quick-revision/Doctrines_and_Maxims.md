## 2. Hindu Succession Act 1956 — Key Sections

- **Sec 2:** Application (Hindus, Buddhists, Jains, Sikhs).
- **Sec 6:** Coparcenary property — daughters as coparceners (2005 Amendment).
- **Sec 8:** Succession to Hindu male (Class I → II → Agnates → Cognates → Escheat).
- **Sec 10:** Distribution among Class I heirs.
- **Sec 14:** Women's limited estate → absolute estate.
- **Sec 15:** Succession to Hindu female.
- **Sec 15(2):** Source-based devolution (father's property → father's heirs).
- **Sec 25:** Murderer disqualified.
- **Sec 26:** Convert's children disqualified.
- **Sec 29:** Escheat to Government.
- **Sec 30:** Testamentary power of Hindus.

## 3. Indian Succession Act 1925 — Key Sections

- **Sec 2(h):** Definition of Will.
- **Sec 59:** Competence of testator.
- **Sec 63:** Execution of unprivileged will (signed + 2 witnesses).
- **Sec 65-66:** Privileged wills (soldiers/mariners).
- **Sec 70:** Revocation of wills.
- **Sec 105:** Lapse of legacies.
- **Sec 113-114:** Void bequests.
- **Sec 127:** Onerous bequests.
- **Sec 180-190:** Election.
