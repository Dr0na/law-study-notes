# Important Case Laws Digest: Family Law II

---

## Module 1: Hindu Joint Family System

### 1. Vineeta Sharma vs. Rakesh Sharma (2020) — SC
**Facts:** Question of whether the 2005 Amendment to Sec 6 (daughters as coparceners) applies retrospectively.
**Held:** The amendment is **retrospective** — a daughter becomes a coparcener by birth irrespective of whether the father was alive on the date of the amendment (09.09.2005).
**Exam Relevance:** Cite when discussing women as coparceners or the 2005 Amendment.

### 2. Pratap Singh vs. Union of India (1985) — SC
**Facts:** Scope of Karta's power to alienate joint family property.
**Held:** Karta can alienate joint family property only for **legal necessity** or **benefit of the estate**. The alienee must prove legal necessity.

---

## Module 2: Intestate Succession

### 3. Gurupad Khandappa Magdum vs. Hirabai (1978) — SC
**Facts:** How to calculate the share of a Hindu female dying intestate.
**Held:** The widow's share in the husband's property must be determined by first ascertaining what the husband would have received on a notional partition.

### 4. Danamma @ Suman Surpur vs. Amar (2018) — SC
**Held:** Daughters are entitled to equal share in coparcenary property even if the father died before the 2005 Amendment came into force (later clarified by *Vineeta Sharma*).

---

## Module 3: Testamentary Succession

### 5. Venkatesh vs. BDA (2018) — SC
**Facts:** Validity and attestation requirements of a will.
**Held:** Reaffirmed that a will under Sec 63 ISA must be attested by two witnesses who saw the testator sign.

---

## Module 4: Pre-emption

### 6. S. Bakhtawar Singh vs. S. Balbir Singh (2007) — SC
**Held:** The right of pre-emption based on vicinage (neighborhood) is constitutionally valid. However, provisions discriminating on the basis of religion violate Article 14.

---

## Module 5: Gifts (Hiba)

### 7. Mahboob Sahab vs. Syed Ismail (1995) — SC
**Held:** Delivery of possession is the **most essential** element of a valid gift under Muslim law. Without it, the gift is incomplete.

---

## Module 6: Wakf

### 8. Md. Ismail Arifi vs. Union of India (Ayodhya Verdict, 2019) — SC
**Relevance:** The court examined the concept of wakf by user and dedication in the context of the Babri Masjid dispute. It clarified that wakf property vests in God and is inalienable.
