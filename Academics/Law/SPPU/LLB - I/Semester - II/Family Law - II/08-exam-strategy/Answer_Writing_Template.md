# Topper's Answer Writing Template — Family Law II

## General Principles
1. **Use Explicit Counts:** "There are 3 essentials of a valid gift under Muslim law..."
2. **Cite Section Numbers:** Always begin definitions with the statutory provision.
3. **Use Subheadings:** Break every long answer into numbered sections.
4. **Include Case Laws:** At least 2 for 15 marks, 1 for 10 marks.
5. **Write a Conclusion:** Every answer must end with a brief conclusive paragraph.

## 5-Mark Template (1-1.5 pages)
1. **Meaning/Definition** (with section number) — 3-4 lines.
2. **Key Points** — 3-4 numbered bullets.
3. **Example or Case Law** — 1 reference.
4. **Conclusion** — 2 lines.

## 10-Mark Template (3-4 pages)
1. **Introduction** — Context and relevance.
2. **Statutory Definition** — Section + Act.
3. **Essentials/Features** — Numbered list.
4. **Detailed Explanation** — Subheadings for each key aspect.
5. **Case Law** — 1 landmark judgment.
6. **Conclusion.**

## 15-Mark Template (5-6 pages)
1. **Introduction** — Historical/conceptual background.
2. **Definition** — Section + Act + 2-3 explanatory lines.
3. **Essential Elements** — Numbered with counts.
4. **Detailed Analysis** — Multiple sub-sections (I, II, III...).
5. **Exceptions/Special Rules.**
6. **Case Laws** — 2+ with brief facts and held.
7. **Conclusion.**

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
- ❌ Writing "as per the law" without citing the specific section.
- ❌ Skipping the conclusion.
- ❌ Mixing up Mitakshara and Dayabhaga rules.
- ❌ Confusing Hiba with Wasiyat (gift vs will).
- ❌ Forgetting the one-third rule for Muslim wills.
