## 🔓 Q2. Discuss the concept of Parole in India, the structure and functions of Parole Boards, and the conditions of release.

### 1. Introduction & Definition
Derived from the French phrase ***Parole d'honneur*** (meaning "word of honor"), **Parole** refers to the conditional release of a prisoner before they have completed their full sentence, subject to state supervision.

Under the Seventh Schedule of the Indian Constitution, prisons and penal administration are **State subjects** (Entry 4, List II). Consequently, each state has its own distinct Parole Rules. From a penological standpoint, parole is not a right but a privilege, designed to act as a bridge between structured confinement and free society.

---

### 2. Structure and Functions of Parole Boards
State Parole Boards are administrative bodies that evaluate and decide on parole applications:

*   **Composition**: Chaired by the State Home Secretary or a District Magistrate, and includes the Inspector General of Prisons, senior police officers, and independent social workers or psychologists.
*   **Functions**:
    1.  **Application Review**: Evaluate the inmate's behavior, work records, and discipline during confinement.
    2.  **Risk Assessment**: Review local police reports regarding the risk of reoffending and public safety concerns in the inmate's home district.
    3.  **Determination of Conditions**: Define specific boundaries, reporting rules, and supervision levels for approved parolees.
    4.  **Revocation Decisions**: Review violations of parole conditions and order re-confinement when necessary.

---

### 3. Conditions of Release & Supervision
If parole is approved, the parolee must adhere to five standard conditions:
1.  **Reporting**: Report regularly to the designated local police station or parole officer.
2.  **Residence**: Live within the geographical limits specified in the parole order.
3.  **Good Behavior**: Maintain peaceful, lawful conduct and avoid any criminal association.
4.  **No Contact**: Refrain from contacting or intimidating victims, witnesses, or co-defendants.
5.  **Re-confinement**: Acknowledge that any violation of these conditions will lead to immediate revocation of parole and return to a closed prison.

---

### 4. Conclusion
Parole is a valuable tool in modern reformative penology. As held in the landmark case **Asfaq v. State of Haryana (2017)**, parole helps prevent recidivism and preserve family bonds, and applications must be processed fairly and without arbitrary administrative delays. To ensure its success, parole systems must balance rehabilitation with public safety, employing objective risk assessments and professional supervision to help parolees successfully transition back into society.
