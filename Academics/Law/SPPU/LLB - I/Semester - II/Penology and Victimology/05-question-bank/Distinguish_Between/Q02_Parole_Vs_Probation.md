### Q2. Distinguish between Parole and Probation.

**1. Concept Map:**  
Parole is an administrative post-sentence release scheme for long-term prisoners, whereas probation is a pre-sentence judicial alternative to incarceration, suspending the prison sentence entirely in favor of community supervision.

**2. Comparison Table:**

| 📊 Basis | 🔓 Parole | 📜 Probation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Meaning & Definition** | Conditional early release of a prisoner before completing their sentence. | Suspending a sentence in favor of community supervision. |
| **Timing** | Granted **post-sentence** after the offender has served a portion of their imprisonment in jail. | Granted **pre-sentence** by a court of law, suspending imprisonment entirely. |
| **Authority** | An **executive/administrative decision** made by state parole boards and prison administrations. | A **judicial decision** governed by the Probation of Offenders Act, 1958. |
| **Target Group** | Long-term, serious offenders who have demonstrated reform in confinement. | First-time, youthful, or petty offenders, keeping them out of prison entirely. |
| **Violation Consequences** | Revocation of parole leads to immediate re-confinement for the remainder of the sentence. | Violation of conditions leads to the court imposing the original prison sentence. |

**3. Landmark Case/Statutory Rule:**  
Governed by the **Probation of Offenders Act, 1958**, which acts as a key reformative tool to keep first-time offenders out of traditional prisons.

**🎯 Conclusion:**  
While probation prevents criminal contamination by keeping offenders out of prison entirely, parole encourages good behavior and rehabilitation for those already incarcerated.
