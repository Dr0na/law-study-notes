### Q5. Explain the main Objectives of the Open Prison system.

**📖 Statutory Provision:** Entry 4 of the State List (Seventh Schedule of the Indian Constitution) and State Prison Rules.

**1. Meaning:**  
An open prison is a minimum-security correctional institution characterized by the absence of material precautions against escape (no walls, locked cells, or armed guards) and built on a system of trust and self-discipline.

**2. Key Objectives:**  
*   **Rehabilitation & Social Reintegration**: Easing the transition from closed confinement to free society by allowing well-behaved long-term prisoners to live with their families (as in the Sanganer model in Rajasthan).
*   **Preserving Human Dignity**: Replacing physical barriers with moral boundaries, helping preserve the prisoner's self-esteem and reduce the psychological trauma of incarceration.
*   **Reducing Cost to the State**: Operating costs are significantly lower than those of traditional closed jails, requiring fewer security personnel and less physical infrastructure.
*   **Alleviating Overcrowding**: Redirecting suitable, reformed convicts away from overcrowded traditional jails.

**3. Economic Self-Sustenance:**  
Encouraging prisoners to engage in agriculture or local industries, allowing them to earn normal wages to support their families and pay taxes.

**🎯 Conclusion:**  
As recognized in *Sangat Singh v. State of Rajasthan (1984)*, the open prison system demonstrates that trust-based environments can be highly effective for rehabilitation, offering a successful model for modern corrections.
