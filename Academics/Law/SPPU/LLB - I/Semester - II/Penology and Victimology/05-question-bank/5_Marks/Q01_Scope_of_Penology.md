### Q1. Define Penology and explain its Scope.

**📖 Statutory Provision:** Preamble and Section 53 of the Indian Penal Code (IPC) / BNS (prescribing forms of punishment).

**1. Meaning:**  
Derived from Latin ***Poena*** (punishment) and Greek ***Logia*** (study), Penology is the scientific study of the principles, methods, and institutions of punishment, along with the treatment and rehabilitation of offenders. It represents a vital branch of criminology.

**2. Core Nature & Objective:**  
Penology is an applied and interdisciplinary science. Unlike criminology, which asks *"Why do crimes occur?"*, Penology focuses on *"What should be done to punish, correct, and rehabilitate the offender?"*.

**3. Main Areas of Scope:**  
*   **Theories of Punishment**: Defining the philosophical foundations (Deterrent, Retributive, Preventive, and Reformative theories).
*   **Correctional Administration**: Standardizing prison conditions, health, discipline, and administrative operations under the Prisons Act of 1894.
*   **Alternatives to Incarceration**: Managing community-based correction systems, such as parole, probation, and furlough, to prevent recidivism.
*   **Rehabilitative Structures**: Developing minimum-security institutions, such as open prisons, and organizing vocational training.

**🎯 Conclusion:**  
The scope of Penology is dynamic, having transitioned from an ancient focus on retributive corporal punishment to a modern emphasis on human rights, rehabilitation, and social reintegration.
