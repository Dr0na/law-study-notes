### Q3. Explain the concept of Secondary Victimization (Double Victimization).

**📖 Statutory Provision:** Article 21 (Right to Dignity) and Section 327 of the CrPC / BNSS (mandating in-camera trials for sexual offenses).

**1. Meaning:**  
Secondary Victimization refers to the additional psychological, emotional, or social harm inflicted on a victim of crime not by the offender, but by the criminal justice system itself during the investigation, trial, and rehabilitation phases.

**2. Key Causes in the Justice System:**  
*   **Insensitive Interrogation**: Hostile, repetitive, or unsympathetic questioning by police officers during the initial investigation.
*   **Invasive Medical Examinations**: Lack of privacy and sensitivity during forensic medical exams, particularly in sexual assault cases.
*   **Aggressive Cross-Examination**: Hostile questioning by defense counsel in court designed to discredit the victim or shift blame onto them.
*   **Media Intrusion**: Sensationalized reporting that compromises the victim's privacy and dignity.

**3. Remedial Judicial Guidelines:**  
*   In *Delhi Domestic Working Women's Forum v. UOI (1995)*, the Supreme Court recognized this trauma and directed the provision of legal aid, counseling, and private assistance during trials.
*   Mandatory in-camera trials (Section 327 CrPC) and identity protection (Section 228A IPC) to shield victims from social stigma.

**🎯 Conclusion:**  
Preventing secondary victimization is essential for a fair trial. It requires ongoing police training, sensitive cross-examination rules, and robust victim-support programs.
