### Q4. Outline the NHRC guidelines on Police-Public Relations.

**📖 Statutory Provision:** Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993, and Article 21 of the Indian Constitution.

**1. Meaning:**  
The National Human Rights Commission (NHRC) has issued mandatory guidelines to check custodial violence, protect human rights during police actions, and build mutual trust between the police force and the public.

**2. Key Guidelines & Directives:**  
*   **Mandatory Custodial Death Reporting**: Any death in police or judicial custody must be reported to the NHRC within 24 hours of occurrence.
*   **Video-Recorded Autopsies**: All autopsies for custodial deaths must be video-recorded to prevent manipulation of medical reports.
*   **Decorum During Arrests**: Adhering to the guidelines laid down in *D.K. Basu v. State of West Bengal (1997)*, including preparing a formal arrest memo and notifying a family member.
*   **Community Policing**: Encouraging states to organize local liaison groups (such as *Mohalla Committees*) to foster positive interactions between police officers and citizens.

**3. Objectives:**  
*   Insulating the public from arbitrary arrests and custodial torture.
*   Shifting the police force's image from a colonial instrument of control to a citizen-friendly service.

**🎯 Conclusion:**  
Adhering to the NHRC guidelines is essential to prevent custodial excesses, protect human rights, and establish a cooperative relationship between the police and the public.
