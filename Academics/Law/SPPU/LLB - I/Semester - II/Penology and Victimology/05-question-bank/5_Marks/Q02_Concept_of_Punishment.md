### Q2. Write a short note on the Concept of Punishment.

**📖 Statutory Provision:** Section 53 of the Indian Penal Code (IPC) / Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) prescribing types of punishments.

**1. Meaning:**  
Punishment is the state-sanctioned, intentional infliction of pain, suffering, or deprivation of rights upon an individual who has been formally adjudicated guilty of violating criminal laws.

**2. Five Essential Characteristics:**  
*   It must involve consequences normally considered unpleasant.
*   It must be for a formal breach of legal rules.
*   It must be imposed on the actual or supposed offender.
*   It must be administered by human beings other than the offender.
*   It must be imposed by an authority constituted by the legal system (the State).

**3. Primary Objectives:**  
*   **Protection of Society**: Excluding or disabling dangerous elements to preserve social order.
*   **Specific & General Deterrence**: Preventing the offender from reoffending while serving as a warning to others.
*   **Rehabilitation**: Utilizing corrective treatment to reform the offender's character.

**🎯 Conclusion:**  
As held in *Dr. Jacob George v. State of Kerala (1994)*, punishment is a complex socio-legal tool that must balance deterrence with rehabilitation to achieve balanced justice.
