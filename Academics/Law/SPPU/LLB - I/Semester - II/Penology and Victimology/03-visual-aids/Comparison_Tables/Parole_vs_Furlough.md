# ⚖️ Comparison Table: Parole vs. Furlough

This visual comparison outlines the differences between two key release schemes in modern penology and Indian prison administration.

---

| 📊 Basis | 🔓 Parole | 🏠 Furlough |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Meaning & Definition** | Discretionary, conditional early release of a prisoner before completing their sentence. | Regular, temporary home leave granted to long-term prisoners to maintain family connections. |
| **Legal Nature** | Considered an **executive privilege** rather than a fundamental right. | Considered an **inmate right** (subject to good behavior and prison discipline). |
| **Purpose** | Granted for specific, urgent personal needs (e.g., family illness, marriage, or death). | A routine break designed to restore family bonds and prevent the negative effects of isolation. |
| **Authority** | Decided by **State Parole Boards** and prison administrations. | Decided by the **Inspector General of Prisons** or designated jail officers. |
| **Sentence Computation** | The time spent on parole **does not count** as part of the sentence served. | The time spent on furlough **counts** as part of the sentence served. |
| **Frequency** | Granted on an **as-needed basis** for validated personal emergencies. | Granted at **regular, pre-determined intervals** (e.g., annually) during a long-term sentence. |

---

> [!TIP]
> **Exam Writing Tip:**
> When writing about release schemes, cite the landmark case **Asfaq v. State of Haryana (2017)**. Explain that while both schemes aim to prevent recidivism, they serve distinct purposes: parole is an emergency release, whereas furlough is a routine home leave right.
