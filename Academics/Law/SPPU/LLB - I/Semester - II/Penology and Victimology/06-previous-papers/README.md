## 📈 SPPU Penology & Victimology Exam Trend Map

Based on standard Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU) examination structures, the following recurring question patterns and weightages apply:

### 1. High-Frequency Descriptive Roster (15 Marks)
*   **Theories of Punishment**: Comparative critical analysis of Deterrent, Retributive, Preventive, and Reformative theories, and the dominant theory underlying the modern Indian penal framework.
*   **Prison Reforms**: Evaluating the major ailments of modern prisons (overcrowding, undertrials) and critical evaluation of judicial directions in *Ramamurthy v. State of Karnataka*.
*   **Victim Assistance & Compensation**: Detailed analysis of statutory compensation frameworks under Section 357 CrPC (and BNSS equivalents) along with the landmark *Rudal Shah v. State of Bihar* ruling.

### 2. High-Frequency Descriptive Roster (10 Marks)
*   **Parole vs. Probation**: Comparison of philosophical roots, conditions, legal frameworks, and structural administration.
*   **Police Reforms**: The landmark Prakash Singh guidelines (2006) and Sorabjee Model Police Act directives on separating investigation from law & order.
*   **Theories of Victimology**: Critical application of Precipitation, Lifestyle, Deviant Place, and Routine Activity theories to modern crime scenarios.

### 3. High-Frequency Short Note Roster (5 Marks)
*   *Open Prisons* (Philosophy and advantages)
*   *Secondary Victimization* (Double victimization in trial process)
*   *NHRC guidelines on police-public relations*
*   *Section 53 of the IPC* (Forms of punishment in India)
*   *UN Declaration on Victims' Rights (1985)*
