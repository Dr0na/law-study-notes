# 📖 START HERE - Media Laws Study Path

Media is the "Fourth Estate" of democracy. To master Media Laws, you need to understand the balance between Freedom of Speech and Expression and its reasonable restrictions. Follow this guided path to prepare for your SPPU exams.

## 🏁 Phase 1: The Foundation (Days 1-2)
- Read **Module 01** and **Module 02** in `01-complete-notes/`.
- Understand the basic functions of media and the six theories of the press.
- **Key Task**: Memorize the differences between Authoritarian, Libertarian, and Social Responsibility theories.

## 🏛️ Phase 2: Constitutional Framework (Days 3-5)
- Study **Module 03**. This is the most crucial module.
- Focus deeply on Article 19(1)(a) and Article 19(2).
- **Key Task**: Master the facets of freedom of speech (right to circulate, right to know) and the grounds for reasonable restrictions.

## ⚖️ Phase 3: Legal & Regulatory Dimensions (Days 6-8)
- Dive into **Modules 04 and 05**.
- Focus on Defamation, Contempt of Court, Obscenity, and Sedition.
- Understand the statutory bodies: Press Council of India, TRAI, and ASCI.
- **Key Task**: Differentiate between Self-Regulation and Statutory Regulation.

## 📝 Phase 4: Exam Drill (Days 9-10)
- Go through the **Most Probable Questions** in `05-question-bank/`.
- Practice writing at least two 15-mark answers using the **Answer Writing Template**.
- Review the **Case Law Digest** to ensure you have 2-3 cases for every major topic (e.g., *Romesh Thappar*, *Sakal Papers*, *Hamdard Dawakhana*).

## ⚡ Phase 5: Final Blitz (Last 24 Hours)
- Scan the **Condensed Notes**.
- Review the **All Mnemonics Cheat Sheet**.
- Check the **Doctrines and Maxims** list.
