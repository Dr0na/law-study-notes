# ⚖️ Landmark Cases Summary - Media Laws

This file contains the "Must-Know" cases for your Media Laws exam. Cite these to score high marks.

1.  **Romesh Thappar v. State of Madras**: Freedom of circulation is part of free speech.
2.  **Sakal Papers v. UOI**: State cannot restrict page limits/prices of newspapers.
3.  **K.A. Abbas v. UOI**: Pre-censorship of films is constitutionally valid.
4.  **Kedar Nath Singh v. State of Bihar**: Sedition requires incitement to violence.
5.  **R. Rajagopal v. State of T.N.**: Right to privacy vs. Freedom of Press (Auto Shankar case).
6.  **Sahara India v. SEBI**: Postponement (gag) orders to prevent Media Trials.
7.  **Shreya Singhal v. UOI**: Struck down Sec 66A of IT Act protecting internet free speech.
