# 📄 Condensed Revision: Modules 04 - 05

## 📘 Module 04: Legal Dimensions
- **Sedition (Sec 124A IPC)**: Requires incitement to violence/public disorder (*Kedar Nath Singh*).
- **Obscenity**: Hicklin Test replaced by Community Standards Test (*Ranjit Udeshi*).
- **Defamation**: Civil (Damages) and Criminal (Jail). Exceptions: Truth for public good, fair comment.
- **Contempt of Court**:
  - Civil: Willful disobedience.
  - Criminal: Scandalizing, Prejudicing (Media Trial), Interfering (**S-P-I**).
- **Executive**: RTI Act (Transparency) vs Official Secrets Act (Secrecy).
- **Journalists**: Working Journalists Act provides job security and wage fixation.

## 📘 Module 05: Regulatory Framework
- **Methods**: Statutory (State-backed) vs Self-Regulation (Industry-backed).
- **Statutes (C-C-P-P-T-I)**:
  1.  **Cinematograph Act**: CBFC certifies films (U, UA, A, S).
  2.  **Cable TV Act**: Programme & Advertising Codes.
  3.  **Prasar Bharti**: Autonomy for DD & AIR.
  4.  **Press Council of India (PCI)**: Watchdog for print. Can only warn/censure ("Toothless Tiger").
  5.  **TRAI**: Regulates telecom and broadcasting.
  6.  **Indecent Representation of Women Act**: Bans indecent ads/publications.
- **Self-Regulation**: ASCI (Advertisements).
- **Contemporary Issues**:
  - **Media Trial**: Prejudices fair trial (Art 21).
  - **Paid News**: Deceives voters, condemned by PCI.
  - **Internet**: Governed by IT Act (*Shreya Singhal* struck down Sec 66A).

---
⚡ **Quick Recall**:
- PCI has no punitive powers.
- ASCI is a voluntary self-regulatory body.
- Gag orders (postponement orders) balance free speech and fair trial (*Sahara Case*).
