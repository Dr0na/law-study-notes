# 📄 Condensed Revision: Modules 01 - 03

## 📘 Module 01: Introduction
- **Media**: The "Fourth Estate" of democracy.
- **Kinds**: Print, Electronic, Digital (**P-E-D**).
- **Functions**: Information, Surveillance, Economic, Hold society together, Entertain, Community forum, Political service (**I-S-E-H-E-C-P**).

## 📘 Module 02: History & Theories of Press
- **History**: UK (Abolition of Licensing), USA (First Amendment - Absolute), India (Art 19(1)(a) - Qualified).
- **UDHR Art 19**: Universal right to seek/impart info.
- **Theories of Press (A-L-C-S-D-D)**:
  1.  **Authoritarian**: State control, censorship.
  2.  **Libertarian**: Free marketplace of ideas, watchdog.
  3.  **Communist**: State-owned, socialist propaganda.
  4.  **Social Responsibility**: Freedom + Ethics/Obligations.
  5.  **Development**: Media supports nation-building.
  6.  **Democratic Participant**: Grassroots, anti-commercial.

## 📘 Module 03: Constitutional Framework
- **Art 19(1)(a)**: Freedom of Speech and Expression (Includes Freedom of Press).
- **Facets (P-C-R-A-T-C)**: Press, Circulation, Receive info, Advertise, Telecast, Censorship.
- **Art 19(2) Reasonable Restrictions (S-S-F-P-D-C-D-I)**:
  1. Sovereignty & Integrity
  2. Security of State
  3. Friendly relations
  4. Public order
  5. Decency/Morality
  6. Contempt of Court
  7. Defamation
  8. Incitement to offence
- **Key Cases**:
  - *Romesh Thappar*: Free speech is foundation of democracy.
  - *Sakal Papers*: Right to circulation protected.
  - *K.A. Abbas*: Film censorship is valid.

---
⚡ **Quick Recall**:
- Press freedom is NOT explicitly mentioned in the Constitution.
- Commercial speech (Ads) is protected.
- Prior restraint is generally unconstitutional.
