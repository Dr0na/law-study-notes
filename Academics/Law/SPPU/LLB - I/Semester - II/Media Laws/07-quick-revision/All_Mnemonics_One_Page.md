# 🧠 All Mnemonics Cheat Sheet - Media Laws

Use these mnemonics for rapid recall during the exam!

## 📘 Module 01: Introduction
- **P-E-D**: **P**rint, **E**lectronic, **D**igital (Kinds of Media).
- **I-S-E-H-E-C-P**: **I**nformation, **S**urveillance, **E**conomic, **H**old society together, **E**ntertain, **C**ommunity forum, **P**olitical service (Functions of Media).

## 📘 Module 02: Theories of Press
- **A-L-C-S-D-D**: **A**uthoritarian, **L**ibertarian, **C**ommunist, **S**ocial Responsibility, **D**evelopment, **D**emocratic Participant (6 Theories).

## 📘 Module 03: Constitutional Framework
- **P-C-R-A-T-C**: **P**ress, **C**irculation, **R**eceive info, **A**dvertise, **T**elecast, **C**ensorship (Facets of Art 19(1)(a)).
- **S-S-F-P-D-C-D-I**: **S**overeignty, **S**ecurity, **F**riendly relations, **P**ublic order, **D**ecency, **C**ontempt, **D**efamation, **I**ncitement (8 Grounds of Art 19(2)).

## 📘 Module 04: Legal Dimensions
- **S-O-D**: **S**edition, **O**bscenity, **D**efamation (Criminal Law triad).
- **S-P-I**: **S**candalizing, **P**rejudicing, **I**nterfering (Criminal Contempt).

## 📘 Module 05: Regulatory Framework
- **C-C-P-P-T-I**: **C**inematograph, **C**able, **P**rasar Bharti, **P**ress Council, **T**RAI, **I**ndecent Representation (Key Statutes).
