### Section B: Medium Answers (Answer any 4) - [4 x 10 = 40 Marks]
1.  Explain the Authoritarian and Libertarian theories of the press.
2.  Is 'Commercial Speech' protected under Article 19(1)(a)? Discuss with cases.
3.  Discuss the powers and functions of the Press Council of India.
4.  What is a 'Media Trial'? How does it conflict with the administration of justice?
5.  Discuss the salient features of the Working Journalists Act, 1955.

### Section C: Long Answers (Answer any 2) - [2 x 20 = 40 Marks]
*(Note: SPPU usually has 15-mark questions, but this mock uses 20 for depth)*
1.  "Freedom of the Press is implicit in the Freedom of Speech and Expression." Discuss the various facets of this freedom under Article 19(1)(a) with landmark judgments.
2.  Discuss the grounds on which reasonable restrictions can be imposed on the media under Article 19(2) of the Constitution.
3.  What is Sedition? Discuss the conflict between the law of sedition and freedom of the press in India.
4.  Discuss the conflict between the Right to Privacy and the Freedom of the Press. How have the Indian courts balanced these two competing rights?
