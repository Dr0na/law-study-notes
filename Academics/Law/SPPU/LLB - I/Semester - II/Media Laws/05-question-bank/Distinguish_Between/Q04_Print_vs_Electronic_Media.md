# Q04: Print Media vs Electronic Media

## 📝 Question
Distinguish between Print Media and Electronic Media in terms of constitutional regulation. (5 Marks)

---

## 🧾 MODEL ANSWER

### 1. 🧾 Answer Synopsis
- **Print**: No pre-censorship, private resources.
- **Electronic**: Pre-censorship valid (films), uses public airwaves.

### 2. 🧠 Introduction
While both are protected under Article 19(1)(a), the Supreme Court has allowed different levels of regulation for print and electronic media due to their different impacts.

### 3. 📖 Body (Comparison Table)

| Basis | 📰 Print Media (Newspapers/Books) | 📺 Electronic Media (TV/Radio/Films) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pre-Censorship** | Generally unconstitutional (*Brij Bhushan*). | Valid for films under Cinematograph Act (*K.A. Abbas*). |
| **Impact** | Appeals to intellect; requires literacy. | Appeals to emotions; mass impact regardless of literacy. |
| **Resource Used** | Uses private resources (paper, ink). | Uses airwaves, which are public property. |
| **Regulation Level** | Lighter regulation (Press Council - self-regulatory). | Heavier regulation (TRAI, Cable TV Act, CBFC). |

### 4. 🧾 Conclusion
The "medium is the message." Because electronic media has a more immediate and profound impact on society, the courts have permitted stricter reasonable restrictions on it compared to print media.
