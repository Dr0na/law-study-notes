# Q03: Authoritarian vs Libertarian Theory

## 📝 Question
Distinguish between the Authoritarian and Libertarian theories of the press. (5 Marks)

---

## 🧾 MODEL ANSWER

### 1. 🧾 Answer Synopsis
- **Authoritarian**: State control, truth is a monopoly.
- **Libertarian**: Free market, watchdog role.

### 2. 🧠 Introduction
These two theories represent the extreme ends of how a state views the role of the media.

### 3. 📖 Body (Comparison Table)

| Basis | 👑 Authoritarian Theory | 🕊️ Libertarian Theory |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core Belief** | Truth is the monopoly of the State/Ruler. | Truth emerges from a free marketplace of ideas. |
| **Role of Press** | To support and advance the policies of the government. | To act as a watchdog and inform the public. |
| **Control Mechanism** | Censorship, licensing, severe punishments. | Minimal state intervention; post-publication laws apply. |
| **Ownership** | Often private, but strictly controlled by the State. | Private ownership in a free market. |

### 4. 🧾 Conclusion
Democracies have moved away from the Authoritarian model towards the Libertarian model, though most now practice a modified "Social Responsibility" version.
