# Q01: Statutory Regulation vs Self-Regulation

## 📝 Question
Distinguish between Statutory Regulation and Self-Regulation of media. (5 Marks)

---

## 🧾 MODEL ANSWER

### 1. 🧾 Answer Synopsis
- **Statutory**: State-backed, coercive, rigid.
- **Self-Regulation**: Industry-backed, voluntary, flexible.
- **Examples**: CBFC vs ASCI.

### 2. 🧠 Introduction
Media regulation ensures accountability. It can be imposed by the government (Statutory) or adopted voluntarily by the media industry itself (Self-Regulation).

### 3. 📖 Body (Comparison Table)

| Basis | 🏛️ Statutory Regulation | 🤝 Self-Regulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Origin** | Created by Acts of Parliament. | Created by media professionals/industry bodies. |
| **Authority** | Backed by the coercive power of the State. | Backed by voluntary compliance and peer pressure. |
| **Punitive Power** | Can impose fines, revoke licenses, or imprison. | Usually limited to warnings, censures, or apologies. |
| **Flexibility** | Rigid; requires legislative amendment to change. | Highly flexible; can adapt quickly to new trends. |
| **Examples** | CBFC (Films), TRAI (Telecom). | ASCI (Ads), NBSA (News Broadcasters). |

### 4. 🧾 Conclusion
While statutory regulation has "teeth," it risks government overreach. Self-regulation preserves media independence but is often criticized for lacking enforcement power.
