# Q05: Civil vs Criminal Contempt of Court

## 📝 Question
Distinguish between Civil Contempt and Criminal Contempt of Court. (5 Marks)

---

## 🧾 MODEL ANSWER

### 1. 🧾 Answer Synopsis
- **Civil**: Disobedience of a court order.
- **Criminal**: Scandalizing the court or prejudicing a trial.

### 2. 🧠 Introduction
The Contempt of Courts Act, 1971 protects the dignity of the judiciary. It classifies contempt into two distinct categories.

### 3. 📖 Body (Comparison Table)

| Basis | 📄 Civil Contempt | 🚨 Criminal Contempt |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Meaning** | Willful disobedience to any judgment, decree, direction, or order of a court. | Publication or act that scandalizes the court or prejudices a judicial proceeding. |
| **Nature** | Private in nature; affects a party to the case. | Public in nature; affects the administration of justice itself. |
| **Media Relevance**| Less relevant for media (unless media disobeys a specific gag order). | Highly relevant (e.g., Media Trials, publishing defamatory articles against judges). |
| **Objective** | To enforce compliance with court orders. | To maintain the dignity of the court and ensure fair trials. |

### 4. 🧾 Conclusion
For journalists, understanding Criminal Contempt is crucial, as aggressive reporting on pending cases (*sub judice* matters) can easily cross the line into prejudicing a trial.
