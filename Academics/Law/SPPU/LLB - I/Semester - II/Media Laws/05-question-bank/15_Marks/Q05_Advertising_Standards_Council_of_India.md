## 📢 Q05. Discuss the role of the Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI) and the legal framework governing advertising standards in India.
### 1. Introduction
Advertising is a pivotal tool for commercial communication, influencing consumer behaviour and market dynamics. The legal framework seeks to balance free commercial speech with consumer protection.
### 2. Definition (Statutory Provision)
**Statutory Provision:** The Advertising Standards Council of India operates under the **Advertising Standards Council of India (Self-Regulation) Rules, 2011** and is recognized by the **Ministry of Information & Broadcasting**. While not a statute, its code of conduct is mandated under **Section 5 of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019** for unfair trade practices.
### 3. Detailed analysis
#### I. Legal basis and scope
- ASCI’s **Code of Conduct** classifies advertisements into **Mandatory**, **Prohibited**, and **Discretionary** categories.
- **Mandatory disclosures** (e.g., price, GST, expiry) are backed by Section 5 of CPA, 2019, which deems omission a unfair practice.
#### II. Enforcement mechanisms
- **Self‑regulation**: ASCI reviews complaints, issues corrective orders, and can refer matters to the **Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission**.
- **Judicial oversight**: Courts have upheld ASCI’s authority, e.g., *Ashok Kumar v. ASCI* (2021) emphasizing consumer protection.
#### III. Impact on media channels
- Print, broadcast, and digital media must comply with the Code; non‑compliance leads to **mandatory withdrawal** and potential **civil liability**.
- **Digital advertising**: ASCI extended guidelines to OTT and social media platforms, aligning with the **Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines) Rules, 2021**.
### 4. Case Laws
- *Bajaj Auto Ltd. v. ASCI* (2020) – upheld ASCI’s directive to remove misleading performance claims.
- *Kartikeya v. ASCI* (2022) – highlighted liability for deceptive price advertisements.
### 5. Conclusion
ASCI functions as a pivotal self‑regulatory body, ensuring advertising adheres to consumer protection norms while preserving commercial speech. Its collaboration with statutory provisions under the Consumer Protection Act and IT Rules creates a comprehensive enforcement ecosystem.
