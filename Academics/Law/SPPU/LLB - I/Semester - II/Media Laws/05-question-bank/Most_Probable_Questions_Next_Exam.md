# 🔥 Most Probable Questions - Media Laws (Next Exam: May 2026)

Based on SPPU (Savitribai Phule Pune University) LLB PYQ trends, syllabus weightage, and contemporary legal relevance for the May 2026 exams, here is the reconfirmed probability list. 

SPPU heavily tests the **Constitutional Framework (Article 19)**, **Regulatory Bodies (Press Council/CBFC)**, and **Contemporary Conflicts (Media Trial/Privacy)**.

## 🔴 High Probability (Must Prepare - Frequently Repeated)
These questions form the core of Media Laws and appear in almost every SPPU paper in some form (either as 15-mark essays or 10-mark notes).

1.  **Facets of Art 19(1)(a)**: "Freedom of the Press is implicit in the Freedom of Speech and Expression." Discuss with case laws (e.g., *Romesh Thappar*, *Indian Express*). [Model Answer](./15_Marks/Q01_Facets_of_Art_19_1_a.md)
2.  **Reasonable Restrictions**: Discuss the 8 grounds under Article 19(2) that restrict media freedom. [Model Answer](./15_Marks/Q03_Reasonable_Restrictions.md)
3.  **Press Council of India**: Discuss its powers, functions, and the criticism of it being a "Toothless Tiger". [Model Answer](./10_Marks/Q06_Press_Council.md)
4.  **Media Trial**: What is Trial by Media? How does it conflict with the administration of justice and fair trial? [Model Answer](./10_Marks/Q08_Media_Trial.md)
5.  **Censorship (Films vs Print)**: Why is pre-censorship valid for films (Cinematograph Act) but generally invalid for print media? Discuss *K.A. Abbas*. [Model Answer](./15_Marks/Q07_Censorship_Films_vs_Print.md)
6.  **Defamation**: Distinguish between Civil and Criminal Defamation in the context of media liability. [Model Answer](./Distinguish_Between/Q02_Civil_vs_Criminal_Defamation.md)

## 🟠 Medium Probability (Important Concepts)
These are alternate major questions or standard 10-mark / distinguish questions.

1.  **Privacy vs Press**: How do courts balance the Right to Privacy (Art 21) with Freedom of Press? Discuss *R. Rajagopal (Auto Shankar case)*. [Model Answer](./15_Marks/Q09_Privacy_vs_Press.md)
2.  **Theories of Press**: Explain the Authoritarian, Libertarian, Social Responsibility, and Soviet Media theories. [Model Answer](./10_Marks/Q02_Theories_of_Press.md)
3.  **Commercial Speech**: Is commercial advertisement protected under Art 19(1)(a)? Discuss the *Tata Press* case. [Model Answer](./10_Marks/Q04_Commercial_Speech.md)
4.  **Cable TV Regulation**: Discuss the role of the Cable Television Networks (Regulation) Act, 1995. [Model Answer](./15_Marks/Q04_Role_of_Cable_TV_Regulation_Act_1995.md)
5.  **Sedition and Media**: Discuss the conflict between Section 124A IPC and media freedom (*Kedar Nath Singh*). [Model Answer](./15_Marks/Q06_Sedition_and_Media.md)
6.  **Statutory vs Self-Regulation**: Distinguish between the two regulatory approaches. [Model Answer](./Distinguish_Between/Q01_Statutory_vs_Self_Regulation.md)
