# Q08: Sting Operations

## 📝 Question
Write a short note on the legality of Sting Operations by the media. (5 Marks)

---

## 🧾 MODEL ANSWER

### 1. 🧾 Answer Synopsis
- **Meaning**: Undercover investigative journalism.
- **Legality**: Valid if in public interest; illegal if for extortion/privacy invasion.
- **Conflict**: Right to Know vs. Right to Privacy.

### 2. 🧠 Introduction
A sting operation involves deceptive tactics by journalists (such as hidden cameras) to expose corruption, crime, or wrongdoing.

### 3. 📖 Body
- **Public Interest**: Sting operations are a vital tool of investigative journalism. If conducted to expose a truth that serves the larger public interest (e.g., exposing a corrupt official), they are protected under Article 19(1)(a).
- **Misuse and Privacy**: However, they cannot be used for blackmail, extortion, or to invade a person's private life without a compelling public reason. The Right to Privacy (Article 21) acts as a boundary.
- **Entrapment**: Media cannot induce or encourage an innocent person to commit a crime just to record it.

### 4. ⚖️ Case Laws
- **R.K. Anand v. Delhi High Court**: The Supreme Court upheld the conviction of lawyers exposed in a sting operation for subverting justice, but cautioned the media against irresponsible stings.

### 5. 🧾 Conclusion
Sting operations are a double-edged sword. They must be used ethically and strictly as a last resort to uncover truths that cannot be found through normal journalistic means.
