# Q04: Paid News

## 📝 Question
Write a short note on the menace of 'Paid News'. (5 Marks)

---

## 🧾 MODEL ANSWER

### 1. 🧾 Answer Synopsis
- **Meaning**: Advertising disguised as news.
- **Impact**: Deceives voters, undermines democracy.
- **Regulation**: Press Council of India guidelines.

### 2. 🧠 Introduction
'Paid News' is a corrupt practice where media organizations publish favorable articles or news reports in exchange for money, passing them off as objective editorial content.

### 3. 📖 Body
- **The Menace**: It is a serious fraud on the public. It destroys the credibility of the media and denies citizens their right to correct information.
- **Electoral Impact**: It is particularly rampant during elections. Candidates pay for positive coverage, creating an uneven playing field and deceiving the electorate.
- **Regulatory Action**: The Press Council of India (PCI) has strongly condemned this practice. The Election Commission of India also monitors paid news to include such expenses in the candidate's election expenditure.

### 4. ⚖️ Case Laws
- **Ashok Shankarrao Chavan v. Madhavrao Kinhalkar**: The Supreme Court upheld the Election Commission's power to disqualify a candidate for failing to accurately report expenses related to paid news.

### 5. 🧾 Conclusion
Paid news is a dual threat: it compromises journalistic ethics and subverts the democratic electoral process. Strict statutory action is required to curb it.
