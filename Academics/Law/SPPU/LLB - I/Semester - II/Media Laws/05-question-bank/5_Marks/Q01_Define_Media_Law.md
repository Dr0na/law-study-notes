### Q01. Define Media Law and its scope.

**📖 Statutory Provision:** No specific statute defines "Media Law"; it is an umbrella term covering various acts such as the Press and Registration of Books Act, 1867; The Information Technology Act, 2000; and the Cable Television Networks (Regulation) Act, 1995.

**1. Meaning:** Media Law refers to the body of legal rules that regulate the creation, dissemination, and consumption of media content, encompassing print, broadcast, and digital platforms.

**2. Core Components:**
- **Freedom of Speech & Expression** (Article 19(1)(a) Constitution of India).
- **Regulatory Statutes** governing specific media forms (e.g., IT Act for digital media, Prasar Bharati Act for public broadcasting).
- **Content Restrictions** such as defamation, obscenity, and contempt provisions.

**3. Objectives:**
- Safeguard democratic discourse.
- Prevent misuse of media for hate, falsehood, or national security threats.
- Ensure fair competition and consumer protection in advertising.

**🎯 Conclusion:** Media Law is a composite framework that balances constitutional freedoms with statutory controls to maintain a responsible and vibrant media ecosystem.
