# Q09: ASCI

## 📝 Question
What is the role of the Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI)? (5 Marks)

---

## 🧾 MODEL ANSWER

### 1. 🧾 Answer Synopsis
- **Nature**: Voluntary self-regulatory body.
- **Purpose**: Regulate advertising content.
- **Code**: Truthful, decent, safe, and fair.

### 2. 🧠 Introduction
The Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI) is a non-statutory, voluntary self-regulatory organization established in 1985 by professionals from the advertising and media industry.

### 3. 📖 Body
- **Objective**: To ensure that advertisements conform to its Code for Self-Regulation.
- **The ASCI Code**: Requires ads to be:
  1.  **Truthful and Honest**: No misleading claims.
  2.  **Non-Offensive**: Should not offend generally accepted standards of public decency.
  3.  **Safe**: Should not promote hazardous products or unsafe behavior (especially regarding children).
  4.  **Fair**: Should not unfairly denigrate competitors.
- **Functioning**: It investigates consumer complaints. If an ad violates the code, ASCI asks the advertiser to withdraw or modify it.

### 4. 🧾 Conclusion
While ASCI lacks the coercive power of a court, its guidelines are highly respected. Furthermore, statutory bodies like the Cable TV Networks Rules mandate that all TV ads must comply with the ASCI Code, giving it indirect legal backing.
