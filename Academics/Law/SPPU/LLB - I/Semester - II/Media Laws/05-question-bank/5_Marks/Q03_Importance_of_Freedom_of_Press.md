### Q03. Explain the importance of Freedom of Press in India.

**📖 Statutory Provision:** Article 19(1)(a) of the Indian Constitution – Right to freedom of speech and expression, which inherently includes freedom of the press.

**1. Meaning:** Freedom of press enables the media to disseminate information, opinions, and criticism without prior restraint.

**2. Democratic Function:**
- **Watchdog Role:** Holds the government accountable and exposes corruption.
- **Public Opinion Shaping:** Informs citizens, facilitating informed voting and civic participation.
- **Platform for Debate:** Encourages diverse viewpoints, essential for a pluralistic society.

**3. Legal Safeguards:**
- The Constitution provides that reasonable restrictions may be imposed only on grounds such as sovereignty, integrity, security, or public order (Article 19(2)).
- Judicial precedents (e.g., *Romesh Thappar v. State of Madras*) have reinforced that any prior restraint is presumptively unconstitutional.

**🎯 Conclusion:** Freedom of press is a cornerstone of Indian democracy, ensuring transparency, accountability, and an informed electorate, while being subject only to narrowly defined reasonable restrictions.
