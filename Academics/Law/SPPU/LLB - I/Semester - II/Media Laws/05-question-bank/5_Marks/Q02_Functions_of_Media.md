# Q02: Functions of Media

## 📝 Question
Write a short note on the functions of media in a democratic society. (5 Marks)

---

## 🧾 MODEL ANSWER

### 1. 🧾 Answer Synopsis
- **Introduction**: Media as the Fourth Estate.
- **Functions**: Information, Surveillance, Entertainment, Community Forum.
- **Conclusion**: Essential for an informed citizenry.

### 2. 🧠 Introduction
In a participatory democracy, the media acts as the "Fourth Estate." Its primary role is to act as a bridge between the state and the citizens, ensuring transparency and accountability.

### 3. 📖 Body
The core functions of media include:
1.  **Information**: Gathering and disseminating factual news to keep the public informed about local and global events.
2.  **Surveillance**: Acting as a watchdog by monitoring the environment and exposing corruption or societal threats (Investigative journalism).
3.  **Community Forum**: Providing a platform for public debate, allowing diverse opinions to be heard (Editorials, Op-eds).
4.  **Entertainment**: Providing relaxation and amusement through movies, music, and sports.
5.  **Social Cohesion**: Transmitting cultural values and holding society together.

### 4. 🧾 Conclusion
Without these functions, a democracy would be blind and mute. The media empowers citizens to make informed choices, especially during elections.
