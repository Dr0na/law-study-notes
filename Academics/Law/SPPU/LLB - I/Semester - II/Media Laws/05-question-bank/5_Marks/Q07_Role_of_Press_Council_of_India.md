### Q07. Outline the role of the Press Council of India.
**📖 Statutory Provision:** Press Council of India Act, 1978.
**1. Meaning:** The Press Council is a quasi‑judicial body established to maintain and improve the standards of newspapers and news agencies, and to safeguard the freedom of the press.
**2. Key Functions:**
- **a) Grievance Redressal:** Receives complaints regarding breach of journalistic ethics and can issue admonitions, fines, or withdraw recognition.
- **b) Code of Ethics:** Frames and updates the *Norms of Journalistic Conduct* applied to all print media.
- **c) Inquiry & Inspection:** Conducts periodic inspections of newspaper offices and audits compliance with ethical standards.
- **d) Advisory Role:** Advises Parliament and the government on law‑making affecting the press, e.g., amendments to the Press & Registration of Books Act.
**3. Composition:** Comprises a Chairman (appointed by the President), elected members from newspaper societies, and nominated members representing the public, ensuring a balanced representation.
**🎯 Conclusion:** The Press Council serves as a watchdog that balances press freedom with responsibility, promoting ethical journalism while providing a forum for redressing media‑related disputes.
