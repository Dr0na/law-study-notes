### Q10. Explain the purpose of the Information Technology Act, 2000 in relation to media.

**📖 Statutory Provision:** Information Technology Act, 2000 (IT Act) – Sections 79, 85, 86, and 87 deal with intermediaries, publishing of electronic content, and penalties for offensive material.

**1. Meaning:** The IT Act regulates electronic communication, digital publishing, and online intermediaries, providing a legal framework for media operating in the digital realm.

**2. Core Objectives for Media:**
- **Legal Recognition of Digital Content:** Grants legal validity to electronic records, enabling online news portals and blogs to be treated as publishing entities.
- **Intermediary Liability:** Section 79 limits the liability of internet service providers, social media platforms, and online news aggregators, provided they follow due‑diligence and grievance‑redress mechanisms.
- **Offensive Content Regulation:** Sections 85‑87 empower the government to block or penalise publishing of material deemed obscene, defamatory, or a threat to public order.
- **Data Protection & Privacy:** Though largely superseded by the Personal Data Protection Bill, the IT Act addresses cyber‑crimes affecting media houses (e.g., hacking of news websites).

**3. Impact on Media Landscape:**
- Enables rapid dissemination of news through digital platforms while imposing a compliance regime.
- Forces media outlets to maintain a **Grievance Redress Mechanism** and appoint a **Compliance Officer** to avoid liability.

**🎯 Conclusion:** The IT Act provides the essential statutory backbone for digital media in India, balancing the facilitation of electronic publishing with safeguards against misuse and harmful content.
