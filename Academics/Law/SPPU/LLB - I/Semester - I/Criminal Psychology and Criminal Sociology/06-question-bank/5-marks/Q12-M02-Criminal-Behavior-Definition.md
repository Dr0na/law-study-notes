# 📝 Question: Define Criminal Behavior and explain its psychodynamics. (5 Marks)

---

# ✍️ Answer:

## 🎯 Definition

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

**Criminal Behavior** is intentional behavior that violates criminal law and is punishable by the state. It represents actions or omissions prohibited by law and subject to formal sanctions.

</div>

---

## 🧠 Psychodynamics of Criminal Behavior

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

**Psychodynamics** refers to psychological forces and processes that motivate human behavior, particularly unconscious mental processes.

### 4 Key Elements:

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

**1. Unconscious Motivations:** Many criminal acts driven by unconscious needs, conflicts, desires person may not be aware of

**2. Childhood Experiences:** Early trauma, abuse, neglect shape personality and predispose to antisocial behavior

**3. Id-Ego-Superego Imbalance:** Weak ego cannot control id's impulses, or weak superego provides no moral guidance

**4. Defense Mechanisms:** Criminals use rationalization, projection, denial to cope with guilt and justify behavior

</div>

</div>

---

## 🎯 Conclusion

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

Criminal behavior is complex interplay of conscious choices and unconscious psychological forces shaped by developmental experiences, personality structure, and environmental factors.

</div>
