## Question
**What is Habeas Corpus? When is it issued?**

**Marks**: 2  
**Pattern**: ⚖️ Doctrinal  
**Topic**: Module 5 - Writs  
**Exam Frequency**: 🔥🔥🔥🔥 (Very common)  
**Time**: 2-3 minutes  

---

## 📋 MODEL ANSWER

**Habeas Corpus:**
Habeas Corpus is a writ meaning **"produce the body"** or "you may have the body" in Latin. It is issued by courts under Articles 32 (Supreme Court) and 226 (High Courts) to release a person from **illegal or unlawful detention**. It is the most important writ for protecting personal liberty.

**When Issued:**
- Person detained without authority of law
- Detention without following proper procedure
- Preventive detention misused
- Private illegal detention
- Custody without proper orders

**Procedure:** Court directs the detaining authority to produce the detained person before it, examines the legality of detention, and if found illegal, orders immediate release.

**Famous Case:** **Rudul Sah v. State of Bihar (1983)** - Person kept in jail even after acquittal, released through Habeas Corpus.
