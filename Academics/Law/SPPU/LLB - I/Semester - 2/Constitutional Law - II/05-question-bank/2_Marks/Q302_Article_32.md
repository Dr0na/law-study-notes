# 📝 Question ID: CON2-2M-Q302

## Question
**What is Article 32? Why did Dr. Ambedkar call it the "Heart and Soul" of the Constitution?**

**Marks**: 2  
**Pattern**: 🧠 Conceptual  
**Topic**: Module 5 - Supreme Court  
**Exam Frequency**: 🔥🔥🔥🔥 (Very common)  
**Time**: 2-3 minutes  

---

## 📋 MODEL ANSWER

**Article 32 - Right to Constitutional Remedies:**
Article 32 guarantees the **right to move the Supreme Court** for enforcement of Fundamental Rights. It empowers the Supreme Court to issue **five types of writs** - Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Certiorari, and Quo Warranto - for protection of Fundamental Rights.

**Why "Heart and Soul":**
Dr. B.R. Ambedkar called Article 32 the **"Heart and Soul"** of the Constitution because:

1. **Without remedy, rights are meaningless** - Rights without enforcement mechanisms are useless
2. **Guarantees access to justice** - Anyone can directly approach Supreme Court
3. **Supreme Court bound to entertain** - Cannot refuse petitions under Article 32
4. **Makes other rights effective** - Ensures all Fundamental Rights can be enforced

**Significance:** Article 32 is itself a **Fundamental Right** (Part III), ensuring even during emergency (except when suspended), people have constitutional remedy.
