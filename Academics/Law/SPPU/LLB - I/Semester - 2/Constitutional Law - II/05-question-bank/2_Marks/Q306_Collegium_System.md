# 📝 Question ID: CON2-2M-Q306

## Question
**What is the collegium system for appointment of judges?**

**Marks**: 2  
**Pattern**: ⚖️ Doctrinal  
**Topic**: Module 5 - Judicial Appointments  
**Exam Frequency**: 🔥🔥🔥 (Common)  
**Time**: 2-3 minutes  

---

## 📋 MODEL ANSWER

**Collegium System:**
The collegium system for judicial appointments evolved through the **Second Judges Case (1993)** and **Third Judges Case (1998)**, whereby a group of senior judges recommend names for appointment of Supreme Court and High Court judges, with the Executive having minimal role.

**Composition:**
- For **Supreme Court**: CJI + 4 senior-most judges (5-judge collegium)
- For **High Court**: CJI + 2 SC judges + Chief Justice of that High Court

**Process:** Collegium recommends names to the President who appoints accordingly. If government rejects, collegium can reiterate, then recommendation becomes binding.

**Significance:** Ensures independence of judiciary from Executive interference, though criticized for lack of transparency and accountability.

---

## 🎯 Key Points

✅ **Second and Third Judges Cases** mentioned  
✅ **CJI + 4** (SC) and **CJI + 2 + HC CJ** (HC)  
✅ **Judges select judges** principle  
✅ **Reiteration makes binding**  
✅ **Independence** from Executive
