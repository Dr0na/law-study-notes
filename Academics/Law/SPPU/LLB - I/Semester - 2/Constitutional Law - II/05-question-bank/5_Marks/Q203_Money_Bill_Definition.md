# 📝 Question ID: CON2-5M-Q203

## Question
**What is a Money Bill under Article 110? Who certifies whether a Bill is a Money Bill?**

**Marks**: 5  
**Pattern**: ⚖️ Doctrinal  
**Topic**: Module 4 - Money Bills  
**Exam Frequency**: 🔥🔥🔥🔥 (Very frequent)  
**Time**: 6-7 minutes  

---

## 📋 MODEL ANSWER

### ⚖️ Article 110 - Definition of Money Bill (3 marks)

**What is a Money Bill:**
A Bill is deemed to be a Money Bill if it contains **only** provisions dealing with matters specified in Article 110. If it contains other matters too, it's not a Money Bill.

### 9 Matters Under Article 110 (COUNT = 9)

**A Bill is Money Bill if it deals with:**

1️⃣ **Imposition, abolition, remission, alteration** of any tax  
2️⃣ **Regulation of borrowing** of money by Government  
3️⃣ **Consolidated Fund or Contingency Fund** of India  
4️⃣ **Custody** of Consolidated Fund or Contingency Fund  
5️⃣ **Appropriation** of money from Consolidated Fund  
6️⃣ **Declaration** of any expenditure charged on Consolidated Fund  
7️⃣ **Receipt** of money on account of Consolidated Fund  
8️⃣ **Audit** of accounts of Union or States  
9️⃣ **Any matter incidental** to the above matters  

🧠 **Memory**: **"I-R-C-C-A-D-R-A-I"** = **"Taj Mahal Built Carefully"** (9 matters)

**Key Point:** Must contain **ONLY** these matters. If contains one provision outside these 9 matters, NOT a Money Bill.

**Examples of Money Bills:**
- Finance Bill (annual budget) - taxes, appropriation
- Bills amending tax laws
- Bills for withdrawal from Consolidated Fund

---

### 👨‍⚖️ Certification by Speaker (Article 110(3)) (2 marks)

**Who Decides:**
The **Speaker of Lok Sabha** certifies whether a Bill is a Money Bill or not.

**Key Features of Speaker's Certificate:**

#### Feature 1: Final and Conclusive 📜
**Nature:** Speaker's decision is **final** and **conclusive**.

- Cannot be questioned in any court
- Not subject to judicial review
- Binding on all (Parliament, President, Courts)

#### Feature 2: When Given ⏰
**Timing:** When a Bill is transmitted to Rajya Sabha and when presented to President.

- Endorsed on Bill: "This is a Money Bill"
- Certificate attached

#### Feature 3: Parliamentary Sovereignty 🏛️
**Reason:** Protects parliamentary autonomy from judicial interference.

- Courts cannot second-guess Speaker
- Parliament supreme in its internal matters
- Separation of powers maintained

**Controversy:** Sometimes misused - ordinary bills certified as Money Bills to bypass Rajya Sabha (Aadhaar Act controversy)

---

### Conclusion (Brief)

Money Bills, defined under Article 110, can only originate in Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha has limited role (14 days, recommend only). The **Speaker's certificate** being final ensures parliamentary autonomy, though it has led to debates when used to bypass Rajya Sabha on contentious legislation.
