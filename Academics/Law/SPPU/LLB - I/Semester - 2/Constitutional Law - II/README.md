# Constitutional Law - II (LCC 0601)

**Course**: Third Year B.A., LL.B. / B.B.A., LL.B. (Semester VI) | First Year LL.B. (Semester II)  
**Credits**: 4 Credits  
**University**: Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU)

---

## 📚 Course Overview

This course acquaints students with the Federal principles of the Indian Constitution and the powers, functions, and structures of various Constitutional bodies. The course is to be studied in the social, economic, and political context in which the constitution operates.

## 📖 Module Breakdown

### Module 1: Nature of Indian Federalism
- Essential characteristics
- Comparison with American Federalism
- Basic Structure Doctrine

### Module 2: Distribution of Legislative and Executive Powers
- Legislative powers distribution
- Territorial extent and jurisdiction
- Executive powers and coordination

### Module 3: Distribution of Financial Powers
- Taxing powers allocation
- Revenue distribution
- Grant-in-Aid provisions

### Module 4: Union and State Legislature
- Structure and composition
- Powers and functions
- Law-making procedure

### Module 5: Supreme Court, High Court and Tribunals
- Judicial independence
- Appointment and powers
- Judicial accountability

### Module 6: Union and State Executive
- President, Vice-President, Governor
- Council of Ministers
- Executive-Legislature interaction

### Module 7: Other Constitutional Institutions
- UPSC, Election Commission
- CAG, Attorney General
- Local self-governing bodies

### Module 8: Emergency Provisions
- National Emergency
- Constitutional Machinery failure
- Financial Emergency
