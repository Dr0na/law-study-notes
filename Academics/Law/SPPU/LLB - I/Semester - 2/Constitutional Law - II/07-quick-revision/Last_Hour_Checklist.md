## ✅ Minute 31-40: Amendments (10 minutes)

### Key Amendments - Write Down:

**42nd Amendment (1976) - Emergency Period:**
- [ ] Made President "shall act" on advice (binding)
- [ ] Most controversial amendment
- [ ] Many provisions struck down later

**44th Amendment (1978) - Post-Emergency Reforms:**
- [ ] "Armed rebellion" (not "internal disturbance")
- [ ] Cabinet recommendation mandatory
- [ ] Special majority for emergency
- [ ] Articles 20-21 cannot be suspended
- [ ] Lok Sabha can revoke emergency
- [ ] President can ask reconsider advice (one time)

**52nd Amendment (1985):**
- [ ] Added Tenth Schedule (Anti-Defection)
- [ ] 4 grounds + 2 exceptions
- [ ] Speaker decides

**73rd Amendment (1992):**
- [ ] Panchayati Raj constitutional status
- [ ] Part IX + Eleventh Schedule (29 subjects)
- [ ] 3-tier system
- [ ] 1/3 women reservation

**74th Amendment (1992):**
- [ ] Municipalities constitutional status
- [ ] Part IXA + Twelfth Schedule (18 subjects)
- [ ] 3 types: Nagar Panchayat, Council, Corporation
- [ ] 1/3 women reservation

**91st Amendment (2003):**
- [ ] Council size: Maximum 15% of Assembly strength
- [ ] Minimum 12 Ministers

**99th Amendment (2014):**
- [ ] Created NJAC
- [ ] Struck down in 2015

**101st Amendment (2016):**
- [ ] GST introduced
- [ ] Articles 246A, 279A added
