# 📋 Article Numbers - Quick Reference Sheet

## 🎯 All Critical Articles - One Page

## 📚 MODULE 1: FEDERALISM

**Article 1** = India is a "Union of States"  
**Article 368** = Amendment procedure  
**Seventh Schedule** = Three Lists (Union, State, Concurrent)  

---

## 📚 MODULE 2: LEGISLATIVE & EXECUTIVE POWERS

### Legislative:
**Article 245** = Territorial extent of laws  
**Article 246** = Subject matter (Three Lists)  
  - 246(1) = Union List (Parliament exclusive)  
  - 246(2) = Concurrent List (Both)  
  - 246(3) = State List (State exclusive)  
**Article 248** = Residuary powers (with Parliament)  
**Article 249** = Rajya Sabha resolution (2/3) → Parliament can legislate on State  
**Article 250** = Emergency → Parliament can legislate on State List  
**Article 252** = Consent of States → Parliament legislates  
**Article 253** = International treaties → Parliament can legislate  
**Article 254** = Repugnancy - Union law prevails on Concurrent  

### Executive:
**Article 73** = Union executive power  
**Article 162** = State executive power  
**Article 256** = States comply with Union directions  
**Article 257** = Union control over States  
**Article 258** = Union delegates to State  
**Article 258A** = State delegates to Union  
**Article 263** = Inter-State Council  
**Article 312** = All India Services  

---

## 📚 MODULE 3: FINANCIAL POWERS

**Article 264-293** = Part XII (Financial Relations)  
**Article 268** = Taxes assigned to States  
**Article 269** = Taxes collected by Union, assigned to States  
**Article 270** = Taxes shared between Union and States  
**Article 275** = Grants-in-Aid (statutory)  
**Article 280** = Finance Commission (every 5 years)  
**Article 282** = Discretionary grants  
**Article 285** = States cannot tax Union property  
**Article 286** = No tax on inter-state trade  
**Article 292** = Union's borrowing power  
**Article 293** = State's borrowing power (with Union consent if indebted)  
**Article 246A** = GST - Both can legislate  
**Article 279A** = GST Council  

---

## 📚 MODULE 4: LEGISLATURE

### Parliament:
**Article 79** = Constitution of Parliament (President + 2 Houses)  
**Article 80** = Composition of Rajya Sabha  
**Article 81** = Composition of Lok Sabha  
**Article 84** = Qualifications (25/30 years, citizen)  
**Article 102** = Disqualifications  
**Article 105** = Privileges of members  
**Article 108** = Joint Sitting (for deadlock)  
**Article 110** = Money Bills (definition, Speaker certifies)  
**Article 111** = President's assent  
**Article 123** = President's Ordinance power  
**Tenth Schedule** = Anti-Defection Law (52nd Amendment, 1985)  

### State Legislature:
**Article 168** = Constitution of State Legislature  
**Article 170-171** = Composition of Assembly and Council  
**Article 173** = Qualifications (25/30 years)  
**Article 194** = Privileges of State Legislature  
**Article 213** = Governor's Ordinance power  

---

## 📚 MODULE 5: JUDICIARY

### Supreme Court:
**Article 124** = Establishment & appointment (retire at 65)  
**Article 124(4)** = Removal by impeachment  
**Article 124A** = NJAC (struck down in 2015)  
**Article 129** = SC is court of record  
**Article 131** = Original jurisdiction (Centre-State disputes)  
**Article 32** = Writ jurisdiction (only FRs) - "Heart and Soul"  
**Article 132** = Appellate jurisdiction (constitutional cases)  
**Article 133** = Appellate (civil cases)  
**Article 134** = Appellate (criminal cases)  
**Article 136** = Special Leave Petition (SLP) - most important  
**Article 137** = Review of own judgments  
**Article 141** = Law declared by SC binding on all courts  
**Article 142** = Complete justice  
**Article 143** = Advisory jurisdiction  

### High Courts:
**Article 214** = High Courts for States  
**Article 217** = Appointment (retire at 62)  
**Article 226** = Writ jurisdiction (FRs + other rights) - WIDER than 32  
**Article 227** = Superintendence over subordinate courts  

### Tribunals:
**Article 323A** = Administrative Tribunals (CAT, SAT)  
**Article 323B** = Other Tribunals (ITAT, NGT, etc.)

**Article 75** = Appointment of PM and Ministers  
**Article 75(3)** = Collective responsibility to Lok Sabha  
**Article 78** = PM's duties toward President  
**Article 85** = Summoning Parliament  
**Article 111** = Assent to bills  
**Article 123** = Ordinance power  

### State Executive:
**Article 153** = Governor of State  
**Article 154** = Executive power of State  
**Article 155** = Appointment of Governor  
**Article 156** = Term (5 years, President's pleasure)  
**Article 157** = Qualifications (35 years)  
**Article 161** = Governor's pardon (not death sentences)  
**Article 163** = Council of Ministers to aid Governor  
**Article 164** = Appointment of CM and Ministers  
**Article 213** = Ordinance power

## 🧠 Article Number Memory Tricks

### By Pattern:

**70s = Executive:**
- 72 = Pardon (President)
- 73 = Union Executive
- 74 = Aid and advice
- 75 = Ministers
- 78 = PM duties

**100s = Legislature & Money:**
- 105 = Privileges
- 108 = Joint sitting
- 110 = Money Bills

**240s = Powers Distribution:**
- 245 = Territorial
- 246 = Lists
- 248 = Residuary
- 249 = Rajya Sabha
- 250 = Emergency
- 252 = Consent
- 253 = Treaties
- 254 = Repugnancy

**320s = Tribunals:**
- 323A = Administrative
- 323B = Others
- 324 = Election Commission

**350s = Emergency:**
- 352 = National
- 356 = State
- 358 = Article 19 suspended
- 359 = Others suspended
- 360 = Financial

## 📝 How to Cite in Exam

### Right Way ✅:
"Under Article 32, Supreme Court can issue writs..."  
"Article 246 distributes legislative powers through three Lists..."  
"As per Article 74, President shall act on advice..."  

### Wrong Way ❌:
"There is an Article about writs..."  
"Some Article says about Lists..."  
"President acts on advice (forgot Article)"
