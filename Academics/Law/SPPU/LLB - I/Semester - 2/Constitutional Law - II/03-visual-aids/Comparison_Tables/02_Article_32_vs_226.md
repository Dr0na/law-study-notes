# 📊 Comparison Table: Article 32 vs Article 226

## 🎯 Supreme Court vs High Court Writ Jurisdiction

**Context:** Both Supreme Court (Article 32) and High Courts (Article 226) can issue writs, but there are important differences in scope and nature.

---

## 📋 Complete Comparison (COUNT = 12 differences)

| # | Feature | Article 32 (SC) 🏛️ | Article 226 (HC) 🏢 |
|---|---------|---------------------|---------------------|
| 1️⃣ | **Scope** | Only for **Fundamental Rights** | For FRs + **Other legal rights** |
| 2️⃣ | **Article** | Article 32 | Article 226 |
| 3️⃣ | **Nature** | Fundamental Right itself | Constitutional power (not FR) |
| 4️⃣ | **Duty** | SC **must entertain** (mandatory) | HC **may entertain** (discretionary) |
| 5️⃣ | **Can Refuse?** | ❌ No (must hear) | ✅ Yes (discretion) |
| 6️⃣ | **Territory** | All India | Only that State/UT |
| 7️⃣ | **Against Whom** | Any authority in India | Authorities in that State |
| 8️⃣ | **Writs** | Same 5 writs (H-M-P-C-Q) | Same 5 writs |
| 9️⃣ | **Suspension** | Can be suspended (Article 359) | Cannot be suspended |
| 🔟 | **Appeal** | Review under Article 137 | Appeal to SC under Article 136 (SLP) |
| 1️⃣1️⃣ | **Accessibility** | Less (apex court) | More (every State) |
| 1️⃣2️⃣ | **Dr. Ambedkar** | "Heart and Soul" of Constitution | Not described as such |

---

## 🔹 Which is Wider?

### Article 226 is WIDER than Article 32

**Why High Court's Power is Wider:**

**Article 32 (SC):**
- ✅ For Fundamental Rights only
- ❌ Not for other legal rights
- ❌ Not for contractual rights
- ❌ Not for statutory rights

**Article 226 (HC):**
- ✅ For Fundamental Rights
- ✅ For other legal rights (property, contract)
- ✅ For statutory rights
- ✅ For any purpose under law

**Example:**
- If contractual right violated (not FR) → Can go to HC under 226, cannot go to SC under 32
- If Fundamental Right violated → Can go to either HC (226) or SC (32)

---

## 🔹 Which is More Accessible?

### High Court (Article 226) More Accessible

**Reasons:**

#### Geographic Accessibility 🗺️
- **SC**: Only in Delhi (one court for entire India)
- **HC**: In every State (closer to people)

#### Cost Factor 💰
- **SC**: Higher litigation costs
- **HC**: Relatively lower costs

#### Choice Factor 🎯
- For FR violations: Can choose HC or SC
- HC often preferred (accessibility + wider scope)

## 📌 Important Cases

### On Article 32:
**Romesh Thappar v. State of Madras (1950)**
- First case enforcing Article 32
- FR protection through writ

### On Article 226:
**T.C. Basappa v. T. Nagappa (1954)**
- Explained scope of Article 226
- Wider than Article 32

### On Both:
**Minerva Mills (1980)**
- Both 32 and 226 are basic structure
- Judicial review cannot be taken away

## 🧠 Memory Trick

**Article 32** = **"3-2"** = **"Three to Two"** = Narrower (Only FRs)  
**Article 226** = **"2-2-6"** = **"Too Too Big"** = Wider (FRs + More)  

**Remember:**
- **32** is smaller number → Smaller scope (Only FRs)
- **226** is bigger number → Bigger scope (FRs + Others)

**"Heart and Soul"** = **32** (Ambedkar's phrase)  
**"Wider Power"** = **226**
