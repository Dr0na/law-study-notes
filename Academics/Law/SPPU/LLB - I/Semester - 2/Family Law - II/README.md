# Family Law - II (LCC 0603)

**Course**: Third Year B.A., LL.B. / B.B.A., LL.B. (Semester VI) | First Year LL.B. (Semester II)  
**Credits**: 4 Credits  
**University**: Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU)

---

## 📚 Course Overview

This course focuses on personal law as it affects property relations, covering Hindu Joint Family system, succession laws (intestate and testamentary) applicable to all denominations, and provisions relating to Wakf and gifts in Muslim law.

**Key Legislations:**
- Hindu Succession Act, 1956
- Indian Succession Act, 1925
- Wakf Act
- Muslim Personal Law (Shariat) Application Act, 1937

- Origin and development
- Essentials and kinds
- Creation formalities

## 🆘 Quick Reference Tips

### **For Succession Problems:**
1. Identify the deceased and religion
2. Determine if intestate or testamentary
3. Apply relevant law (Hindu/Muslim/ISA)
4. List all heirs
5. Calculate shares
6. Check disqualifications
7. Conclude with final distribution

### **For Comparative Questions:**
- Make a table: Hindu | Muslim | Christian | Parsi
- Point-by-point comparison
- Highlight key differences
- Mention relevant provisions

### **For Conceptual Questions:**
- Define the concept
- Explain essential elements
- Cite relevant provisions/cases
- Give examples
