### Q10. Distinguish between Sale and Agreement to Sell.

**📖 Statutory Provision:** Section 4 of the Sale of Goods Act, 1930.

| 📊 Basis | 🛒 Sale | ⏳ Agreement to Sell |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Transfer of Property** | Ownership passes **immediately**. | Ownership passes at a **future time** or upon condition. |
| **Nature** | ✅ Executed Contract. | ⏳ Executory Contract. |
| **Risk** | Falls on the **Buyer** (risk follows ownership). | Falls on the **Seller**. |
| **Buyer's Breach** | Seller can sue for the **price**. | Seller can only sue for **damages**. |
| **Insolvency of Buyer** | Seller must deliver goods to receiver, can only claim a dividend. | Seller can refuse delivery until price is paid. |

**🎯 Conclusion:** The key difference is the **timing of transfer of ownership**. In a sale, property passes immediately, and all risk and remedies flow from that fact. In an agreement to sell, the seller retains control until the condition is fulfilled.
