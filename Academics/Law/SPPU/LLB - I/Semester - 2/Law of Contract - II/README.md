**Course**: Third Year B.A., LL.B. / B.B.A., LL.B. (Semester VI) | First Year LL.B. (Semester II)  
**Credits**: 4 Credits  
**University**: Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU)

---

## 📚 Course Overview

This course covers special provisions of law that apply to special contracts - indemnity, guarantee, bailment, pledge, agency, sale of goods, and partnership. These transactions play a vital role in commerce and trade.

**Key Legislations:**
- Indian Contract Act, 1872 (Sections 124-238)
- Sale of Goods Act, 1930
- Indian Partnership Act, 1932
- Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008

## 📖 Module Breakdown

### Module 01: Contracts of Indemnity (Secs 124-125)
- Principle and definition
- Liability of indemnifier
- Practical examples

### Module 02: Contracts of Guarantee (Secs 126-147)
- Formation and essential features
- Distinction from indemnity
- Surety's rights and liabilities
- Co-sureties and bank guarantees

### Module 03: Contracts of Bailment (Secs 71, 148-171, 180-181)
- Definition and kinds
- Rights and duties of bailor/bailee
- Finder of goods as bailee

### Module 04: Contracts of Pledge (Secs 172-179)
- Definition and creation
- Distinction: Pledge, Bailment, Hypothecation
- Rights of pawnor and pawnee

### Module 05: Contracts of Agency (Secs 182-238)
- Definition and creation
- Agent's authority and delegation
- Rights, duties, and liabilities
- Termination of agency

### Module 06: Sale of Goods (SOGA 1930)
- Essential features
- Conditions and warranties
- Transfer of title and property
- Unpaid seller's rights
- Caveat emptor rule

### Module 07: Partnership (IPA 1932, LLP Act 2008)
- Definition and formation
- Partners' relations
- Dissolution and registration
- Limited Liability Partnership

## 💡 Important Study Points

### **Key Distinctions to Master:**
1. Indemnity vs Guarantee
2. Bailment vs Pledge vs Hypothecation
3. Sale vs Agreement to Sell
4. Condition vs Warranty
5. Agent vs Servant vs Independent Contractor
6. Partnership vs LLP
7. Dissolution of Partnership vs Dissolution of Firm

### **Essential Sections to Remember:**
- Secs 124-125 (Indemnity)
- Secs 126-147 (Guarantee)
- Secs 148-181 (Bailment & Pledge)
- Secs 182-238 (Agency)
- Key sections from SOGA and IPA

### **High-Weightage Topics:**
- Agency (creation, authority, liability)
- Sale of Goods (transfer of property, unpaid seller)
- Partnership (relations, dissolution)
- Guarantee (surety's rights)

## 🆘 Quick Reference Tips

1. **For Problem Questions**: Identify the contract type → Apply relevant provisions → Cite cases → Conclude
2. **For Definition Questions**: Define → Essential elements → Legal provisions → Example/case
3. **For Distinction Questions**: Define both → Point-by-point differences → Legal basis
4. **For Section-based Questions**: Section number → Provision → Explanation → Case law → Application
