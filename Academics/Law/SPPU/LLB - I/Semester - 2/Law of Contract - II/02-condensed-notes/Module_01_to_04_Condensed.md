# Condensed Notes: Modules 1 to 4
*(For Quick Revision Before Exams)*

## Module 1: Contracts of Indemnity (Sec 124-125)
- **Definition:** Promise to save the other from loss caused by the promisor or any other person.
- **Parties (2):** Indemnifier (promisor), Indemnity Holder (promisee).
- **Essentials:** Valid contract, loss by human agency (in India).
- **Rights of Indemnity Holder (Sec 125):** Can recover:
  1. All damages
  2. All costs of suit
  3. All sums paid under compromise
- **Commencement of Liability:** Arises when the liability of the indemnity holder becomes absolute and certain (need not actually pay first - *Gajanan Moreshwar* case).

## Module 2: Contracts of Guarantee (Sec 126-147)
- **Definition:** Contract to perform the promise or discharge liability of a third person in case of default.
- **Parties (3):** Surety (Guarantor), Principal Debtor (PD), Creditor.
- **Essentials:** Tripartite agreement, existing debt, consideration (benefit to PD is enough).
- **Nature of Liability:** Secondary and conditional (co-extensive with PD).
- **Continuing Guarantee (Sec 129):** Extends to a series of transactions. Revocable for future transactions by notice (Sec 130) or death of surety (Sec 131).
- **Discharge of Surety:** By revocation, variance in terms, release of PD, compounding, loss of security.
- **Surety's Rights:**
  - *Against PD:* Subrogation (Sec 140), Indemnity (Sec 145).
  - *Against Creditor:* Right to securities (Sec 141), Set-off.
- **Co-Sureties:** Share burden equally (Sec 146) unless contracted otherwise.

## Module 3: Contracts of Bailment (Sec 148-171)
- **Definition:** Delivery of goods by one person to another for some purpose, upon a contract to return/dispose when purpose is accomplished.
- **Parties (2):** Bailor (delivers), Bailee (receives).
- **Essentials:** Delivery of possession (actual/constructive), specific purpose, return of *specific* goods. Movable goods only.
- **Duties of Bailee:** Take reasonable care (Sec 151), no unauthorized use, no mixing, return goods, return accretions.
- **Rights of Bailee:** Recover expenses, Right of Lien (Particular Sec 170, General Sec 171).
- **Termination:** Expiry of time, purpose achieved, unauthorized use, death (if gratuitous), destruction of goods.
- **Finder of Goods (Sec 71):** Treated as bailee. Has right of lien for expenses, right to sell under specific conditions (perishable, charges > 2/3rd value).

## Module 4: Contracts of Pledge (Sec 172-179)
- **Definition:** Bailment of goods as security for payment of a debt or performance of a promise.
- **Parties (2):** Pawnor (pledgor/bailor), Pawnee (pledgee/bailee).
- **Essentials:** Delivery, purpose is *security*, returnable on payment.
- **Rights of Pawnee:** Right of retainer (Sec 173), claim extraordinary expenses.
- **Remedies on Default (Sec 176):**
  1. Sue pawnor and retain goods as collateral.
  2. Sell the goods after *reasonable notice*.
- **Right to Redeem:** Pawnor can redeem anytime *before* actual sale, paying additional default costs.
- **Exceptions to Nemo Dat (Valid pledge by non-owner):** Mercantile agent (Sec 178), Person with voidable title (Sec 178A), Person with limited interest (Sec 179).
