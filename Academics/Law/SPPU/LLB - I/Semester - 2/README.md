## 📖 Subject-wise Overview

### 1. **Constitutional Law – II (LCC 0601)**

**Focus**: Federal structure, Constitutional institutions, Emergency provisions

**Key Topics**:
- Nature of Indian Federalism
- Distribution of Powers (Legislative, Executive, Financial)
- Union and State Legislature
- Supreme Court, High Courts, Tribunals
- Constitutional Institutions (Election Commission, CAG, etc.)
- Emergency Provisions

**Prerequisites**: Constitutional Law - I (Semester I)

**Folder**: `Constitutional Law - II/`

---

### 2. **Law of Contract – II (LCC 0602)**

**Focus**: Special contracts - Indemnity, Guarantee, Bailment, Pledge, Agency, Sale of Goods, Partnership

**Key Topics**:
- Contracts of Indemnity and Guarantee
- Bailment and Pledge
- Agency
- Sale of Goods Act, 1930
- Partnership Act, 1932
- Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008

**Prerequisites**: Law of Contract - I (Semester I)

**Folder**: `Law of Contract - II/`

---

### 3. **Family Law – II (LCC 0603)**

**Focus**: Property aspects of personal laws - Succession, Gifts, Wakf

**Key Topics**:
- Hindu Joint Family System
- Intestate Succession (Hindu, Muslim, Parsi, Christian)
- Testamentary Succession (Wills)
- Pre-emption Rights
- Gifts under Muslim Law (Hiba)
- Wakf Administration

**Prerequisites**: Family Law - I (Semester I)

**Folder**: `Family Law - II/`

---

### 4. **Jurisprudence (LCC 0604)**

**Focus**: Foundation of legal theory, schools of law, basic legal concepts

**Key Topics**:
- Introduction to Jurisprudence
- Sources of Law
- Natural Law, Positivism, Sociological, Realist, Historical Schools
- Critical Legal Studies
- Person, Rights, Duties
- Ownership, Possession, Title
- Obligations and Liability

**Prerequisites**: None (Foundation course)

**Folder**: `Jurisprudence/`

---

### 5. **Media Laws (LGE 0609)** [Generic Elective]

**Focus**: Legal framework governing media, press freedom, media regulations

**Key Topics**:
- Media and Democracy
- History and Theories of Press (6 theories)
- Constitutional Framework (Article 19)
- Legal Dimensions (Defamation, Contempt, Sedition)
- Regulatory Framework (Press Council, TRAI, Cable TV Act)
- Contemporary Issues (Internet freedom, paid news, OTT regulation)

**Prerequisites**: None (Foundation knowledge of Constitutional Law helpful)

**Folder**: `Media Laws/`

---

### 6. **Penology and Victimology (LDSE 0607)** [Discipline Specific Elective]

**Focus**: Punishment theories, penal institutions, prison reforms, victim rights and assistance

**Key Topics**:
- Penology and Crime Control Mechanisms
- Theories of Punishment (Deterrent, Retributive, Preventive, Reformative)
- Police System in India
- Prison System and Reforms
- Open Prisons and Parole System
- Victimology - Theories of Victimization
- Victims' Rights and Assistance Programs
- International and National Legal Framework

**Prerequisites**: Knowledge of Law of Crimes helpful

**Folder**: `Penology and Victimology/`

## 💡 Study Tips for Semester II

### **1. Build on Semester I Knowledge**
- Review Semester I subjects before starting Part II
- Understand progression from basics to advanced
- Link concepts across semesters

### **2. Create Comparison Charts**
- Compare Hindu, Muslim, Christian, Parsi laws (Family Law)
- Compare different contracts (Contract Law)
- Compare schools of jurisprudence

### **3. Master Legal Provisions**
- Memorize important section numbers
- Understand not just 'what' but 'why'
- Practice citing provisions in answers

### **4. Case Law is King**
- Read landmark judgments
- Understand ratio decidendi
- Practice case citation format

### **5. Practice Answer Writing**
- Structure: Introduction → Body → Conclusion
- Use headings and sub-headings
- Cite provisions and cases
- Write to the point

### **6. Time Management**
- Allocate time based on credits (all equal)
- Balance theory (Jurisprudence) and application subjects
- Practice writing within time limits

## 🎓 Examination Tips

### **General Guidelines:**
1. Read questions carefully
2. Plan your answers before writing
3. Use proper legal terminology
4. Cite provisions and cases wherever relevant
5. Write legibly with clear handwriting
6. Manage time - don't spend too long on any one question

### **Subject-specific Tips:**

**Constitutional Law II:**
- Draw diagrams for federal structure
- Use flowcharts for legislative procedures
- Cite Articles and landmark cases

**Contract Law II:**
- Always cite section numbers
- Use practical examples
- For problem questions: Facts → Issue → Law → Application → Conclusion

**Family Law II:**
- Draw family trees for succession problems
- Show calculations for shares clearly
- Compare different personal laws

**Jurisprudence:**
- Structure theoretical answers well
- Critical analysis is important
- Link theory with contemporary issues
