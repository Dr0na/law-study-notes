### Q1. Discuss the salient features of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS).

**📖 Statutory Provision: Act No. 46 of 2023, replacing the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973**

**1. Meaning**: The BNSS, 2023 is the primary procedural law governing criminal administration in India. It came into force on **1st July 2024** and consolidates the law relating to arrests, investigations, trials, bail, and execution.

**2. Key Feature — Specific Timelines**:
- Mandatory charge framing within **60 days** of first hearing (Sec. 263).
- Pronouncement of judgment within **45 days** of trial conclusion (Sec. 392).
- Deemed sanction for prosecuting public servants if not decided in **120 days** (Sec. 218).

**3. Key Feature — Technology & Digitalization**:
- Registration of E-FIR allowed, with signatures obtained within 3 days (Sec. 173).
- Mandatory audio-video recording of search and seizure operations (Sec. 105).
- Summons can be served electronically via SMS, email, or messaging apps (Sec. 64).

**4. Key Feature — Forensic Mandate**:
- Mandatory forensic collection for offences punishable with **7 years or more** (Sec. 176).

**5. Key Feature — Victim-Centric Approach**:
- Mandatory update on investigation progress to the victim within **90 days** (Sec. 193).
- Victim's right to appeal against acquittal or inadequate compensation (Sec. 413).

**🎯 Conclusion**: The BNSS modernizes India's criminal justice system by integrating digital evidence, enforcing strict timelines, and strengthening victim rights.
