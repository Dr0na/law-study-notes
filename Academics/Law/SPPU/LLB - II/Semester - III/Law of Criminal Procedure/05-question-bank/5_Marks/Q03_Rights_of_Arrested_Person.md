### Q3. Discuss the key rights of an arrested person under the BNSS.

**📖 Statutory Provisions: Sections 47, 48, 51, and 58 of the BNSS, 2023 & Article 22 of the Constitution of India**

**1. Meaning**: An arrested person has constitutional and statutory rights to protect them from arbitrary state action and custodial abuse, ensuring due process of law.

**2. Right to Know Grounds & Bail (Sec. 47)**:
- Police must immediately inform the arrested person of the grounds/reason for arrest.
- If it is a bailable offence, police must inform them of their right to be released on bail.

**3. Right to Legal Counsel & Notification (Secs. 41D & 48)**:
- Right to meet and consult an advocate of choice during interrogation (Sec. 41D / Art. 22(1)).
- Right to have one relative or friend informed of the arrest and place of detention (Sec. 48).

**4. Right to Medical Checkup & Magistrate Production (Secs. 51 & 58)**:
- Mandatory medical examination by a registered medical practitioner right after arrest (Sec. 51).
- Must be produced before the nearest Magistrate within **24 hours** of arrest, excluding travel time (Sec. 58 / Art. 22(2)).

**🎯 Conclusion**: These statutory safeguards ensure that arrests are made transparently and protect the fundamental right to life and liberty under Article 21.
