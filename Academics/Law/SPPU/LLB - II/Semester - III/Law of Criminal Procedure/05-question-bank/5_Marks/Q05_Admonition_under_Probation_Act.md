### Q5. Explain the concept of 'Release after Admonition' under the Probation of Offenders Act, 1958.

**📖 Statutory Provision: Section 3 of the Probation of Offenders Act, 1958 & Section 401(3) of the BNSS, 2023**

**1. Meaning**: Admonition means a formal warning or scolding given by the court to an offender before releasing them, instead of sentencing them to imprisonment or fine. It is reserved for first-time offenders who commit minor crimes.

**2. Essential Conditions for Grant**:
- The offender must be found guilty of an offence under the IPC/BNS (e.g., theft, dishonest misappropriation, cheating) punishable with imprisonment of **not more than 2 years**, or fine, or both.
- No previous conviction must be proved against the offender.
- The court must take into account the offender's age, character, antecedents, and the circumstances of the offence.

**3. Effect**:
- The court releases the offender after due admonition (warning). 
- It wipes out the stigma of conviction, helping the offender reform without being exposed to hardened criminals in prison.

**🎯 Conclusion**: Release after admonition is a reformative sentencing option designed to give petty, first-time offenders a chance to correct their behavior without prison custody.
