### Q2. Explain the sentencing powers of the Chief Judicial Magistrate (CJM) under the BNSS.

**📖 Statutory Provision: Section 23(1) of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023**

**1. Meaning**: The Chief Judicial Magistrate (CJM) is appointed by the High Court in every district (Sec. 10) and heads the subordinate judicial magistracy in that district. 

**2. Sentencing Powers**:
- Under **Section 23(1)**, a CJM may pass any sentence authorized by law except:
  - Death sentence.
  - Imprisonment for life.
  - Imprisonment for a term exceeding **7 years**.

**3. Administrative Powers**:
- The CJM has administrative control over all other Judicial Magistrates in the district (Sec. 13).
- The CJM distributes business and makes rules consistent with the BNSS for the subordination of magistrates (Sec. 13(2)).

**🎯 Conclusion**: The CJM holds the highest sentencing power among Judicial Magistrates, limited to a maximum of 7 years, and acts as the administrative head of the district's magistracy.
