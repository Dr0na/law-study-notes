# 🎯 Most Probable Questions - Law of Criminal Procedure (LCC 0701)

These questions represent predicted examination trends for the upcoming Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU) LLB examinations, with focus on the transition from the old CrPC, 1973 to the new **Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS)**.

---

## 📅 Priority Preparation Order for Modules

To secure maximum marks in the shortest time, prepare modules in this order of exam weightage:

1. **Module 3 (Information & Investigation)**: Highest weightage (FIR, Arrest, Search, Custody, Confessions).
2. **Module 5 (Charge & Trials)**: High weightage (Sessions Trial, Warrant Trial, Charge Exceptions).
3. **Module 7 (Juvenile Justice)**: High weightage (JJB, CWC, 16-18 preliminary assessment).
4. **Module 4 (Bail & Maintenance)**: High weightage (Anticipatory Bail, Section 144 Maintenance).
5. **Module 1 (Introduction & Courts)**: Medium weightage (Court hierarchy, sentencing, PPs).
6. **Module 6 (Judgment & Appeals)**: Medium weightage (Judgment timelines, appeals, revision).
7. **Module 2 (Jurisdiction)**: Low weightage (Complaint procedure, territorial committal).
