### ⚖️ Q1. Distinguish between 'Inquiry' and 'Investigation'.

**(Refer to Sections 2(1)(k) & 2(1)(l) of the BNSS, 2023)**

| 📊 Basis | 📜 Inquiry [Sec. 2(1)(k)] | 🔍 Investigation [Sec. 2(1)(l)] |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definition** | Every inquiry, other than a trial, conducted under this Sanhita by a Magistrate or Court. | Includes all the proceedings under this Sanhita for the collection of evidence conducted by a police officer or by any person (other than a Magistrate) who is authorized by a Magistrate. |
| **Conducted by** | Conducted exclusively by a **Magistrate** or a **Court**. | Conducted by a **Police Officer** or any other authorized person (but not a Magistrate). |
| **Primary Object** | To determine the truth or falsehood of certain facts or allegations (prima facie check). | To **collect evidence** relating to the commission of the alleged crime. |
| **Commencement** | Commences after a complaint is filed or police report is received by the Magistrate. | Commences immediately after the registration of an FIR or receipt of a magisterial order. |
| **Nature of Proceedings** | Usually a judicial proceeding. Witnesses are examined under oath. | An executive/administrative proceeding. Witnesses are not examined under oath. |
| **Conclusion** | Concludes with an order like committal of the case (Sec. 228) or dismissal of complaint (Sec. 224). | Concludes with the submission of a Final Report or Charge Sheet to the Magistrate (Sec. 193). |
