### ⚖️ Q3. Distinguish between a 'Summons Case' and a 'Warrant Case'.

**(Refer to Sections 2(1)(x) & 2(1)(z) of the BNSS, 2023)**

| 📊 Basis | ✉️ Summons Case [Sec. 2(1)(x)] | 📜 Warrant Case [Sec. 2(1)(z)] |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definition** | A case relating to an offence, and not being a warrant case. | A case relating to an offence punishable with death, life imprisonment, or imprisonment for a term exceeding two years. |
| **Sentence Limit** | Imprisonment up to **2 years** or fine. | Imprisonment **exceeding 2 years**, life, or death. |
| **Trial Procedure** | Simplified trial (Secs. 274-282). No formal charge needs to be framed. | Elaborate trial (Secs. 261-273). Formal charge must be framed. |
| **Withdrawal** | Complainant can withdraw the complaint at any time with Magistrate's permission (Sec. 280). | Cannot be withdrawn easily; requires public prosecutor's withdrawal (Sec. 360). |
| **Conversion** | A summons case can be converted into a warrant case by the Magistrate in interest of justice. | A warrant case cannot be converted into a summons case. |
| **Standard Process** | Process is initiated by issuing a Summons. | Process is initiated by issuing a Warrant or a Summons. |
