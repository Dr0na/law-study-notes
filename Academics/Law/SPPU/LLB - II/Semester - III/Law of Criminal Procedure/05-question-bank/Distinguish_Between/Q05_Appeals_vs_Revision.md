### ⚖️ Q5. Distinguish between 'Appeal' and 'Revision'.

**(Refer to Sections 413-435 (Appeals) & Sections 438-445 (Revision) of the BNSS, 2023)**

| 📊 Basis | 📣 Appeal (Secs. 413-435) | 🔍 Revision (Secs. 438-445) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nature of Right** | A statutory right created by law. It is a continuation of the trial. | An discretionary power of the court. The applicant has no absolute right to be heard. |
| **Scope of Hearing** | Court rehears the entire case on both questions of **fact** and questions of **law**. | Court only checks the **correctness, legality, propriety, or regularity** of the proceedings. |
| **Competent Court** | Vested in higher courts depending on the trial court (e.g., Session Court, HC, or SC). | Vested exclusively in the **High Court** and the **Court of Session** (Sec. 438). |
| **Power to Convert Acquittal**| Appellate court **can** convert an acquittal into a conviction (Sec. 419). | Revisional court **cannot** convert a finding of acquittal into one of conviction (Sec. 438(3)). |
| **Abatement** | An appeal can abate (end) upon the death of the appellant/accused (Sec. 435). | A revision does not abate automatically upon death; court can proceed *suo motu*. |
