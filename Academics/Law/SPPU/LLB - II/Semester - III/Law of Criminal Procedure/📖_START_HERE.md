# 📖 START HERE - Law of Criminal Procedure Study Path

Criminal Procedure governs how the state enforces substantive criminal law. In the SPPU examinations, mastering the procedural steps, court sentencing powers, and statutory section numbers under the **Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS)** is the key to scoring high marks. Follow this guided path to navigate the resources:

---

## 🏁 Phase 1: Groundwork & Learning (Weeks 1-4)
- **Objective**: Build a robust conceptual understanding of all procedural stages.
- **Action**:
  - Read Modules 1 to 7 in `01-complete-notes/`.
  - Focus deeply on **Module 3 (Information & Investigation)** and **Module 5 (Charge & Trials)**, as they carry the highest marks in examinations.
  - Review the **Court Hierarchy and Sentencing Powers** in Module 1.

---

## 📐 Phase 2: Visualizing Workflows (Weeks 5-6)
- **Objective**: Master sequential legal processes (e.g., trials, investigation, bail).
- **Action**:
  - Review **Flowcharts & Diagrams** in `03-visual-aids/Flowcharts/`.
  - Practice sketching the *Sessions Trial Flowchart* and the *Investigation Process Flowchart* by hand.
  - Memorize the comparison tables in `03-visual-aids/Comparison_Tables/` to answer "Distinguish Between" questions quickly.

---

## ⚖️ Phase 3: Case Law Memorization (Weeks 7-8)
- **Objective**: Anchor your answers with authoritative judgments.
- **Action**:
  - Scan the **Landmark Cases Summary** table under `04-case-laws/`.
  - Read the detailed facts and holdings of the 14 core cases in the **Module-wise Case Digest** under `04-case-laws/Module_Wise_Cases/`.
  - Ensure you can cite at least 2 cases for 15-mark questions (e.g., *Lalita Kumari* for FIR, *D.K. Basu* for Arrest, *Gurbaksh Singh Sibbia* for Bail).

---

## 📝 Phase 4: Exam Drills & Strategy (Weeks 9-11)
- **Objective**: Master answer writing and time management.
- **Action**:
  - Open the **Answer Writing Template** under `08-exam-strategy/` to learn the required structure for 5, 10, and 15-mark answers.
  - Review the **Most Probable Questions** in `05-question-bank/`.
  - Solve at least 2 questions under each folder (`5_Marks`, `10_Marks`, `15_Marks`, `Distinguish_Between`) under exam conditions.

---

## ⚡ Phase 5: Last Minute Blitz (24 Hours Before Exam)
- **Objective**: Maximize recall speed and reinforce section numbers.
- **Action**:
  - Read the **Condensed Revision Notes** (`Condensed_Revision_M1_M3.md` and `Condensed_Revision_M4_M7.md`).
  - Scan the **Doctrines and Maxims** list under `07-quick-revision/`.
  - Memorize the consolidated mnemonics on the **All Mnemonics Cheat Sheet** under `07-quick-revision/`.
  - Take the **Self Assessment Test** under `09-practice-worksheets/` as a final mock.
