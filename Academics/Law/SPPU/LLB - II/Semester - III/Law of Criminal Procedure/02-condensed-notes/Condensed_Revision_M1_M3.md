## 🏛️ Module 1: Introduction to BNSS, 2023

### A. Inquisitorial vs Adversarial Systems
- **Adversarial (Indian)**: Judge is neutral umpire. Burden of proof on Prosecution. Accused presumed innocent. *Nandini Satpathy* (Art. 20(3) protection).
- **Inquisitorial (Continental)**: Judge is active investigator. Shared burden of proof. Active truth discovery.

### B. Hierarchy & Sentencing Powers (Secs. 6, 22, 23)
- **High Court (Sec. 22(1))**: Any sentence authorized by law.
- **Sessions / Addl. Sessions Judge (Sec. 22(2))**: Any sentence (death penalty requires HC confirmation).
- **Chief Judicial Magistrate (Sec. 23(1))**: Up to **7 years** imprisonment.
- **Judicial Magistrate First Class (Sec. 23(2))**: Up to **3 years** / Fine up to **₹50,000** (or both, or Community Service).
- **Judicial Magistrate Second Class (Sec. 23(3))**: Up to **1 year** / Fine up to **₹10,000** (or both, or Community Service).

### C. Public Prosecution (Secs. 18-20)
- **Public Prosecutor / Addl. PP (Sec. 18)**: Vetted panel, minimum **7 years** practice.
- **Special Public Prosecutor (Sec. 18(8))**: Appointed for specific cases, minimum **10 years** practice.
- **Assistant Public Prosecutor (Sec. 19)**: Conducts prosecution in Magistrates' courts. Cannot be a police officer who investigated the case.
- **Directorate of Prosecution (Sec. 20)**: Headed by Director of Prosecution (under Home Dept).

### D. Key Definitions (Section 2)
- **Bailable Offence [Sec. 2(1)(c)]**: Bail is a matter of right. Shown in First Schedule.
- **Cognizable Offence [Sec. 2(1)(g)]**: Police can arrest without warrant. FIR mandatory.
- **Non-Cognizable Offence [Sec. 2(1)(o)]**: Police cannot arrest without warrant. Requires Magistrate's order.
- **Complaint [Sec. 2(1)(h)]**: Allegation made to a Magistrate to take action; excludes police report.
- **Inquiry [Sec. 2(1)(k)]**: Conducted by Magistrate/Court; excludes trial.
- **Investigation [Sec. 2(1)(l)]**: Conducted by police/authorized person for collection of evidence.
- **Summons Case [Sec. 2(1)(x)]**: Offence not being a warrant case (punishment ≤ 2 years).
- **Warrant Case [Sec. 2(1)(z)]**: Offence punishable with death, life, or imprisonment exceeding **2 years**.
- **Victim [Sec. 2(1)(y)]**: Includes guardian and legal heir (expanded in BNSS).

## ⚖️ Module 2: Jurisdiction of Criminal Courts (Inquiry & Trials)

### A. General Jurisdiction (Secs. 197-209)
- **Rule of Locality (Sec. 197)**: Every offence ordinarily inquired into and tried by the court within whose local jurisdiction it was committed.
- **Uncertain Local Area (Sec. 198)**: Try where offence was begun, continued, or completed.
- **Offence on Journey (Sec. 203)**: Try by court through whose local jurisdiction the person/thing passed.
- **Offences Committed Abroad (Sec. 208)**: Needs prior sanction of the Central Government.

### B. Complaint to Magistrates (Secs. 223-226)
1. **Examination of Complainant (Sec. 223)**: Magistrate examines complainant and witnesses under oath. Written record signed by them.
2. **Dismissal of Complaint (Sec. 224)**: Dismissed if no sufficient grounds for proceeding (reasons must be recorded).
3. **Issue of Process (Sec. 225)**: Summons (for summons cases) or warrant (for warrant cases) issued to secure accused's appearance.

### C. Inquiry Proceedings (Secs. 227-232)
- **Committal of Case (Sec. 228)**: Magistrate commits case to Sessions Court if offence is triable exclusively by Sessions.
- **Postponement of Issue (Sec. 223(2))**: Magistrate may postpone process and conduct inquiry/direct police investigation to check truth of complaint.

## 🔍 Module 3: Information to Police and Investigation

### A. First Information Report (FIR) — Section 173
- **E-FIR (Sec. 173(1))**: Can be registered electronically. Signatures must be taken within **3 days**.
- **Preliminary Inquiry (Sec. 173(3) - New)**: For offences punishable with 3-7 years, police can conduct a preliminary inquiry within **14 days** to check if a prima facie case exists before registering FIR.
- **Evidentiary Value**: FIR is not substantive evidence. Used for corroboration (Sec. 145 Evidence Act/BSA) or contradiction (Sec. 157 BSA).
- **Landmark Case**: *Lalita Kumari v. Govt. of UP* (2014) — Registration of FIR is mandatory if info discloses a cognizable offence.

### B. Investigation & Interrogation (Secs. 179-193)
- **Witness Attendance (Sec. 179)**: Police can require attendance of witnesses (except males < 15 or > 60, women, or disabled, who must be examined at their residence).
- **Recording Statements (Sec. 180)**: Statements to police need not be signed. Cannot be used for any purpose except contradicting prosecution witnesses.
- **Recording of Confessions (Sec. 183)**: Recorded by Metropolitan/Judicial Magistrate. Warning must be given that they are not bound to confess. Audio-video recording preferred.
- **Special Remand (Sec. 187)**: Maximum custody up to **15 days** (can be spread over first 40/60 days under BNSS).
- **Police Report / Charge Sheet (Sec. 193)**: Completed investigation report submitted to Magistrate. Must include medical reports, forensic details, and audio-video logs.

### C. Arrest of Persons (Secs. 35-58)
- **Arrest Without Warrant (Sec. 35)**: Permissible for cognizable offences, habitual offenders, proclaimed offenders, or obstructing police.
- **Arrest Procedure (Sec. 43)**: Handcuffs allowed under BNSS for specific heinous crimes (terrorism, organized crime, rape, murder).
- **Rights of Arrested Persons (Secs. 47-58, Art. 22)**:
  - Right to know grounds of arrest (Sec. 47).
  - Right to consult advocate of choice (Sec. 41D / Art. 22(1)).
  - Right to inform a friend/relative (Sec. 48).
  - Right to medical examination (Sec. 51).
  - Right to be produced before magistrate within **24 hours** (Sec. 58 / Art. 22(2)).
- **Landmark Case**: *D.K. Basu v. State of West Bengal* (1997) — Vetted guidelines to prevent custodial violence.

### D. Search and Seizure (Secs. 96-110, 185-186)
- **Search Warrant (Sec. 96)**: Issued by court when person unlikely to produce documents, or for general search.
- **Search of Closed Place (Sec. 103)**: Occupant must be allowed to attend search. List of seized items prepared and signed by witnesses.
- **Mandatory Video Recording (Sec. 105 - New)**: Search and seizure process must be recorded via audio-video electronic means.
