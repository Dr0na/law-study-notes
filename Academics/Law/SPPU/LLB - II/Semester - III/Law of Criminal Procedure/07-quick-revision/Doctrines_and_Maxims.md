# ⚖️ Doctrines and Maxims - Law of Criminal Procedure (LCC 0701)

A quick reference sheet of the essential legal doctrines and Latin maxims applicable to the criminal procedure syllabus.

---

## 🏛️ Legal Doctrines

### 1. Doctrine of Autrefois Acquit and Autrefois Convict
- **Statutory Provision**: Section 337 of the BNSS, 2023 & Article 20(2) of the Constitution of India.
- **Meaning**: A person who has once been tried by a court of competent jurisdiction for an offence and acquitted or convicted of such offence shall not be liable to be tried again for the same offence, or on the same facts for any other offence.
- **Application**: Restricts the State from repeatedly prosecuting a citizen for the same criminal act. Commonly referred to as the rule against **Double Jeopardy**.

### 2. Doctrine of Fair Trial
- **Statutory Provision**: Preamble of the BNSS, 2023 & Articles 14, 21, and 22 of the Constitution.
- **Meaning**: The principle that every accused person has the right to a public, impartial, and speedy trial conducted by an independent court, with full opportunity to defend themselves.
- **Application**: Enforces procedures like: providing copies of the charge sheet and evidence to the accused, conducting cross-examinations, holding trials in open court, and providing free legal aid.

### 3. Principle of Subsidiarity (Adoption)
- **Statutory Provision**: Section 59 of the Juvenile Justice Act, 2015.
- **Meaning**: The principle that inter-country adoption should only be considered as a last resort when domestic adoption options are exhausted.
- **Application**: Enforces a mandatory **60-day window** for finding a domestic adoptive family before a child is cleared for inter-country adoption.

## 📜 Latin Maxims

| Latin Maxim | 📖 Literal English Meaning | ⚖️ Procedural Law Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ***Doli Incapax*** | Incapable of committing an offence. | **Absolute Immunity** for children under 7 years of age (Section 20 of the BNS, 2023). |
| ***Doli Capax*** | Capable of committing an offence. | **Qualified Immunity** for children between 7 and 12 years of age (Section 21 of the BNS, 2023). The prosecution must prove maturity. |
| ***Audi Alteram Partem*** | Hear the other side; no person should be condemned unheard. | Enforces the requirement to hear the accused before framing charges, examining witnesses, or passing a sentence (fair trial). |
| ***Interest Reipublicae Ut Sit Finis Litium*** | It is in the interest of the State that there should be an end to litigation. | The rationale behind the **Law of Limitation** for taking cognizance of minor offences (Sections 513-519 of the BNSS). |
| ***Nemo Debet Bis Vexari Pro Una Et Eadem Causa*** | No man should be twice vexed for the same cause. | The foundation of the rule against double jeopardy and Section 337 of the BNSS (Autrefois Acquit/Convict). |
