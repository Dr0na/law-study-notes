# 🧠 All Mnemonics Cheat Sheet - Criminal Procedure (LCC 0701)

This cheat sheet consolidates all memory aids created across the study pack for rapid, last-minute recall before entering the examination hall.

---

## 1. Court Hierarchy and Sentencing Powers
Helps remember the hierarchy and sentencing limits of criminal courts in India under Sections 6, 22, and 23.

> 🧠 **Mnemonic — HSC-First-Second:** *"High Sessions Chief First Second"*
> - **H** = **H**igh Court (Sentence: Any sentence allowed by law)
> - **S** = **S**essions Court (Sentence: Any sentence; death needs HC confirmation)
> - **C** = **C**JM (Sentence: Up to 7 years imprisonment)
> - **First** = **F**irst Class Magistrate (Sentence: Up to 3 years, ₹50,000 fine, or community service)
> - **Second** = **S**econd Class Magistrate (Sentence: Up to 1 year, ₹10,000 fine, or community service)

---

## 2. Rights of an Arrested Person
Helps remember the key statutory and constitutional rights of a person immediately upon their arrest.

> 🧠 **Mnemonic — G-R-A-M-S:** *"Go Register At Medical Station"*
> - **G** = **G**rounds of arrest must be informed (Sec. 47)
> - **R** = **R**elative/friend must be notified of the arrest (Sec. 48)
> - **A** = **A**dvocate consultation right during interrogation (Sec. 41D)
> - **M** = **M**edical checkup immediately after arrest (Sec. 51)
> - **S** = **S**peedy production before a Magistrate within 24 hours (Sec. 58 / Art. 22(2))

---

## 3. Exceptions to Separate Trials for Charges
Helps remember the circumstances under which multiple charges against an accused can be tried together in a single trial.

> 🧠 **Mnemonic — O-S-A:** *"Offences Share Accounts"*
> - **O** = **O**ffences of the same kind committed within 12 months (Sec. 242)
> - **S** = Offences committed in the course of the **S**ame transaction (Sec. 243)
> - **A** = **A**lternative charges where it is doubtful which offence was committed (Sec. 266 / Sec. 246)

---

## 4. Types of Child Care Institutions (CCIs)
Helps remember the five categories of institutional homes established under the Juvenile Justice Act, 2015.

> 🧠 **Mnemonic — O-S-C-F-P:** *"Officially Safe Children Find Protection"*
> - **O** = **O**bservation Homes (temporary reception during inquiry - Sec. 47)
> - **S** = **S**pecial Homes (reformatory custody post-conviction - Sec. 48)
> - **C** = **C**hildren's Homes (long-term care for neglected children - Sec. 50)
> - **F** = **F**it Facilities (recognized private/NGO homes - Sec. 51)
> - **P** = **P**lace of Safety (security containment for 16-18 heinous CCLs - Sec. 49)

---

## 5. Grounds for Denying Maintenance to a Wife
Helps remember the three statutory conditions under Section 144(4) where a wife cannot claim monthly maintenance.

> 🧠 **Mnemonic — A-R-S:** *"Adultery, Refusal, Separation"*
> - **A** = Wife is living in **A**dultery.
> - **R** = Wife **R**efuses to live with her husband without a sufficient reason.
> - **S** = Husband and wife are living **S**eparately by mutual consent.
