# ✍️ SPPU LLB Answer Writing Template - Procedural Law

This template defines the mandatory structure and presentation standards required to maximize marks in the Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU) examinations for **Law of Criminal Procedure (LCC 0701)**.

---

## 📌 General Exam Presentation Guidelines
1. **Pointwise Writing**: Never write long, unstructured essay-style paragraphs. Examiners prefer neat, numbered points for readability.
2. **Explicit Counts**: If a concept has a specific number of elements or exceptions (e.g., "5 essentials of a valid search"), explicitly state the count in the heading (e.g., **"Essential Requisites of Search (5)"**).
3. **Statutory Reference**: Always cite the relevant Act and Section number at the start of your answer. Underline or bold section numbers (e.g., **Section 173 of the BNSS, 2023**).
4. **Underline Key Terms**: Underline or bold core legal keywords (e.g., *prima facie*, *doli incapax*, *double jeopardy*) to catch the examiner's eye.
5. **Draw Flowcharts**: Where applicable, draw quick diagrams (like court hierarchy or trial steps) to illustrate procedures.

## 📝 1. Template for 5 Marks Questions (Short Notes)
*Target Length: 1.5 to 2 Pages (approx. 200–300 words)*

```markdown
### Q. Write a short note on: [Topic Name]

**📖 Statutory Provision: Section [X] of the [Act Name]**

**1. Meaning / Definition**: 
- Provide a clear, 2-3 line statutory or academic definition of the term.

**2. Core Elements / Key Features**:
- Detail 3-4 key points explaining the concept. Use sub-bullets if necessary.

**3. Landmark Case Law / Example**:
- Cite at least 1 landmark case law or practical illustration to support the definition.
- *Format*: *Case Name* (Year) — Held: [1 sentence ratio].

**🎯 Conclusion**: 
- Provide a 1-sentence summary of the legal position.
```

## 📝 2. Template for 10 Marks Questions (Descriptive)
*Target Length: 3 to 4 Pages (approx. 500–700 words)*

```markdown
## ⚖️ Q. [Question Text]

### 1. Introduction
- Introduce the topic by explaining its background, rationale, and constitutional/social significance.
- State the relevant Act and Chapter numbers.

### 2. Statutory Framework (Section [X])
- Cite the exact section number and quote or paraphrase the statutory definition.
- Differentiate between key statutory terms if necessary.

### 3. Essential Ingredients / Characteristics (Count: N)
- List the essential ingredients required to constitute the legal state or procedure.
- Write pointwise explanations for each ingredient:
  - **Ingredient 1**: Explanation + Example.
  - **Ingredient 2**: Explanation + Example.

### 4. Case Laws (Minimum 3 Cases)
Detailed analysis of at least 3 cases:
- ***Case 1***: Facts, Issue, Held.
- ***Case 2***: Facts, Issue, Held.
- ***Case 3***: Facts, Issue, Held.

### 5. Conclusion
- Conclude by summarizing the current legal position and its impact on the administration of justice.
```

## 📝 3. Template for 15 Marks Questions (Long Answers/Essays)
*Target Length: 5 to 6 Pages (approx. 800–1200 words)*

```markdown
## 🏛️ Q. [Question Text]

### 1. Introduction
- Detailed introduction of the concept, historical background, and constitutional context (e.g., Articles 20, 21, 22).

### 2. Definition & Statutory Provision
- Precise citations of all relevant sections and chapters under the BNSS/BNS.
- Breakdown of statutory definitions.

### 3. Step-by-Step Procedure / Detailed Analysis
- Detail the legal process or classification system under clear sub-headings.
- Use flowcharts to map out timelines or sequential steps.
- Provide explicit counts in headings.

### 4. Exceptions / Safeguards (Count: N)
- List the exceptions to the rule or procedural safeguards available to protect citizens.
- Detail the consequences of procedural failure (e.g., effect of an illegal search).

### 5. Landmark Case Laws (Minimum 5 Cases)
Detailed case briefs:
- ***Case 1***: Facts, Issue, Held, Exam Relevance.
- ***Case 2***: Facts, Issue, Held, Exam Relevance.
- ***Case 3***: Facts, Issue, Held, Exam Relevance.
- ***Case 4***: Facts, Issue, Held, Exam Relevance.
- ***Case 5***: Facts, Issue, Held, Exam Relevance.

### 6. Comparative / Critical Analysis
- Provide a comparison table (e.g., Summons vs Warrant trial) or list the criticisms of the provision (e.g., abuse of arrest powers).

### 7. Conclusion
- Provide a summary and the forward-looking reform trends (e.g., Malimath Committee recommendations or BNS integration).
```

## ⚖️ 4. Template for 'Distinguish Between' Questions
*Target Length: 1.5 to 2 Pages*

```markdown

**(Refer to Sections [X] & [Y] of [Act Name])**

| 📊 Basis of Distinction | 🛡️ [Concept A] | 🤝 [Concept B] |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Statutory Definition** | Defined in Section [X] | Defined in Section [Y] |
| **Core Nature** | Primary purpose/nature | Primary purpose/nature |
| **Key Distinction 1** | Pointwise detail | Pointwise detail |
| **Key Distinction 2** | Pointwise detail | Pointwise detail |
| **Key Distinction 3** | Pointwise detail | Pointwise detail |

💡 **Exam Tip**: Make sure to write the "Basis" column. Answers without a "Basis" column lose 1-2 marks.
```
