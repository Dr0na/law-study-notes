# ⚖️ Landmark Cases Summary - Law of Criminal Procedure (LCC 0701)

This executive summary table lists the most critical judgments in criminal procedure. Use these as primary citations in your answers to secure maximum marks.

---

## Master Case Law Summary Table

| Case Name & Year | 📂 Module | ⚖️ Core Legal Issue | 📜 Principle Established / Ratio | 🎯 Exam Relevance |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ***Maneka Gandhi v. Union of India*** (1978) | 1 | Meaning of "procedure established by law" (Art 21). | Criminal procedure must be **just, fair, and reasonable**, not arbitrary or fanciful. | Add to any answer on constitutional rights. |
| ***Hussainara Khatoon v. State of Bihar*** (1979) | 1 | Rights of undertrials and speedy trial. | **Right to speedy trial** is a fundamental right implicit under Article 21. | Essential for "Rights of Accused" or "Fair Trial". |
| ***Lalita Kumari v. Govt. of UP*** (2014) | 3 | Is police registration of FIR mandatory? | Police **must** register FIR under Sec. 154 (Sec. 173 BNSS) if info shows cognizable offence. | Mandatory for "FIR" and "Investigation" questions. |
| ***D.K. Basu v. State of West Bengal*** (1997) | 3 | Custodial violence and police arrest powers. | Laid down **11 guidelines** for arrest (codification of grounds, medical checkups, info to relative). | Critical citation for "Arrest Powers" and "Arrested Rights". |
| ***Gurbaksh Singh Sibbia v. State of Punjab*** (1980) | 4 | Scope of granting Anticipatory Bail. | Anticipatory bail is a device protecting personal liberty. Conditions cannot be overly restrictive. | Primary citation for "Anticipatory Bail" (Sec. 482). |
| ***Shah Bano Begum v. Mohd. Ahmed Khan*** (1985) | 4 | Maintenance rights of Muslim wives. | Section 125 CrPC (Sec. 144 BNSS) is a **secular provision** overriding personal/religious laws. | Mandatory for "Maintenance" questions. |
| ***Subramanian Swamy v. Raju*** (2014) | 7 | Age classification under JJ Act. | Upheld age of 18 as constitutional, but led to the 2015 Act permitting adult trial for 16-18 heinous CCLs. | Mandatory for "Juvenile Justice" and "Preliminary Assessment". |
| ***Sampurna Behrua v. Union of India*** (2018) | 7 | Implementation of JJ Act infrastructure. | Ordered mandatory setup of functional JJBs, CWCs, and registration of all CCIs under social audits. | Essential for "CWC", "JJB", and "Rehabilitation". |
| ***Bachan Singh v. State of Punjab*** (1980) | 6 | Constitutionality of death penalty. | Death penalty is constitutional but must be restricted to the **"rarest of rare cases"**. | Cite in "Judgment" and "Death Penalty Confirmation". |
| ***Hari Ram v. State of Rajasthan*** (2009) | 7 | Date for determining juvenility. | The **date of commission of the offence** determines if a person is a juvenile, not date of trial. | Essential for "Juvenile Jurisdiction". |
| ***Nandini Satpathy v. P.L. Dani*** (1978) | 1, 3 | Right to silence during interrogation. | Article 20(3) protection against self-incrimination extends to police interrogation stage. | Cite in "Interrogation" or "Rights of Arrested". |
| ***State of Haryana v. Bhajan Lal*** (1992) | 5 | Quashing of criminal proceedings. | Set out **7 guidelines** for the High Court to exercise its inherent power to quash an FIR. | Cite in "Inherent Powers of High Court" (Sec. 528). |
