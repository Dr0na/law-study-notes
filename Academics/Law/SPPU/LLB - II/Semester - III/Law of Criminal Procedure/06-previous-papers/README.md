# 📜 Previous Year Papers - Law of Criminal Procedure (LCC 0701)

## ⚠️ Important Note on Syllabus Shift
Following the legislative changes in July 2024, Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU) replaced the **Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 (CrPC)** with the **Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS)** as the primary statutory framework for the procedural law exam.

### 1. Status of Past Papers
- Because the BNSS is a newly enacted statute, there are **no official past university exam papers** using the new BNSS section numbers.
- Frivolous attempts to study from pre-2024 CrPC papers without proper guidance can lead to major errors in the exam, as many section numbers, timelines, and provisions (e.g., E-FIR, preliminary inquiries, arrest handcuffing, and community service) have changed.

### 2. Strategy for Revision
- Do not memorize CrPC section numbers. Focus entirely on the **BNSS Section Mapping** provided in the Complete and Condensed Notes.
- For practice, use the customized **[Self Assessment Test](../09-practice-worksheets/Self_Assessment_Test.md)** included in this study pack. It has been designed specifically to reflect the new SPPU pattern under the BNSS, 2023.
