# Study Path — Interpretation of Statutes

## Recommended sequence

1. Read the [LCC 0703 syllabus](<./00-syllabus-and-references/00 - LCC 0703 Interpretation of Statutes - Syllabus.md>) and note the seven modules.
2. Study the [complete notes](./01-complete-notes/) module by module. Keep the [General Clauses Act, 1897](./00-syllabus-and-references/references/) available when working through Module 1 and Module 7.
3. Consult the [extracted source notes](./00-syllabus-and-references/extracted-markdown/) for the original classroom handouts; use them as supplementary material, not as a substitute for checked law.
4. Review the [comparison tables](./03-visual-aids/Comparison_Tables/Comparison_Tables_Interpretation.md) and [flowcharts](./03-visual-aids/Flowcharts/Flowcharts_Interpretation.md).
5. Memorise the leading cases in the [case digest](./04-case-laws/Landmark_Cases_Summary.md), then practise the [model answers](./05-question-bank/).
6. In the final revision, use the [condensed modules](./02-condensed-notes/), [rule checklist](./07-quick-revision/One_Page_Rules_and_Cases.md), and [self-assessment paper](./09-practice-worksheets/Self_Assessment_Test.md).

## Syllabus checklist

- [ ] Module 1: Interpretation, construction, statutes and General Clauses Act
- [ ] Module 2: General rules, Mimansa and interpretive doctrines
- [ ] Module 3: Internal and external aids
- [ ] Module 4: Approaches, judicial/juristic restraint and jurisdictional presumptions
- [ ] Module 5: Constitutional interpretation and precedent
- [ ] Module 6: Penal, taxing and remedial statutes; mens rea and vicarious liability
- [ ] Module 7: Commencement, operation, expiry and repeal
