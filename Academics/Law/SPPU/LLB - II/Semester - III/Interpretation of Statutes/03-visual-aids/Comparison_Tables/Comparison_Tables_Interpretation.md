# Interpretation of Statutes — Comparison Tables

## Literal, golden and mischief rules

| Rule | Starting point | When useful | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Literal | Ordinary grammatical meaning in context | Text is clear or ordinary meaning resolves issue | Not dictionary isolation; context still matters |
| Golden | Ordinary meaning | Literal reading creates genuine absurdity or inconsistency | Minimal adjustment only; not judicial rewriting |
| Mischief | Purpose and defect addressed | Ambiguity; need to understand remedy intended | Purpose cannot contradict clear language |

## Ejusdem generis and noscitur a sociis

| Basis | Ejusdem generis | Noscitur a sociis |
|---|---|---|
| Meaning | Of the same kind | Known by its associates |
| Pattern | Specific words followed by general words | Ambiguous word surrounded by associated words |
| Requirement | Identifiable genus among listed specifics | Contextual association among neighbouring words |
| Effect | Narrows general words to the genus | Colours meaning of the uncertain word |

## Internal and external aids

| Feature | Internal aid | External aid |
|---|---|---|
| Location | Within the enactment | Outside the enactment |
| Examples | Preamble, definition, proviso, heading, schedule | Debates, committee report, prior law, dictionary, treaty |
| Use | Context and statutory scheme | Historical/policy background or technical meaning |
| Weight | Part of enactment, but weight differs by aid | Supplementary; cannot override clear enacted text |

## Repeal and expiry

| Feature | Repeal | Expiry of temporary Act |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanism | Later legislature revokes all/part expressly or impliedly | Act ceases under its own sunset/term |
| General rule | GCA s.6 may preserve accrued rights/proceedings unless contrary intention | Examine expiry and saving clauses; s.6 does not automatically apply |
| Key question | Is there clear repeal and what is saved? | What consequences survive expiration? |
