# Condensed Revision: Modules 1–3

## Module 1 — Interpretation and GCA

- Interpretation ascertains the meaning conveyed by enacted words and applies it to facts; text and context lead.
- Interpretation/construction distinction is useful but not absolute in judicial usage.
- Classify statutes by duration, purpose, coverage, form and operation; classification guides but does not decide construction.
- General Clauses Act, 1897 supplies default definitions and rules for Central Acts/Regulations unless context or special definition indicates otherwise.
- GCA s.3: definitions; s.5: commencement; s.6: repeal savings; s.13: gender/number; s.21: amend/vary/rescind power; s.24: continuity of instruments after repeal and re-enactment.

## Module 2 — Rules

- Start with ordinary meaning in context; clear text normally controls.
- Literal: ordinary grammatical meaning.
- Golden: limited adjustment to avoid genuine absurdity.
- Mischief: prior law → defect → remedy → construction advancing remedy (Heydon's Case).
- Harmonious: give effect to all provisions where possible.
- Last antecedent: qualifier usually attaches to nearest words; context can displace.
- Non-obstante: overrides identified conflicting provisions only within its scope.
- Legal fiction: accept statutory assumption and necessary consequences, but do not extend beyond purpose.
- “And/or”: ordinary grammatical sense may change where context requires.
- Ejusdem generis: general words confined to a clear genus after specific words.
- Noscitur a sociis: word understood from its associates.
- Reddendo singula singulis: distribute phrases to corresponding subjects.
- Mimansa: Shruti, Linga, Vakya, Prakarana, Sthana, Samakhya; structured aids, not substitutes for enacted text.

## Module 3 — Aids

- Internal: preamble, definitions, long/short title, headings, sections, proviso, explanation, illustration, schedule, punctuation.
- External: prior law/history, Objects and Reasons, debates, committee reports, dictionaries, other statutes, international materials, social/technical context, precedent.
- Use text first; internal context next; external aids where uncertainty remains. Aids clarify, not rewrite.
