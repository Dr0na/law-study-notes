# Condensed Revision: Modules 4–7

## Module 4 — Approaches and jurisdiction

- Textual, intentionalist, purposive and pragmatic approaches all remain bounded by enacted text and legal authority.
- Judicial restraint respects institutional roles; activism enforces law/rights robustly within jurisdiction.
- Civil jurisdiction is not excluded lightly; ouster may be express or necessary by implication. Constitutional review is not ordinarily excluded. *Dhulabhai*; *L. Chandra Kumar*.
- Jurisdiction must be conferred; check subject matter, parties, territory and remedy.
- Prefer construction consistent with international law if text permits; clear domestic legislation controls.

## Module 5 — Constitution

- Constitutional interpretation accounts for text, structure, history, precedent, purpose and values.
- Textualism focuses on text; originalism on meaning at adoption; neither is the sole Indian method.
- Pith and substance: true nature of law tests legislative competence.
- Colourable legislation: cannot do indirectly what legislature cannot do directly; motive is not the test.
- Article 254 addresses repugnancy in concurrent field; harmonise first.
- Residuary power: Article 248/List I Entry 97.
- Basic structure limits Article 368 amendment power: *Kesavananda Bharati*; *Minerva Mills*.
- Art.141 binding precedent; Art.143 advisory jurisdiction; prospective overruling adopted in *Golak Nath*.
- Anti-majoritarian objection: tension between elected lawmaking and constitutional judicial review.

## Module 6 — Penal, taxing, remedial

- Tax requires clear statutory authority; no tax by implication. Charge vs exemption analysis differs.
- Penal provisions require fair notice; no offence/punishment by analogy; Art.20(1) limits retrospectivity.
- Mens rea presumed unless excluded expressly or by necessary implication: *Nathulal*; context may exclude: *Mayer Hans George*.
- BNS 2023 commenced 1 July 2024; offence-specific statute and transitional rules matter.
- Vicarious criminal liability requires statutory language and pleaded ingredients: *S.M.S. Pharmaceuticals*.
- Remedial law may be construed beneficially where text allows; do not erase eligibility conditions.
- Mandatory/directory depends on purpose, consequences and prejudice, not “shall/may” alone.

## Module 7 — Operation and repeal

- Commencement date comes from Act or notification; assent/enactment alone may not settle date.
- Presumption against retrospective imposition of new substantive burdens; procedural rules may apply to pending matters subject to intent.
- Temporary statute expires by its terms; check express savings. Do not assume GCA s.6 automatically governs expiry.
- Repeal may be express or implied; implied repeal narrowly inferred where laws cannot coexist or later law replaces field.
- GCA s.6 preserves prior operation, accrued rights/liabilities, penalties and related proceedings unless different intention appears.
- Check savings, transitional clauses, s.24 continuity, Article 20(1) and exact conduct dates.
