## Working with the extracted notes

The extracted Markdown files preserve text from the supplied local DOCX and text-bearing PDF handouts, grouped by topic and labelled with original filenames. They are source transcriptions, not vetted authorities. Some source handouts contain repetition, typographical errors, outdated examples, or propositions that need qualification; the complete notes provide a clearer, syllabus-aligned account. Image-only PDFs remain available in `00-syllabus-and-references/Notes/` and are identified in the extraction index.
