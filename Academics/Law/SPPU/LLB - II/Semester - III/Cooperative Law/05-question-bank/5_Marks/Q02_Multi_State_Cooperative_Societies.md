# Q2. Multi-State Co-operative Societies

The Multi-State Co-operative Societies Act, 2002 governs cooperatives whose objects are not confined to one State and whose members or operations serve more than one State, subject to the Act's requirements. It is administered by the Central Registrar. The statute provides for registration, bye-laws, governance, audit, inquiry, winding up and dispute settlement.

Part IXB remains applicable to multi-State cooperative societies following *Union of India v. Rajendra N. Shah* (2021), even though it was held inapplicable to State cooperatives for want of State ratification.

**Distinction:** A Maharashtra-only society is generally governed by the MCS Act, 1960; a qualifying multi-State society is governed by the 2002 central Act.
