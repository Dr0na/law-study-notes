# Q3. Audit and rectification of defects

Section 81 of the MCS Act requires statutory audit of cooperative societies through the prescribed process by an eligible auditor. Audit examines accounts, transactions and compliance, and identifies irregularities or weaknesses. Under section 82, the society must rectify defects and file the prescribed rectification report within the statutory period.

Audit supports member protection and financial accountability, but does not replace the committee's duty to maintain proper books or the Registrar's separate inquiry and inspection powers. If audit reveals serious misapplication or breach of trust, further proceedings may follow under the Act, including section 88 where its conditions are met.
