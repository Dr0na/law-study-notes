# Q1. Freedom to form cooperative societies under Article 19(1)(c)

The 97th Constitutional Amendment inserted “co-operative societies” into Article 19(1)(c), which protects the right to form associations or unions. The right is subject to reasonable restrictions permitted by Article 19(4). Article 43B separately directs the State to promote voluntary formation, autonomous functioning, democratic control and professional management of cooperatives.

In *Union of India v. Rajendra N. Shah* (2021), the Supreme Court held that Part IXB was invalid insofar as it applied to State cooperative societies because the amendment affecting State legislative power required ratification by half the States. The decision upheld its application to multi-State societies and Union Territories.

**Recall:** Article 19 gives the freedom; Article 43B gives the policy direction; *Rajendra N. Shah* marks the federal limit.
