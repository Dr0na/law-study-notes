# Q2. Distinguish audit, inquiry and inspection under the MCS Act, 1960.

| Mechanism | Section | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Audit | s.81 | Periodic examination of accounts and compliance by an eligible auditor through the statutory process. |
| Rectification | s.82 | Requires the society to address defects identified in audit and submit the prescribed report. |
| Inquiry | s.83 | Registrar's examination of the society's constitution, working or financial condition, on own motion or a qualifying application. |
| Inspection | s.84 | Targeted inspection of books of an indebted society on a creditor's application meeting statutory conditions. |
| Surcharge | s.88 | Assessment against persons responsible for misapplication, retention, misfeasance or breach of trust, after statutory process. |

Audit is regular and accounts-focused. Inquiry is broader and can investigate how the society is constituted and functioning. Inspection is a specific examination under the statutory trigger. Findings may lead to rectification, directions, costs, surcharge or other action, but each consequence requires its own legal conditions and procedural safeguards.

**Conclusion:** These powers protect members and creditors and support accountable governance; they are not interchangeable labels for the same process.
