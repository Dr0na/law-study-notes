# Q3. Explain the roles of the general body and managing committee in a cooperative society.

Section 72 of the MCS Act makes the general body the society's final authority, subject to the Act, Rules and bye-laws. It exercises authority through properly convened meetings and lawful resolutions. The AGM under section 75 reviews the society's annual business and financial position; a special general meeting under section 76 deals with specified or urgent matters and may be requisitioned under statutory conditions.

The managing committee under section 73 manages the society's affairs between general meetings. It implements resolutions, protects assets, maintains records and accounts, ensures statutory filings, convenes meetings and oversees member services. It must act within powers granted by law and bye-laws and remains accountable to the general body and statutory authorities.

Both bodies must follow requirements on notice, agenda, quorum, voting eligibility, minutes and conflict management. Registrar supervision is available under specified statutory provisions, including inquiry, audit, inspection and committee-related remedies. An unlawful general-body resolution does not become valid merely because members approved it.

**Conclusion:** The general body supplies democratic authority; the committee supplies ongoing management. Effective cooperation requires both member oversight and competent administration.
