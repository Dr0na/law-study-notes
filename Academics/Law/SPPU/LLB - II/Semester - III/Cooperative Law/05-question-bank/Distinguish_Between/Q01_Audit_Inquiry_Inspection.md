# Distinguish between Audit, Inquiry and Inspection

| Basis | Audit | Inquiry | Inspection |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main provision | MCS Act s.81 | s.83 | s.84 |
| Main purpose | Examine accounts and compliance | Examine constitution, working or financial condition | Targeted examination of books under specified conditions |
| Usual character | Periodic statutory process | Broader investigation by Registrar | Triggered process, including qualifying creditor application |
| Follow-up | Rectification under s.82; further action if warranted | Findings, directions or other statutory steps | Findings and connected oversight remedies |

All three promote accountability, but each has a distinct statutory trigger, scope and procedure.
