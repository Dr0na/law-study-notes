# Study Path — Cooperative Law

## Recommended sequence

1. Read the [syllabus](<./00-syllabus-and-references/00 - LDSE 0707 Cooperative Law - Syllabus.md>) and the six [complete notes](./01-complete-notes/).
2. Use the [comparison tables](./03-visual-aids/Comparison_Tables/Comparison_Tables_Cooperative_Law.md) and [process flowcharts](./03-visual-aids/Flowcharts/Flowcharts_Cooperative_Law.md) to connect the concepts with statutory procedure.
3. Revise the [case law digest](./04-case-laws/Landmark_Cases_Summary.md) and attach relevant cases to constitutional and cooperative autonomy questions.
4. Practise the [model answers](./05-question-bank/) with the [answer-writing template](./08-exam-strategy/Answer_Writing_Template.md).
5. Before the exam, revise the [condensed notes](./02-condensed-notes/), [mnemonics](./07-quick-revision/All_Mnemonics_One_Page.md), and [statutory checklist](./07-quick-revision/Statutory_Section_Checklist.md).
6. Attempt the [self-assessment paper](./09-practice-worksheets/Self_Assessment_Test.md) under timed conditions.

## Syllabus checklist

- [ ] Module 1: Theory and principles of cooperation
- [ ] Module 2: History, growth, State role, institutions, legislation and Constitution
- [ ] Module 3: Registration, membership, bye-laws and structural changes
- [ ] Module 4: Incorporation, privileges, governance, elections and Registrar supervision
- [ ] Module 5: Disputes, Co-operative Courts, appeals, recovery, offences and liquidation
- [ ] Module 6: State aid, funds, accounts, audit and inspection

Use the supplied Act and Rules PDF for the precise text. Some reference study guides contain outdated or oversimplified statements; the notes identify places where the Act's wording is more specific.
