# Previous Papers and Exam Trends

## Available paper

The references folder includes **708_Cooperative_Law_Q.P..pdf**, an SPPU LL.B. Semester III Cooperative Law paper under the 2017 pattern (November 2022 and April 2022 papers are extractable). The supplied 2023 CBCS study materials describe a 70-mark theory paper, but students should confirm the current university pattern from the latest examination circular.

## Recurring themes in the supplied paper

- development of the cooperative movement in India;
- admission, removal and expulsion of members;
- registration, limited/unlimited liability and deregistration;
- general body meetings;
- State support to cooperatives;
- comparison with companies, trusts and partnerships;
- rights, duties and privileges of societies;
- accounts and registers;
- liquidation, Multi-State societies, apex/federal societies and constitutional freedom.

## Preparation guidance

Prepare each listed theme as a full answer, but cross-map it to the current six-module CBCS 2023 syllabus. The older paper's marks distribution and question wording are useful for practice, not proof of the current exam pattern.
