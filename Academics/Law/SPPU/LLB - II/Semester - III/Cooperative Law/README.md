## Statutory use note

The core statute is the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, 1960 (MCS Act) and the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Rules, 1961. Section references in these notes are keyed to the supplied bare-act PDF and should be checked against the latest official consolidated text and applicable amendments before relying on a deadline, amount, form, or procedure. The Supreme Court's decision in *Union of India v. Rajendra N. Shah* materially limits the application of Part IXB to State cooperative societies.

The syllabus is the controlling outline. Housing-society rules and the Multi-State Co-operative Societies Act are included only as focused subtopics where relevant; they do not replace the six syllabus modules.
