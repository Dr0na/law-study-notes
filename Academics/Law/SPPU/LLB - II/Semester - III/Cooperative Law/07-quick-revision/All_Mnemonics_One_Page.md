# Cooperative Law — Quick Mnemonics

## ICA principles: “V-D-E-A-E-C-C”

- **V**oluntary and open membership
- **D**emocratic member control
- **E**conomic participation by members
- **A**utonomy and independence
- **E**ducation, training and information
- **C**ooperation among cooperatives
- **C**oncern for community

## Registration path: “O-L-P-A-R-C”

- **O**bjects fit s.4
- **L**iability form (s.5)
- **P**romoters and conditions (s.6)
- **A**pplication and bye-laws (s.8)
- **R**egistrar's scrutiny and decision (s.9)
- **C**ertificate/classification (ss.10, 12)

## Dispute jurisdiction: “S-P-L-F”

- **S**ubject matter touches constitution/election/meeting/management/business
- **P**arties are within s.91 classes
- **L**imitation checked under s.92
- **F**orum and remedy identified

## Financial oversight: “A-R-I-S”

- **A**udit — s.81
- **R**ectification — s.82
- **I**nquiry / inspection — ss.83–84
- **S**urcharge — s.88

## Liquidation stages: “O-H-R-C”

- **O**rder (s.102)
- **H**andover and liquidator (s.103)
- **R**ealise assets and resolve claims (s.105)
- **C**lose with audited final report and surplus disposition (ss.108–110)
