# Cooperative Law — Comparison Tables

## Cooperative, company, partnership and trust

| Basis | Cooperative | Company | Partnership | Trust |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Organising purpose | Common member needs and service | Business and shareholder return | Business and profit-sharing | Purpose/benefit stated in trust instrument |
| Participants | Members, often users | Shareholders | Partners | Trustees administer; beneficiaries benefit |
| Control | Democratic; generally one member, one vote subject to MCS Act | Board/shareholder rights, often share-weighted | As agreed by partners | Trustee duties and instrument |
| Separate entity | Yes, body corporate under MCS Act s.36 | Yes | Not ordinarily separate from partners in the same way | Depends on the trust's legal form and governing law |
| Distribution | Regulated; reserves/member benefit | Dividends under company law | Agreed profit share | Applied to trust purposes/beneficiaries |

## Audit, inquiry and inspection

| Feature | Audit — s.81 | Inquiry — s.83 | Inspection — s.84 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Examine accounts and compliance | Examine constitution, working or financial condition | Targeted examination of books under statutory conditions |
| Initiation | Periodic statutory requirement | Registrar or qualifying application | Creditor application subject to statutory conditions |
| Result | Audit report; defects may require rectification | Findings and directions/next statutory steps | Information relevant to creditor/Registrar oversight |
| Follow-up | Rectification under s.82; possible further action | Costs, directions or surcharge as law permits | Costs and defect reporting under connected provisions |

## Deregistration and winding up

| Point | Deregistration (s.21A) | Winding up / liquidation (ss.102–110) |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Cancellation of registration on statutory ground | Process to realise assets, settle liabilities and conclude the society |
| Main actor | Registrar under the specific deregistration provision | Registrar and appointed liquidator |
| Central concern | Whether registration should continue | Orderly settlement of claims and surplus |
| Result | Legal consequences specified by the Act | Termination after liquidator's final process and statutory closure |
