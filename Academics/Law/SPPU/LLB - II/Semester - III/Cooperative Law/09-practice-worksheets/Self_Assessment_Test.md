# Self-Assessment Test — Cooperative Law

**Suggested duration:** 3 hours. Confirm the current university format before attempting. Answer with statutory sections and reasoned conclusions.

## Part A — Long answers (attempt any three; 15 marks each)

1. Explain the principles and characteristics of a cooperative society. Compare it with a company, partnership and trust.
2. Trace the history and development of the cooperative movement in India and explain the role of the State, NCDC and NABARD.
3. Describe the registration of a cooperative society under the MCS Act, including limited/unlimited liability, application, Registrar's decision and bye-laws.
4. Explain the general body, managing committee and Registrar's supervisory role in governance of a cooperative society. Address the effect of *Rajendra N. Shah* where relevant.
5. Explain the jurisdiction of the Co-operative Court and the procedure for dispute settlement, appeal and recovery under the MCS Act.
6. Describe winding up and liquidation of a cooperative society, from the initial order to final report and disposal of surplus.

## Part B — Short answers (attempt any four; 10 marks each)

1. Open membership, membership categories and voting rights.
2. State aid and State partnership in cooperatives.
3. Amalgamation, transfer, division and conversion of societies.
4. Audit, rectification, inquiry and inspection: distinguish their functions.
5. Reserve funds, profit appropriation and investment of society funds.
6. Constitutional freedom to form cooperative societies and Article 43B.

## Part C — Short notes (attempt any five; 5 marks each)

1. NCDC.
2. NABARD and cooperative credit.
3. One member, one vote under section 27.
4. Rights of members to inspect records.
5. *Union of India v. Rajendra N. Shah*.
6. Nominal membership.
7. Section 101 recovery certificate.
8. Difference between deregistration and liquidation.

## Self-review checklist

- Did I cite sections accurately from the Act?
- Did I distinguish the MCS Act from the Rules and bye-laws?
- Did I explain the required process and safeguards, not merely list powers?
- Did I connect cases to the precise legal issue?
- Did I check current amendments for time limits, thresholds and percentages?
