# Condensed Revision Notes: Modules 4–6

## Module 4 — Governance

- S.36: registered society is a body corporate with perpetual succession and common seal.
- S.72: general body is final authority, subject to Act, Rules and bye-laws.
- Ss.75–76: AGM and SGM; follow statutory time, notice, quorum and agenda.
- S.73: committee manages affairs subject to law, bye-laws and general body decisions.
- Election law, committee tenure and reservations must be checked against current Maharashtra law; Part IXB limits after *Rajendra N. Shah*.
- Registrar powers include registration, audit/inquiry/inspection, enforcement and statutory remedies; identify source and hearing requirements.
- Meeting validity checklist: notice → quorum → eligible voters → lawful agenda → recorded decision.

## Module 5 — Disputes and liquidation

- S.91: Co-operative Court jurisdiction requires covered subject matter **and** covered parties.
- S.91A establishes Co-operative Courts; ss.94–97 cover procedure, decision and appeal.
- S.92: special limitation rules; election disputes generally two months; specified officer/member matters six years; other disputes under Limitation Act subject to section.
- S.163: civil court bar applies to matters assigned by the Act; not every dispute involving a society.
- Ss.98/101/156: statutory recovery routes. S.101 only for specified classes/dues and statutory conditions.
- Ss.102–110: winding-up order → liquidator → realisation and claims → audited accounts/final report → termination and surplus disposition.
- Deregistration (s.21A) is different from winding up (Chapter X).

## Module 6 — Finance and audit

- State partnership/support: Chapter VI, ss.50 onwards; consider aid and autonomy together.
- Ss.43–45: borrowing, lending policy and non-member transactions.
- Ss.64–70: funds, profit appropriation, reserve, education/public purposes and investment.
- S.32: member access to specified books and copies; s.38: register of members.
- S.81 audit; s.82 rectification; s.83 inquiry; s.84 inspection; s.88 surcharge.
- Audit is recurring examination; inquiry examines working/constitution/financial condition; inspection is targeted.
- Always check current amendments for thresholds, percentages, timelines and society-specific rules.
