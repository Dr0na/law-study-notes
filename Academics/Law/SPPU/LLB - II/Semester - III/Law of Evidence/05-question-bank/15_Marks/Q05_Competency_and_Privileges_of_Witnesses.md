# Q5. Discuss the competency and compellability of witnesses, and the rules of Privileged Communications under BSS, 2023.

## 1. Competency and Dumb Witnesses (BSS Sections 124 & 125)
- **Section 124 BSS**: All persons are competent to testify unless tender years, extreme old age, or disease prevent them from giving rational answers.
- **Section 125 BSS**: Dumb witnesses can testify in writing or signs in open court, which is treated as oral evidence.

## 2. Privileged Communications
Confidential disclosures protected by law from being forced into evidence:
- **Matrimonial Privilege (Sec. 128)**: Protects communications between spouses during marriage.
- **State Privilege (Sec. 129)**: Protects unpublished official records relating to affairs of State.
- **Professional Legal Privilege (Sec. 132)**: Protects communications between client and advocate.

## 3. Accomplice competency (Section 138 BSS)
An accomplice is a competent witness, and conviction is not illegal on their uncorroborated testimony. However, prudence requires material corroboration.

## 4. Conclusion
Witness rules facilitate the extraction of truth in court, while privileged communications protect crucial societal and state relationships.
