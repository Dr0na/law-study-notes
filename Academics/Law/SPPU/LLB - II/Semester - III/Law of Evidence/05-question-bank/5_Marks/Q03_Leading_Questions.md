# Q3. What are Leading Questions and when can they be asked?

## Statutory Definition (Section 144 BSS)
A leading question is a question which suggests the answer which the person putting it wishes or expects to receive.

## Rules of Admissibility (Sections 145 & 146 BSS)
1. **When they must NOT be asked (Sec. 145)**: In Examination-in-chief or Re-examination, if objected to by the adverse party, except with the permission of the Court. The court allows them for introductory or undisputed matters.
2. **When they MAY be asked (Sec. 146)**: In Cross-examination.
3. **Rationale**: Leading questions are forbidden in chief-examination to prevent lawyers from putting words into the mouths of friendly witnesses.
