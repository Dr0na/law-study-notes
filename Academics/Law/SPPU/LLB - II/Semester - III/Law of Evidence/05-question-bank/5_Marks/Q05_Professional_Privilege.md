# Q5. Explain Legal Professional Privilege under BSS, 2023.

## Statutory Provision (Section 132-134 BSS)
No advocate, attorney, or pleader shall be permitted, unless with his client's express consent, to disclose any communication made to him in the course and for the purpose of his professional employment.

## Exceptions
1. Communications made in furtherance of any illegal purpose (e.g., client asking how to commit a fraud).
2. Facts observed by the advocate showing that a crime or fraud has been committed since the employment began (e.g., advocate noticing that a document was forged after taking the case).
