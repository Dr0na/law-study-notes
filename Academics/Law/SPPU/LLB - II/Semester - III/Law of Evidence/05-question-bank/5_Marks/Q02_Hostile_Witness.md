# Q2. Explain the concept of a "Hostile Witness" under BSS, 2023.

## Statutory Framework (Section 157 BSS)
Under Section 157 of BSS (formerly Section 154 IEA), the Court may, in its discretion, permit the party who calls a witness to put any questions to him which might be put in cross-examination by the adverse party. A hostile witness is one who is unwilling to tell the truth at the instance of the party calling him.

## Evidentiary Value (Indian Position)
1. In India, the testimony of a hostile witness is not completely rejected.
2. The parts of the testimony that support either the prosecution or the defence can still be relied upon by the court.
3. Case: *State of Rajasthan v. Kartar Singh (AIR 1970 SC 1305)*: Hostile witness testimony remains admissible; the court must evaluate it with caution.
