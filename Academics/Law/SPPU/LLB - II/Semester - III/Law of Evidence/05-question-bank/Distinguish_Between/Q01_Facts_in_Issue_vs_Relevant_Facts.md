# Q1. Distinguish between Facts in Issue and Relevant Facts.

| Basis | Facts in Issue (Sec. 2(g)) | Relevant Facts (Sec. 2(k)) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Meaning** | Core disputed facts asserting rights or liabilities. | Facts logically and legally connected to facts in issue. |
| **Determination** | Determined by substantive law and pleadings. | Determined by Sections 3 to 50 of BSS. |
| **Priority** | Primary facts to be proved. | Secondary facts proving or disproving facts in issue. |
| **Example** | Whether A hit B with intent to kill. | Whether A was seen running with a knife after B's death. |
