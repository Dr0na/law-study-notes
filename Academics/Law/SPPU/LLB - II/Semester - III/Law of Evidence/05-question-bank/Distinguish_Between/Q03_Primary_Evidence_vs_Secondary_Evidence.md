# Q3. Distinguish between Primary and Secondary Evidence.

| Basis | Primary Evidence (Sec. 57) | Secondary Evidence (Sec. 58) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Definition** | The original document itself produced. | Copies, certified copies, or oral accounts. |
| **Priority** | Highest priority (best evidence rule). | Admissible only under exceptions (Sec. 60). |
| **Notice** | No prior notice required. | Requires prior notice to produce to opponent (Sec. 60). |
| **Example** | Original signed contract. | Certified duplicate or photocopy. |
