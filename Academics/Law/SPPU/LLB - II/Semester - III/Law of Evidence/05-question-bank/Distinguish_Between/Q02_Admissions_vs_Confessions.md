# Q2. Distinguish between Admissions and Confessions.

| Basis | Admissions (Secs. 15-21) | Confessions (Secs. 22-24) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scope** | Genus. Statement suggesting any inference of fact. | Species. Direct admission of guilt of an offence. |
| **Proceedings** | Applicable to both civil and criminal cases. | Applicable only to criminal cases. |
| **Evidentiary Value** | Not conclusive proof; acts as estoppel. | Substantive evidence; can form sole basis of conviction. |
| **Admissibility to Police** | Admissible if made under civil transactions. | Strictly inadmissible under Section 23(1). |
