# Q5. Distinguish between Estoppel and Res Judicata.

| Basis | Estoppel (Sec. 121) | Res Judicata (Sec. 34) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Source** | Party's representations (words/actions). | Court's final decision/judgment. |
| **Purpose** | Prevents inconsistent conduct in court. | Prevents multiplicity of litigation. |
| **Nature** | Rule of Evidence / Equity. | Rule of Public Policy / Civil Procedure. |
| **Application** | Applies to representations of fact. | Applies to matters directly in issue in prior suits. |
