# Q5. Explain the procedure and order of examination of witnesses under BSS, 2023.

## 1. Introduction & Order of Examination (Section 141 BSS)
Section 141 of BSS (formerly Section 138 IEA) outlines the sequential order of witness examination:
1. **Examination-in-chief**: By the party who calls the witness.
2. **Cross-examination**: By the adverse party.
3. **Re-examination**: By the party calling the witness (to clarify inconsistencies from cross-examination).

## 2. Cross-Examination: Crucial for Truth
- Cross-examination is a fundamental right. It aims to test the witness's accuracy, credibility, and detect bias.
- Section 146 allows leading questions during cross-examination.

## 3. Re-Examination: Limits
Re-examination is limited to explaining matters referred to in cross-examination. No new matter can be introduced without court permission.

## 4. Conclusion
The C-C-R sequential order ensures logical presentation of facts while protecting the right to challenge evidence.
