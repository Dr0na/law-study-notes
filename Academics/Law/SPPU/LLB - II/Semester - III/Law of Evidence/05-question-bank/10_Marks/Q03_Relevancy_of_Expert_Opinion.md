# Q3. Discuss the relevancy and evidentiary value of Expert Opinions under BSS Section 39.

## 1. Introduction & Statutory Provision
Section 39 BSS (formerly Section 45 IEA) states that when the Court has to form an opinion upon a point of foreign law, science, art, or as to identity of handwriting or finger impressions, the opinions of experts are relevant facts.

## 2. Evidentiary Value of Expert Opinion
1. **Advisory Nature**: Expert opinions are corroborative and not substantive evidence. The court is not bound by the opinion and must form its own view (*State of HP v. Jailal*).
2. **Technical Aid**: It assists the judge in understanding technical fields where they lack specialized training.
3. **Weight**: The weight of the opinion depends on the reliability of the data and the scientific reasons provided.

## 3. Modern Digital Expert Opinions (Sec. 40 BSS)
With BSS, opinions of the Examiner of Electronic Evidence (certified under the IT Act, 2000) are relevant for electronic records (emails, logs, signatures).

## 4. Conclusion
Expert opinions are crucial in modern litigation, especially with forensics and digital evidence, but are strictly advisory.
