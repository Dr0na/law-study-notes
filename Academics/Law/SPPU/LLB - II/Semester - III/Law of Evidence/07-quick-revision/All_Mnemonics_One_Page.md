# All Mnemonics & Recall Aids

## 1. May, Shall, Conclusive Presumptions
*   **Mnemonic**: **MSC**
    - **M** -> May Presume (Sec. 2(h) BSS) — Court has discretion to presume or require proof.
    - **S** -> Shall Presume (Sec. 2(l) BSS) — Court must presume, rebuttable.
    - **C** -> Conclusive Proof (Sec. 2(b) BSS) — Court must presume, irrebuttable.

## 2. Relevancy of Fact Exceptions (Hearsay Exceptions)
*   **Mnemonic**: **R-C-A-D**
    - **R** -> Res Gestae (Sec. 4)
    - **C** -> Conspiracy common intention (Sec. 8)
    - **A** -> Admissions & Confessions (Sec. 15-24)
    - **D** -> Dying Declarations (Sec. 26(1))

## 3. Privileged Communications
*   **Mnemonic**: **M-S-O-P**
    - **M** -> Matrimonial communications (Sec. 128)
    - **S** -> State secrets (Sec. 129)
    - **O** -> Official communications in confidence (Sec. 130)
    - **P** -> Professional legal communications (Sec. 132)

## 4. Burden of Proof vs Onus
*   **Mnemonic**: **BO**
    - **B** -> Burden of proof (Legal burden, constant, Sec. 104)
    - **O** -> Onus of proof (Evidentiary onus, shifting, Sec. 105)
