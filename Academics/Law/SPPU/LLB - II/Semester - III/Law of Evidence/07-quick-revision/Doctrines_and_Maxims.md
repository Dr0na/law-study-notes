# Key Doctrines & Legal Maxims

## 1. Doctrines
- **Doctrine of Res Gestae (BSS Sec. 4)**: Spontaneous contemporaneous statements forming part of the same transaction are admissible.
- **Plea of Alibi (BSS Sec. 9)**: The claim that the accused was elsewhere at the time of the crime.
- **Doctrine of Estoppel (BSS Sec. 121)**: Restricts a person from denying their own prior representation of fact.
- **Doctrine of Promissory Estoppel**: Binds administrative authorities to promises acted upon by citizens.

## 2. Legal Maxims
- ***Nemo moriturus praesumitur mentiri***: "A dying man is not presumed to lie." (Basis of Dying Declarations under Sec. 26(1)).
- ***Allegans contraria non est audiendus***: "He is not to be heard who alleges things contradictory to each other." (Basis of Estoppel under Sec. 121).
- ***Interest reipublicae ut sit finis litium***: "It is in the interest of the State that litigation should have an end." (Basis of Res Judicata under Sec. 34 and Section 169 BSS).
