## ⚡ BSA, 2023 Statutory Highlights

The Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 replaces the Indian Evidence Act, 1872 for proceedings governed by the BSA. Key provisions reflected in these notes include:
1.  **Modernized Definitions**: Fact, Relevant Fact, and Facts in Issue mapped under Section 2.
2.  **Digital Records**: Section 61 gives electronic and digital records the same legal effect as other documents, subject to the BSA. Section 62 says their contents are proved under section 63, which sets the admissibility rules. Section 57 addresses when electronic or digital records count as primary evidence.
3.  **Section Restructuring**: The section numbers differ from the IEA (e.g., Res Gestae is section 4 BSA / section 6 IEA; Estoppel is section 121 BSA / section 115 IEA).
