## ⚡ BSS, 2023 Statutory Highlights

The Bharatiya Sakshya Sanhita, 2023 replaces the Indian Evidence Act, 1872. Key changes reflected in these notes include:
1.  **Modernized Definitions**: Fact, Relevant Fact, and Facts in Issue mapped under Section 2.
2.  **Digital Records**: Electronic records are now primary evidence under Section 62, with certification requirements under Section 63.
3.  **Section Restructuring**: Mapping equivalent sections of BSS and IEA (e.g., Res Gestae Section 4 BSS / Section 6 IEA; Estoppel Section 121 BSS / Section 115 IEA).
