# Previous Year Papers (PYQs)

With the transition from the Indian Evidence Act, 1872 to the **Bharatiya Sakshya Sanhita (BSS), 2023** (effective 1st July 2024), there are no official university past papers for BSS.

Students should practice the model question paper provided in [09-practice-worksheets/Self_Assessment_Test.md](../09-practice-worksheets/Self_Assessment_Test.md) which mirrors the new SPPU exam pattern.
