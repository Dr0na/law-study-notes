# Condensed Revision Notes: Modules 5 to 8

## Module 5: Witnesses
- **Competency (Sec. 124 BSS)**: Capacity to understand questions and give rational answers. Tender years or old age are not bars if rational.
- **Verbal Incapacity (Sec. 125 BSS)**: Deaf/dumb witness can write or sign in open court (treated as oral evidence).
- **Privileged Communications**:
  - Matrimonial (Sec. 128)
  - State secrets (Sec. 129)
  - Legal Professional (Sec. 132)
- **Accomplice (Sec. 138 BSS)**: A competent witness against co-accused. Conviction not illegal on uncorroborated testimony, but prudence requires material corroboration.

## Module 6: Burden of Proof
- **Legal Burden (Sec. 104 BSS)**: Burden to prove the case. Never shifts (always on prosecution in criminal trials).
- **Evidentiary Onus (Sec. 105 BSS)**: Burden to introduce evidence. Shifts continuously.
- **Special Knowledge (Sec. 107 BSS)**: Burden of proving facts especially within one's knowledge is on that person (e.g., ticketless travel).
- **Dowry Death Presumption (Sec. 120 BSS)**: If cruelty for dowry is shown soon before death, the court *shall* presume the husband caused it.

## Module 7: Oral Evidence
- **Direct Evidence (Sec. 55 BSS)**: Oral evidence must always be direct (what was seen, heard, or perceived). Hearsay is excluded.
- **Examination Order (Sec. 141 BSS)**:
  1. Examination-in-chief
  2. Cross-examination
  3. Re-examination
- **Hostile Witness (Sec. 157 BSS)**: Witness turning rogue. Party calling him can cross-examine him with court's permission.

## Module 8: Estoppel
- **Estoppel (Sec. 121 BSS)**: Prevents a person from denying a statement of fact they previously induced others to act upon.
- **Promissory Estoppel**: Applies to government promises acting in administrative capacity. Case: *Motilal Padampat Sugar Mills*.
- **Exceptions**: No estoppel against a statute/law, or against the Constitution.
- **Improper Admission/Rejection (Sec. 169 BSS)**: Retrial not granted if other sufficient evidence exists or if rejected evidence wouldn't change the outcome.
