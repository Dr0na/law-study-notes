## Accuracy and citation note

The supplied reference PDFs have been reviewed where their scanned text is legible. The materials support the conceptual organization of ethics and historical background in these notes; their case summaries and rule statements are checked against the Acts, BCI Rules and judgments rather than treated as controlling. Several PDFs are scanned images with inconsistent OCR, so unclear passages are not quoted. The principal legal sources remain the Advocates Act, 1961; Bar Council of India Rules; Contempt of Courts Act, 1971; Constitution; official e-Courts/Department of Justice material; and the prescribed judgments. Citation differences in the syllabus are called out in the case digest. Verify amendments, current rules and court directions before relying on procedural details.
