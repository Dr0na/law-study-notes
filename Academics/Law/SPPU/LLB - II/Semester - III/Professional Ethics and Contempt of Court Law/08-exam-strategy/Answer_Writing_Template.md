# Answer-Writing Template

## For a problem question

1. **Issue:** Identify the precise ethical, statutory or contempt question raised by the facts.
2. **Rule:** State the governing section/rule and its elements. Do not substitute a general maxim for the statutory test.
3. **Authority:** Give one or two relevant cases with accurate citations and a short ratio.
4. **Application:** Apply each element to the facts, including knowledge, authority, wilfulness, prejudice, available safeguards or the advocate’s instructions as relevant.
5. **Conclusion:** State the likely legal consequence, while noting any missing fact that could change the result.

## For a 10- or 15-mark essay

- Open with a concise definition and explain why the doctrine matters.
- Set out the constitutional/statutory framework in logical order.
- Use clear subheadings for elements, powers/procedure, safeguards and remedies.
- Analyse at least two relevant cases; explain their principle rather than listing names.
- Address the competing value where relevant: client rights, fair trial, professional independence or free speech.
- Conclude with a balanced statement tied to the statute and authorities.

## Citation checklist

- Verify party names, reporter, year, volume and page from the judgment or a reliable reporter.
- Prefer a verified SCC citation when the syllabus AIR citation appears inconsistent.
- Distinguish a case’s holding from obiter observations and commentary.
- Never invent a quotation, fact, date, bench strength or procedural result; paraphrase unless the judgment text is available.
- If unsure, state the proposition cautiously and identify the uncertainty rather than guessing.
