# Question Bank — Professional Ethics and Contempt

These are syllabus-aligned practice prompts, not predictions of an examination paper.

## Long answers (15 marks)

1. Trace the development of the legal profession in India from the pre-colonial period to the Advocates Act, 1961.
2. Explain an advocate’s duties to the court, client, opponent and colleagues under the BCI Rules.
3. Explain the composition, functions and rule-making powers of the State Bar Councils and Bar Council of India.
4. Discuss admission, enrolment, senior advocates, transfer between State rolls and pre-audience under the Advocates Act.
5. Explain the right to practise under sections 29–34 and analyse the law on advocates’ strikes with reference to *Harish Uppal*.
6. Explain the disciplinary process under sections 35–44 of the Advocates Act, including appeals and review.
7. Define civil and criminal contempt and explain statutory defences, procedure, punishment and appeal under the Contempt of Courts Act, 1971.
8. Discuss the relationship between freedom of speech and contempt, with reference to *Mulgaokar*, *Arundhati Roy* and *Prashant Bhushan*.
9. Evaluate the benefits and risks of e-Courts, including access, due process, privacy and the digital divide.
10. Discuss responsible use of AI in court administration and legal practice, distinguishing assistance from adjudication.

## Medium answers (10 marks)

1. Explain the restrictions on advertising and other employment for advocates.
2. What is the role of a vakalatnama? Discuss the limits on counsel’s authority to compromise or concede.
3. Explain the meaning of “professional or other misconduct” under section 35.
4. Discuss the complaint and reference routes for criminal contempt under section 15.
5. Explain apology and truth as matters relevant to contempt proceedings.
6. Compare contempt under the 1971 Act with CPC Order XXXIX rule 2A.
7. Explain e-filing, registry scrutiny and e-service, noting the role of local court rules.
8. Write a note on electronic records and the need to use current evidence-law provisions.

## Short notes (5 marks)

1. Right of pre-audience.
2. Section 48AA review and *D. Saibaba*.
3. Advocates’ duty to attend criminal trials: *S.J. Chaudhary*.
4. Bar resolutions and representation of accused persons: *A.S. Mohammed Rafi*.
5. Truth defence under section 13(b).
6. Fair and accurate reporting under section 4.
7. Section 20 limitation.
8. National Judicial Data Grid and public case information.
9. Digital divide in virtual hearings.
10. Citation correction: *L.D. Jaikwal* and *S. Mulgaokar*.

## Problem questions

1. Counsel agrees to a settlement that gives up a client’s substantive claim without first obtaining instructions. Identify the relevant duties and authority.
2. A Bar Association passes a resolution that no member will represent a person accused of an unpopular offence. Analyse the rights and duties involved.
3. A litigant has the ability to comply with a clear injunction but deliberately disobeys it. Explain the civil contempt test and distinguish CPC Order XXXIX rule 2A.
4. A person publishes a fair, evidence-based criticism of a final judgment. Identify the statutory safeguards and explain why context matters.
5. A lawyer posts a contemptuous allegation about a pending case on social media. Explain the criminal contempt framework and procedural safeguards without assuming the outcome from the medium alone.
