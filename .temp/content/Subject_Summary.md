# Subject Summary: LCC 0702 Law of Evidence

This subject summary compiles the structural details, statutory layout, and core principles of the **Law of Evidence** under the **Bharatiya Sakshya Sanhita, 2023** (BSS) framework.

## Statutory Overview: BSS, 2023 vs IEA, 1872
The Bharatiya Sakshya Sanhita, 2023 replaces the Indian Evidence Act, 1872. While it maintains the core principles of evidence law, it restructures the chapters and sections, and introduces comprehensive rules for electronic and digital evidence.

### Comparison of Structure:
- **Indian Evidence Act, 1872**: 167 Sections, 11 Chapters, 3 Parts.
- **Bharatiya Sakshya Sanhita, 2023**: 170 Sections, 12 Chapters, 4 Parts.

---

## Module-wise Core Focus Areas

### Part 1: Relevancy of Facts (Modules 1-3)
- **Introduction**: Focuses on fact classifications, standard of proof (proved, disproved, not proved), and presumptions (may presume, shall presume, conclusive proof).
- **Relevance**: Concepts of relevance vs admissibility, res gestae (Sec. 4 BSS), identification parades (Sec. 7 BSS), common intention (Sec. 8 BSS), and plea of alibi (Sec. 9 BSS).
- **Admissions and Confessions**: Admissions (Secs. 15-21 BSS) and Confessions (Secs. 22-24 BSS), including the exclusionary rules for police confessions and the discovery of fact exception (Sec. 23(2) BSS).
- **Statements by Unavailable Persons**: Dying Declarations (Sec. 26(1) BSS) and Expert Testimony (Sec. 39 BSS).

### Part 2: Mode of Proof (Modules 4 & 7)
- **Oral Evidence**: Must be direct in all cases (Sec. 55 BSS), excluding hearsay except for statutory exceptions.
- **Documentary Evidence**: Primary vs Secondary evidence (Secs. 57-60 BSS), and electronic evidence certification (Secs. 61-63 BSS).
- **Exclusion of Oral by Documentary**: General exclusion of oral evidence to contradict written terms (Secs. 94-95 BSS).

### Part 3: Production and Effect of Evidence (Modules 5, 6 & 8)
- **Burden of Proof**: Shifting of onus vs constant burden of proof (Secs. 104-107 BSS), and presumptions in dowry death (Sec. 120 BSS) or abetment of suicide (Sec. 119 BSS).
- **Estoppel**: Prevents blowing hot and cold (Sec. 121 BSS), including Promissory Estoppel against administrative actions.
- **Witnesses**: Competency (Sec. 124 BSS), examination stages (chief, cross, re-examination under Sec. 141 BSS), and hostile witness treatment (Sec. 157 BSS).
