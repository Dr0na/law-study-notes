# Probable Questions for Law of Evidence

# Most Probable Questions for LCC 0702 Law of Evidence

## 15 Marks Essay Questions (Probable 5)
1. **Explain the hierarchy and relevancy of confessions under Bharatiya Sakshya Sanhita, 2023. Distinguish clearly between Admissions and Confessions. Explain the admissibility of a confession made to a police officer.**
2. **What is Electronic and Digital Evidence? Discuss the admissibility and relevancy of Electronic Records with special reference to Section 63 of BSS, 2023 and landmark Supreme Court decisions.**
3. **What is Estoppel? Explain the different types of Estoppel. Discuss the doctrine of Promissory Estoppel with relevant case laws.**
4. **Explain the general rules of Burden of Proof under BSS. When does the burden of proof shift? Discuss the presumptions as to certain offences.**
5. **Discuss the competency and compellability of witnesses. Explain the concept of Privileged Communications under BSS with illustrations.**

## 10 Marks Descriptive Questions (Probable 5)
1. **Explain the Doctrine of Res Gestae under Section 4 of BSS. Discuss its essentials and limitations.**
2. **What is a Dying Declaration? Under what circumstances is it admissible? Can a conviction be based solely on a dying declaration?**
3. **Who is an Expert Witness? Discuss the relevancy of Expert Opinion and the evidentiary value of expert testimony under BSS Section 39.**
4. **Distinguish between Primary and Secondary Evidence. When is secondary evidence admissible in court?**
5. **Explain the procedure of examination of witnesses: Examination-in-chief, Cross-examination, and Re-examination.**
