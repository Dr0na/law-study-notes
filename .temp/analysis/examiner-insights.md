# Examiner Insights & Analysis

Based on historical trends of SPPU Evidence Law exams, the focus remains heavily on statutory provisions and their application in practical cases. With the transition to BSS 2023, examiners will test the corresponding new section numbers and the updated digital evidence provisions under Sections 61-63. Candidates must cite both the BSS 2023 sections and the historic IEA 1872 sections to score maximum marks.
