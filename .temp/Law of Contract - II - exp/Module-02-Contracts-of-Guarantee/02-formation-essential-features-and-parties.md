## 📘 Formation, Essential Features, and Parties to Contract of Guarantee

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Sections 126–127, Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

Formation requires a **valid principal contract** (usually) between creditor and principal debtor, plus **surety’s** undertaking to **answer on default**. **Section 127** clarifies **consideration** for surety: anything done or **promise made** for the benefit of **principal debtor** can suffice.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Parties

- **Creditor** — person to whom guarantee is given  
- **Principal debtor** — person on whose default guarantee is entered  
- **Surety** — person giving guarantee

#### 🔹 Essential features

1. **Tripartite relationship**  
2. **Primary obligation** exists (typically)  
3. **Collateral** promise of surety  
4. **Lawful consideration** (Section 127 pathways)  
5. **Competence** and **free consent**

#### 🔹 Formation modes

Express guarantee deed, **signed** instrument, or **oral** (valid if proved—hard evidentially).

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

- **Consideration** may be past benefit to debtor if within Section 127 language—quote statute in exam.  
- **Misrepresentation/fraud** affecting surety’s consent may vitiate (discharge topics).
