## 📘 Continuing Guarantee and Its Revocation

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Sections 129–130, Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

A **continuing guarantee** extends to a **series of transactions** (Section 129). Revocation rules differ depending on **death of surety**, **surety’s notice**, and **specific contexts** such as **partnership** guarantees (Section 38 Partnership Act sometimes cross-read in advanced courses—stick to ICA unless syllabus says).

**Sections 130–131** (and related) govern revocation in specific situations—learn **bare Act numbering** exactly.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Continuing vs specific

- **Continuing**: covers future obligations in a running relationship (cash credit).  
- **Specific**: one transaction.

#### 🔹 Revocation pathways (ICA)

Study precisely:

- **Section 130** — surety’s revocation for future transactions (wording: “revokes…” effects)  
- **Section 131** — death of surety in continuing guarantee (as per section)

Exam trap: revocation does not always erase **already accrued** liabilities—phrase carefully per statute.

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

1. **Prospective operation** of revocation where statute says so.  
2. **Notice** requirements—match section text.
