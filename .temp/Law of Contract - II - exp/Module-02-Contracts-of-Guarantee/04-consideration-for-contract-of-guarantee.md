## 📘 Consideration for a Contract of Guarantee

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Section 127, Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

Surety’s promise must be supported by **consideration**. Section 127 provides that anything done, or any promise made, **for the benefit** of the **principal debtor** may be sufficient consideration to the surety for giving the guarantee—this broadens practical banking guarantees.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Statutory language (must learn)

Read **Section 127** in bare Act: covers:

- Forbearance to sue  
- Giving time  
- **Any benefit** to principal debtor flowing from creditor’s act/promise

#### 🔹 Past consideration nuance

Indian law context: align with **Section 25** exceptions carefully—examiners may trap you; follow faculty on **past voluntary** acts.

#### 🔹 Absence of direct payment to surety

Unlike common intuition, **surety need not be paid** directly if statutory consideration exists.

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

1. **Benefit to principal debtor** is the statutory anchor.  
2. **Forbearance** and **time** are classic examples.  
3. **Illegal/forbidden** consideration fails.
