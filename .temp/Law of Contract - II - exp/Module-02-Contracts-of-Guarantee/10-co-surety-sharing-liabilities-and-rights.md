## 📘 Co-surety and Manner of Sharing Liabilities and Rights

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Sections 146–147, Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

**Co-sureties** are two or more sureties for **same debt**. Sections **146–147** fix **contribution** between co-sureties: a surety who discharges more than his share may **compel contribution** from others, subject to contractual variation.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Section 146

Co-sureties liable **between themselves** to pay **equal shares** as default rule, **unless** bound by contract to pay **unequal** shares (quote nuance from Act).

#### 🔹 Section 147

If co-sureties bind themselves for **different sums**, liability as **between sureties** follows statutory formula (learn from bare Act: maximum shares principle—reproduce accurately in exam).

#### 🔹 Contractual freedom

Parties may agree **unequal contribution** explicitly.

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

- **External liability** to creditor may still be joint/several per contract.
