## 📘 Surety's Rights Against the Principal Debtor

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Sections 140–141 (and related), Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

After paying the creditor, the surety becomes **subrogated**-like in effect: Sections **140–145** structure surety’s remedies. Against the **principal debtor**, key rights include **right of subrogation** (Section 140 context), **indemnity** from debtor (Section 145), and **right to securities** (Section 141).

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Right to sue creditor’s remedies (subrogation)

Section **140**: upon payment, surety is invested with **all rights** of creditor against principal debtor (as per statutory wording—memorise).

#### 🔹 Right to indemnity (Section 145)

Every surety is entitled to **indemnity** from principal debtor against **loss** suffered by reason of guarantee (quote exceptions/limitations as in Act).

#### 🔹 Right to benefit of creditor’s securities (Section 141)

If surety pays, surety entitled to **benefit of securities** creditor had—**loss of security** by creditor negligence links to discharge (140) and rights.

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

- **Payment** is prerequisite for many rights (timing).  
- Surety cannot **double recover**.
