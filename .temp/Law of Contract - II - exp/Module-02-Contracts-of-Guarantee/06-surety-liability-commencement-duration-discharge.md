## 📘 Nature and Extent of Surety's Liability — Commencement, Duration, Termination, and Discharge

🎚️ Difficulty: 🔴 Hard

**Statute:** Sections 128–141 (core), Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

Surety’s liability is **co-extensive** with that of the principal debtor unless otherwise contracted (Section 128). That single sentence drives **extent**. **Commencement** is upon **principal debtor’s default** to creditor (subject to instrument). **Discharge** of surety occurs on grounds in **Sections 130–141** (variation of terms, release of debtor, composition, etc.—memorise list headings).

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Co-extensive liability (Section 128)

Surety is liable for the **same amount** and on the **same conditions** as debtor, unless contract narrows surety’s liability.

#### 🔹 Commencement

Typically when debtor **defaults** (non-payment/breach). Bank guarantees may use **on-demand** wording—special commercial law treatment may be taught separately.

#### 🔹 Duration

Runs until **discharge** by payment, performance, or **operation of law** under discharge sections.

#### 🔹 Discharge of surety (headings to memorise)

Study and tabulate (verify wording in the **bare Act**):

- **Sections 130–131** — revocation / death in **continuing guarantee**  
- **Section 133** — discharge by **variance** in contract without surety’s consent  
- **Section 134** — discharge by **release or discharge** of principal debtor  
- **Section 135** — discharge when creditor **compounds with**, **gives time to**, or **agrees not to sue** principal debtor (unless surety **assents**)  
- **Section 136** — surety **not** discharged when time is given by contract with a **third person** (not with debtor)  
- **Section 137** — **mere forbearance** to sue / enforce remedies does **not** discharge surety (unless guarantee says otherwise)  
- **Section 138** — release of **one co-surety** does not discharge others; inter se contribution may still arise  
- **Section 139** — creditor’s **act or omission** impairing surety’s **eventual remedy** against principal debtor discharges surety

Exam technique: **one fact per section** in problem answers.
