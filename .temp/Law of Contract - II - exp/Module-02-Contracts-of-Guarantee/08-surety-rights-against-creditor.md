## 📘 Surety's Rights Against the Creditor

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Sections 141–143, Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

Surety’s rights against creditor focus on **securities** and **settlement of accounts** once surety performs. **Section 141** is pivotal: surety is entitled to **benefit of every security** creditor has against debtor at time when contract entered—**even if** surety unaware (statutory text must be checked in bare Act for exact phrasing).
