## 📘 Contract of Guarantee Distinguished from Contract of Indemnity

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Sections 124 vs 126, Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

Students conflate **indemnity** and **guarantee** because both provide **financial backup**. Legally: **indemnity** (S.124) is primarily about **saving from loss**; **guarantee** (S.126) is about **performing/discharge of another’s liability on default**. Number of parties, **trigger**, and **rights** differ.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Parties

- Indemnity: typically **bilateral** (indemnifier–indemnity-holder)  
- Guarantee: **tripartite** (surety–creditor–principal debtor)

#### 🔹 Trigger

- Indemnity: **loss** within contract/S.124–125 frame  
- Guarantee: **default** of principal debtor to creditor

#### 🔹 Liability character

- Indemnity: **reimbursement** orientation  
- Guarantee: **secondary** (surety) obligation to creditor (with nuances in bank instruments)

#### 🔹 Contractual overlap

Documents may label clauses vaguely—courts look to **substance**.
