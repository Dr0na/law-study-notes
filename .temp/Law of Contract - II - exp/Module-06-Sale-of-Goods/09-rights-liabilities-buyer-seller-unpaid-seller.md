## 📘 Rights and Liabilities of Buyer and Seller; Unpaid Seller

🎚️ Difficulty: 🔴 Hard

**Statute:** Sections 45–52, Sale of Goods Act, 1930

---

### 🧠 Introduction

**Unpaid seller** defined in **Section 45** (seller not paid, negotiable instrument received dishonoured, etc.—quote). Rights: **lien** (**47**), **stoppage in transit** (**50–52**), **resale** (**48**).

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Lien

Possession retained until payment—**part delivery** nuances.

#### 🔹 Stoppage in transit

Buyer **insolvent**, goods **in transit**, seller exercises **stoppage in transit**.

#### 🔹 Resale

Power to **resell** under **48** subject to conditions.

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

Problem: carrier hands goods to buyer—**transit ends** → stoppion impossible.

---

### ⚖️ Case Laws

**Sims v. Marryat** (transit—English anchor).

---

### 🧾 Conclusion

Unpaid seller rights are **strong possessory remedies**.
