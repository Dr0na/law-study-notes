## 📘 Auction Sales

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟢 Easy

**Statute:** Section 64, Sale of Goods Act, 1930

---

### 🧠 Introduction

**Auction sale** rules govern **offer/acceptance** direction: **bid is offer**, **fall of hammer is acceptance** (Section 64—read exact wording). **Reserve price**, **pretend bidding (puff) prohibition**, **seller’s right to bid** only if **expressly reserved** (section conditions—memorise).

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Completion

Hammer; **conditional** hammer if auctioneer announces.

#### 🔹 Reserve

Auctioneer may **withdraw** if no bid meets reserve (per rules/custom).
