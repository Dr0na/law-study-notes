## 📘 Transfer of Title, Nemo Dat, and Passing of Risk

🎚️ Difficulty: 🔴 Hard

**Statute:** Sections 18–30, Sale of Goods Act, 1930

---

### 🧠 Introduction

**Nemo dat quod non habet**: seller cannot give **better title** than he has (**Section 27** general rule) with **exceptions** (**Sections 27–30** cluster: **mercantile agent**, **voidable title**, **seller in possession**, **buyer in possession**—memorise headings).

**Risk** generally follows **property** unless agreed (**Section 26**).

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Passing of property rules

**Specific goods**: intention (**Section 19**), unconditional appropriation (**Section 20**), **delivery** states (**Section 21**).  
**Unascertained**: rules under **Section 23** etc.

#### 🔹 Exceptions to nemo dat

Examiners love **table** of four statutory exceptions with **facts**.
