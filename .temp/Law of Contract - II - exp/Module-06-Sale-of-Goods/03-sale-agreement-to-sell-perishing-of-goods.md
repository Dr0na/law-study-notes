## 📘 Sale as Transfer of Property; Sale and Agreement to Sell; Perishing of Goods

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Sections 4(3), 6–8, 20–23, Sale of Goods Act, 1930

---

### 🧠 Introduction

**Sale** vs **agreement to sell** affects **remedies** (property passes in sale; breach of agreement to sell may be **damages** or **specific goods** rules). **Perishing** before contract (**Section 7**) vs **after agreement to sell but before risk passes** (**Section 8**)—memorise outcomes.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Agreement to sell becomes sale

When **conditions fulfilled** / **time elapses**, an **agreement to sell** becomes a **sale** (**Section 4(3)**, second part—quote your bare Act).

#### 🔹 Specific goods perished without seller knowledge (Section 7)

Contract **void** where specific goods **perished** before contract and seller **unaware**.

#### 🔹 Agreement to sell specific goods (Section 8)

If goods perish **without fault** before risk passes, agreement **void**.
