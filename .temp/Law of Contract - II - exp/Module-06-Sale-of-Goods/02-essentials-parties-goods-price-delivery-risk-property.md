## 📘 Essentials of Contract of Sale: Parties, Goods, Price, Delivery, Risk, and Property

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Sections 2(1), 2(7), 2(10), 26–39, Sale of Goods Act, 1930

---

### 🧠 Introduction

Essentials: **two parties** (buyer/seller), **goods**, **price** (money), **transfer of property** intention, **delivery** mechanism, and rules on **risk** following **property** unless otherwise agreed (**Section 26** default—verify).

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Parties

Seller transfers; buyer pays.

#### 🔹 Goods

Must be **movable** and identified per contract.

#### 🔹 Price

Ascertainable; **fixed** by parties or mechanism (**Section 10** valuation third party—if applicable).

#### 🔹 Delivery and acceptance

**Sections 33–39** cluster—modes and seller duties.

#### 🔹 Risk vs property

Generally **risk prima facie passes with property** (**Section 26**—confirm wording in bare Act).

---
