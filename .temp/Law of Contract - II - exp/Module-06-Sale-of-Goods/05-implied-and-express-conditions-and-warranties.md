## 📘 Implied and Express Conditions and Warranties

🎚️ Difficulty: 🔴 Hard

**Statute:** Sections 11–17, Sale of Goods Act, 1930

---

### 🧠 Introduction

**Condition** is a stipulation **essential** to the main purpose; **warranty** is **collateral**. Breach of **condition** may allow **repudiation** + damages; breach of **warranty** generally **damages only** unless **treated as breach of warranty** under **Section 13** election—learn **exact** scheme.

**Implied conditions** include **title** (**Section 14**), **sale by description** (**Section 15**), and **quality / fitness / merchantability** rules under **Section 16** (read **sub-clauses** in your bare Act carefully—exam answers must track the exact subsection used).

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Section 12 definitions

Condition vs warranty.

#### 🔹 Section 14

**Title**: right to sell, quiet possession.

#### 🔹 Sections 15–16

**Description** (**15**), **quality / fitness / merchantability** (**16**)—including **buyer’s reliance** on seller’s skill for **16(1)** where applicable.

#### 🔹 Express terms

Parties may elevate warranty to condition or vice versa **clearly**.
