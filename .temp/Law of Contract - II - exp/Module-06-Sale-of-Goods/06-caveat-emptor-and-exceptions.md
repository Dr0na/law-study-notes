## 📘 Caveat Emptor and Its Exceptions

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Section 16, Sale of Goods Act, 1930 (and related), **Section 16** title in Indian Act is fitness/quality—**caveat emptor** is **Section 16** opening doctrine historically taught as buyer beware with **statutory exceptions**.

---

### 🧠 Introduction

**Caveat emptor** means **buyer beware**—buyer must **judge quality** unless law implies protections. Under SoGA, exceptions dominate modern consumer reality: **title (14)**, **description (15)**, **fitness (16(1))**, **merchantable quality (16(2))**, **sale by sample (17)**, **fraud**, **usage of trade**.
