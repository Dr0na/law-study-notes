## 📘 Definition of a Contract of Sale of Goods

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟢 Easy

**Statute:** Section 4(1), Sale of Goods Act, 1930

---

### 🧠 Introduction

A **contract of sale** is a contract whereby the **seller transfers or agrees to transfer** the **property in goods** to the buyer for a **price** (Section 4(1) SoGA). It may be **absolute** or **conditional**.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Transfer vs agreement to transfer

- **Sale**: property passes **immediately** (or as per parties)  
- **Agreement to sell**: transfer is **future** / condition-dependent (**Section 4(3)**)

#### 🔹 Price

Distinguish **barter** (not sale—no money price).

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

Link to **goods** (Section 2(7)) and **price** (Section 2(10)).
