## 📘 Examples: Contracts of Indemnity in Practice

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟢 Easy

**Statute:** Sections 124–125, Indian Contract Act, 1872 (applied to facts)

---

### 🧠 Introduction

Syllabus lists **illustrative indemnities**: **duplicate share certificate**, **bank deposit receipt**, **owner-to-authority for commencement certificate**, **publisher–printer clause**, **seller’s title assurance in sale deed**. These examples show **risk** (forgery, error, regulatory liability, defamation/infringement, title defect) shifting to an **indemnifier**.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Duplicate share certificate / bank receipt

Company/bank may issue duplicate instrument only if applicant gives **indemnity bond**—if a **fraudulent** duplicate harms a genuine holder, indemnity supports **recourse**.

#### 🔹 Landowner and local authority (commencement certificate)

Authority demands indemnity so that if **third-party claims** arise from construction commencement, **costs/losses** are recoverable from indemnifier.

#### 🔹 Publisher and printer

Publisher indemnifies printer (or vice versa depending on drafting) for **IP infringement claims** arising from manuscript/artwork—**contractual scope** is king.

#### 🔹 Sale deed title assurance

Seller gives **covenant/indemnity** for **quiet enjoyment** / **defect in title**—buyer’s remedy chain often mixes **warranty** language with **indemnity**.

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

1. Each example must map to **loss** + **identified risk**.  
2. **Drafting** determines whether it is strict **S.124** indemnity or broader commercial promise.  
3. **Public authority** indemnities often **standard forms**.
