## 📘 Formation and Essential Features of Contract of Indemnity

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Sections 124–125, Indian Contract Act, 1872 (+ general Sections 10–11)

---

### 🧠 Introduction

Formation of indemnity follows **general contract law**: **agreement**, **lawful consideration**, **lawful object**, **competent parties**, and **free consent**. “Essential features” in exams mean: **promise to save from loss** (Section 124), identifiable **risk/event**, and (practically) **clear words** on **scope**, **claims procedure**, and **limits**.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Agreement

Offer + acceptance can be **express** (deed, clause) or implied in fact (rare; usually written for proof).

#### 🔹 Consideration

Consideration may be **forbearance**, **payment of premium-like sum**, **main contract** being entered (composite transactions), or **any lawful quid pro quo** under general law.

#### 🔹 Lawful object and public policy

Indemnity for **fraud**, **crime**, or **injury to person/property** contrary to law is void under general principles.

#### 🔹 Essential features checklist

1. **Parties** identifiable  
2. **Promise to save from loss** within Section 124 (if claiming as “contract of indemnity”)  
3. **Causal link** between loss and covered event  
4. **Certainty** — vague clauses may fail for **uncertainty** (Section 29 context)

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

1. **Special contract + general essentials** — always write both layers.  
2. **Written form** — not mandatory under Act, but **evidence-strong**.  
3. **Composite transactions** — indemnity may be **ancillary** to lease, sale, banking arrangement.

### 🧾 Conclusion

Formation merges **general contract essentials** with the **specific promise** in Section 124. Strong answers add **drafting** points: **scope, caps, notice, mitigation**.

#### 🎯 10 Mark — “Essential features of contract of indemnity.”

##### 🧾 Answer Synopsis

- General law essentials.  
- Section 124 **specific** promise.  
- Practical **certainty** and **lawful object**.  
- Example: **ancillary** indemnity in sale deed.

##### 🧠 Introduction

Every indemnity, like any contract, must satisfy the **Indian Contract Act** requirements of valid agreements. Additionally, it must match the **indemnity definition** to attract **Section 125** rights.

##### 📖 Body

Discuss **parties**, **consent**, **consideration**, **capacity**, **lawful object**. Then isolate **promise to save from loss** and **third-party/promisor conduct** channel. Mention **writing** as proof strategy.

##### ⚖️ Case Laws Section

**Gajanan Moreshwar** — one paragraph linking interpretation to **commercial indemnity**.

##### 🧾 Conclusion

Essential features are **dual-layered**: **general contract validity** plus the **statutory indemnity promise** under **Section 124**.
