## 📘 Nature and Extent of Liability of the Indemnifier

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Section 125, Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

**Section 125** states the **rights of the indemnity-holder** when the indemnity contract covers certain kinds of **liability**: (i) liability **discharged** by payment; (ii) liability **incurred** but not yet discharged (compromise, etc., as per section text). The indemnifier’s liability is therefore **linked to measurable consequences**—**damages**, **costs**, and **sums paid under compromise** where applicable.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Nature of liability

The indemnifier’s duty is **to indemnify**, i.e., to **place** the indemnity-holder in the position **as if the loss had not occurred** (subject to contract limits). It is **not a guarantee of another’s debt** in the Section 126 sense.

#### 🔹 Extent: Section 125 buckets

Study the **exact wording** of Section 125 in the bare Act. Typical exam structure:

- **Damages** compelled to pay in a suit/covered claim  
- **Costs** of litigation/bringing/defending as covered  
- **Sums under compromise** if indemnity covers that scenario (as per section)

#### 🔹 Contract can widen or narrow

Parties may cap liability (**ceiling**), exclude consequential loss (**if lawful**), or require **notice** and **cooperation**. Public policy and **clear wording** govern.

#### 🔹 Mitigation

Courts expect **reasonable mitigation** of loss unless contract says otherwise—practical point in problem questions.

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

1. **Measure**: usually **actual loss** + **covered costs**.  
2. **No guarantee confusion** — different sections and remedy structure.  
3. **Compromise** — must fall within indemnity wording + Section 125.  
4. **Contribution** — not same as **surety’s** rights (different chapter).

Nature/extent questions are **Section 125–centric**: translate the indemnity-holder’s **compelled payments and reasonable litigation costs** into the indemnifier’s obligation, **subject to the contract** and **commencement** rules.

##### 🧠 Introduction

The indemnifier’s liability is the **flip side** of the indemnity-holder’s **right to reimbursement** for covered losses. Section 125 enumerates key **entitlements** that define **extent** in statutory indemnities.

Extent is primarily **Section 125**, refined by **contract**; nature is **indemnificatory**, not **suretyship** unless parties also create guarantee.
