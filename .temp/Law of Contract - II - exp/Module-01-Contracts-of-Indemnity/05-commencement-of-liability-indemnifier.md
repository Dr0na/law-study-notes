## 📘 Commencement of Liability of the Indemnifier

🎚️ Difficulty: 🔴 Hard

**Statute:** Sections 124–125, Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

A high-scoring debate is **when** the indemnifier must pay: only after the indemnity-holder **actually pays** the third party, or **earlier** (for example, when liability becomes **absolute**)? English common law historically stressed **actual payment** in some lines; Indian courts moved towards allowing **earlier** relief in suitable cases—classically discussed in **Gajanan Moreshwar**.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 English narrow view (historical context)

Older English position: indemnity-holder must **first discharge** the liability out of pocket before suing indemnifier. This could cause **cash-flow injustice**.

#### 🔹 Indian position (exam core)

In **Gajanan Moreshwar Parelkar v. Moreshwar Madan Mantri**, the court allowed indemnity-holder to **compel indemnifier** to **place funds** to meet the liability **even before actual payment** in appropriate circumstances—read the **exact holding** in your textbook/bare Act commentary.

Practical framing for answers:

- If indemnity-holder has a **clear, imminent** obligation and **would be forced to pay**, equity supports **advance indemnification** where contract/statute permits.
- If liability is **speculative**, courts hesitate.

#### 🔹 Section 125 alignment

Map commencement discussion to **Section 125** limbs: some losses are **paid**, some are **incurred**—examiners reward statutory tie-in.

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

1. **Actual loss vs imminent/certain liability** — central tension.  
2. **Contract wording** may fix **payment triggers** (on demand, on judgment, etc.).  
3. **Mitigation and notice** may affect commencement factually.

---

|------|-----------|

Commencement is **not purely mechanical**; it blends **Section 125** with **equitable** development in **Gajanan Moreshwar**. Exam answers should **contrast older English view** with **Indian approach**, then conclude with **contractual triggers**.
