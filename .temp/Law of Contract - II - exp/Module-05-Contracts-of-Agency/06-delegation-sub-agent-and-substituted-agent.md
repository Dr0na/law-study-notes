## 📘 Delegation: Sub-agent, Substituted Agent, and Principal’s Relations

🎚️ Difficulty: 🔴 Hard

**Statute:** Sections 190–195, Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

General rule: **non delegable** unless **custom**, **principal consent**, or **nature of work** permits (**Section 190**). **Sub-agent** works under agent; **substituted agent** (Section 194 naming) is person **appointed by agent** at principal’s direction—**liability pattern differs**.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Sub-agent (191–193)

When valid, **privity** issues: principal may be bound under **192** conditions; agent remains **responsible** for sub-agent defaults under **193** (read bare Act).

#### 🔹 Substituted agent (194–195)

Where principal **allows** agent to name another person, that person is **substituted**—agent’s liability drops if **due diligence** in selection (Section 195—verify).

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

Problem: “A appoints B; B appoints C without consent” → usually **breach** + **no binding** principal (exceptions narrow).
