## 📘 Agent's Authority: Express, Implied, Ostensible, Emergency, and Restrictions

🎚️ Difficulty: 🔴 Hard

**Statute:** Sections 186–188, 190–191, 204–210, Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

**Express authority** is explicitly stated. **Implied authority** is necessary to effectuate express authority and usual course of business (**Section 187**—read exact wording). **Ostensible/apparent authority** arises when the **principal’s conduct** leads a third party reasonably to believe the agent has authority (**holding out / estoppel**). Map this accurately to the **Sections 235–238** cluster (and related provisions) in your **bare Act** edition.

**Emergency authority** under **Section 189** (agency of necessity) overlaps; also **Section 189** read with **agent’s duty** to contact principal where possible.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Implied authority (187)

Includes **incidental** acts, **usual** trade practices.

#### 🔹 Ostensible authority

Principal **clothed** agent with appearance of authority—third party **good faith** + **reasonable belief**.

#### 🔹 Secret restrictions

Internal memo limiting agent does **not** bind third party if **ostensible** authority exists.

#### 🔹 Emergency

Protect principal’s property/interest where communication impossible—**bona fide** action.
