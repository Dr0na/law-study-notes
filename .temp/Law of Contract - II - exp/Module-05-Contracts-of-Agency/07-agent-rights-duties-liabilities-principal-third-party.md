## 📘 Agent: Rights, Duties, Immunities, Disabilities, and Liabilities (Principal and Third Party)

🎚️ Difficulty: 🔴 Hard

**Statute:** Sections 211–225, 226–238 (selected), Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

**Duties:** skill/care (**212**), obey lawful instructions, **accounts** (**213**), **communicate** (**214**), **not delegate** unlawfully, **not secret profit** (**215–216** cluster).

**Rights:** **remuneration** (**219–220**), **lien** (**221**), **indemnity** (**222–223**), **compensation** for injury (**225**).

**Towards third party:** agent signs contract—**disclosed principal** rules vs **undisclosed** (later file). **Personal liability** if agent **breaches warranty of authority**.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Secret profits

Principal may **repudiate** and **claim** gains.

#### 🔹 Misconduct

Agent loses remuneration (**220**).

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

Use **issue spotting**: secret profit, competing business, bribery.

---

### ⚖️ Case Laws

**Armstrong v. Jackson** (warranty of authority—English anchor).

---

### 🧾 Conclusion

Agent is **fiduciary-light** in many commercial settings: **loyalty + disclosure** dominate.
