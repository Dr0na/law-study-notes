## 📘 Principal: Rights, Duties, Immunities, Disabilities, and Liabilities (Agent and Third Party)

🎚️ Difficulty: 🔴 Hard

**Statute:** Sections 211, 215–216, 223–225, 226–238, Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

**Principal’s rights:** enforce instructions, **accounts**, **ratify/refuse** unauthorised acts, **sue** for secret profits.

**Principal’s duties:** pay **remuneration**, **indemnify** agent for lawful acts (**222**), compensate wrongful revocation (**211**).

**Towards third party:** principal **bound** by agent within **actual/ostensible** authority; **disclosed vs undisclosed** rules affect **suit** parties.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Indemnity

Agent acting within authority entitled to indemnity for **lawful** acts and **liabilities** incurred.

#### 🔹 Wrongful revocation

**Section 211** compensation when principal revokes contrary to contract.

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

Balance **control** with **indemnity**.

---

### ⚖️ Case Laws

**Syed Abdul Khader v. Rami Reddy** — indemnity—if taught.

---

### 🧾 Conclusion

Principal cannot **revoke arbitrarily** if agency is **for a fixed period** with **interest** (irrevocable topic links).
