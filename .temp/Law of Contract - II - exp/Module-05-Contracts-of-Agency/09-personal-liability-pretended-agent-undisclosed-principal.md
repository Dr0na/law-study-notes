## 📘 Personal Liability of Agent, Pretended Agent, and Undisclosed Principal

🎚️ Difficulty: 🔴 Hard

**Statute:** Sections 230–238, Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

**Pretended agent** has **no authority**—liable for **breach of warranty of authority** to third party who relied.

**Undisclosed principal** can **sue and be sued** on contract made by agent within authority though principal’s name hidden—subject to **third party’s election** and **personal liability** of agent in some situations.

**Disclosed principal**—agent generally **not personally bound** if contracts “as agent”.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Warranty of authority

Agent impliedly warrants **authority exists**; falsity → **damages**.

#### 🔹 Undisclosed principal doctrine

Principal may **intervene** unless **contract excludes** intervention or third party shows **intention** to deal only with agent (Sections **230–231** cluster—read bare Act carefully).

#### 🔹 Agent personal liability

If **agent signs without disclosing** principal, may be personally liable depending on **words** and **custom**.

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

Problem: invoice header, signature block, trade custom.

---

### ⚖️ Case Laws

**Humble v. Hunter**; **Fred Drughorn Ltd v. Rederiaktiebolaget Transatlantic** (English)—often cited in Indian textbooks.

### 🧾 Conclusion

This cluster separates **true agency** from **personal** contracting.
