## 📘 Essential Features, Parties, and Kinds of Agents and Agencies

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Sections 182–186, Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

Essentials: **principal competent** (generally), **consideration not always mandatory** in agency (Section 185—gratuitous agent may exist), **authority** (express/implied), and **control** element varying by kind.

**Kinds:** general vs special agent; universal agent (rare); **sub-agent** vs **substituted agent** (later topic); **del credere** agent (undertakes extra credit risk for extra commission—locate exact section and definition in your **bare Act / textbook**).

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Parties

**Principal** and **agent**; third party appears in external relations.

#### 🔹 Kinds (exam classification)

- **General agent** — broad course of dealing authority  
- **Special agent** — single transaction limited  
- **Mercantile agent** (SoGA/ICA cross-refs where taught)

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

**Sections 211–214** set out core **duties** of the agent (conduct of business, accounts, communication). **Sections 186–189** map **authority** (express/implied/extent/emergency)—often examined together with creation of agency.
