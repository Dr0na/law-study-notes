## 📘 Creation of Agency

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Sections **182–185** (relationship basics), **189** (emergency), **196–201** (ratification), Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

Agency may arise by **express appointment**, **implication** (conduct/circumstances), **necessity** (Section 189), **ratification** (Sections 196–200 cluster), or **estoppel** (holding out—linked to apparent authority).

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Express

Deed, resolution, power of attorney.

#### 🔹 Implied

From **relationship** or **course of dealing**.

#### 🔹 Agency of necessity

**Section 189**: circumstances force agent to act to prevent loss to principal—strict conditions.

#### 🔹 Ratification

Principal adopts unauthorised act—**retrospective** agency if conditions satisfied (competence, knowledge material facts, whole transaction).

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

Ratification requires **affirmation** with free consent and within **time** (implied).

---

### ⚖️ Case Laws

**Bolton Partners v. Lambert** (English ratification timing)—often compared in notes.

---

### 🧾 Conclusion

Creation modes are **PYQ grid**: express/implied/necessity/ratification/estoppel.
