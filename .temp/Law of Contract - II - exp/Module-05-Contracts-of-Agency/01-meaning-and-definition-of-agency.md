## 📘 Meaning and Definition of Contract of Agency

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟢 Easy

**Statute:** Section 182, Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

**Agency** is the relationship where one person (**agent**) is authorised to **act for** or **represent** another (**principal**) in dealings with third persons. Section **182** defines **agent** and **principal**.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Core idea

**Representation** + **authority** + **legal effects on principal** (generally).

#### 🔹 Not every helper is agent

Distinguish **servant** (employer liable for servant in master-servant tort contexts) and **independent contractor** (topic 4).

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

- **Consent** (express/implied) typically underlies agency.  
- **Minors** cannot be principals in many situations—follow textbook.

---

### ⚖️ Case Laws

**State of Rajasthan v. Firm Ram Bilas** — agency identification—if in notes.

### 🧾 Conclusion

Definition is **182**; analysis always moves next to **authority** (topic 5).
