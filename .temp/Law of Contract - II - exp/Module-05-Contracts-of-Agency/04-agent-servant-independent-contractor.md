## 📘 Distinction Between Agent, Servant (Employee), and Independent Contractor

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Contract Act agency chapters + **law of torts** (vicarious liability) cross-link

---

### 🧠 Introduction

**Agent** binds **principal contractually** within authority. **Servant** acts under **employer’s control** as to **manner** of work; employer may be **vicariously liable** for torts in course of employment. **Independent contractor** controls **method**; principal generally **not liable** for contractor’s torts (exceptions: **non-delegable** duties, **hazardous** acts—torts syllabus).

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Control test

Servant: **detailed control**. Contractor: **result** oriented. Agent: **representational** authority even if not supervised minutely.

#### 🔹 Contractual vs tort lens

Same person may be **agent for some acts** and **servant for others** (salesrep example).
