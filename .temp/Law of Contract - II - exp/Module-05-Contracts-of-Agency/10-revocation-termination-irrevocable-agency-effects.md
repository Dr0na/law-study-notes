## 📘 Revocation, Termination of Agency, Irrevocable Agency, and Effects

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Sections **202–210**, **203–208**, Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

Agency terminates by **revocation** (principal), **renunciation** (agent), **completion**, **death/insanity** (**204–205**), **mutual agreement**, or **destruction** of subject matter (general law).

**Irrevocable agency:** where **agent has interest** in agency/exercise of authority, principal **cannot revoke** to prejudice (**Section 202**—read exact wording in bare Act).

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Revocation (209–210)

Principal may revoke **unless** agency coupled with interest or **breach** of contract.

#### 🔹 Effect of termination (207–208)

When termination takes **effect** for third parties—**notice** crucial.

#### 🔹 Agent’s duties post-termination (206, 208)

Preserve goods, account, **good faith** wind-down.

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

**Section 202** is the **favourite trap**: agency **coupled with interest**.
