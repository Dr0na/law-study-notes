## 📘 Rights, Liabilities, Duties, and Disabilities of the Pawnor

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Sections 173–177, Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

**Pawnor’s** core duty is to **redeem** by paying debt; **disclose** defects affecting security value; not to **defraud** pawnee. Rights include **redemption** before sale (subject to conditions), and **notice** protections under **Section 176** procedure.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Duties

- Pay debt/perform promise  
- Indemnify for **title defects** if impliedly warranted  
- Bear **expenses** as per contract/statute

#### 🔹 Rights

- **Redeem** on payment  
- **Accurate accounting** demand where commercial

#### 🔹 Disabilities

Cannot demand return **without** discharging secured obligation (subject to equity).

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

Link **redemption** with **Section 176** notice before sale.
