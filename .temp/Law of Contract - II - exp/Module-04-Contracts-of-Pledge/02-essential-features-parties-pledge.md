## 📘 Essential Features and Parties to Contract of Pledge

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Sections 172–173, Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

Parties: **pawnor** (debtor or owner pledging) and **pawnee** (creditor with possession). Essentials: **contract**, **delivery of possession**, **debt/promise** secured, **goods** as subject.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Pawnor may not be owner in some statutory pledges

Sections **178, 178A, 179** allow certain non-owners to create valid pledges in commercial settings—preview here, detail in topic 7.

#### 🔹 Pawnee’s special interest

Right to **retain** until payment; **extraordinary care** not required beyond bailee rules unless contract—usually **ordinary prudence** (178A context).

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

Map **possession + debt + goods**.
