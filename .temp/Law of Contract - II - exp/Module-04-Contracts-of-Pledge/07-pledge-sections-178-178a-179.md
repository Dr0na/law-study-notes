## 📘 Pledge by Mercantile Agent and Other Special Pledges (Sections 178, 178A, 179)

🎚️ Difficulty: 🔴 Hard

**Statute:** Sections 178, 178A, 179, Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

Ordinary rule: **owner** pledges. **Exceptions** validate pledges by **mercantile agents** in course of business (**178**), pledges in **seller’s possession after sale** (**178A**—memorise conditions), and **pledge where pawnor has** only **limited interest** (**179**).

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Section 178

Mercantile agent / **custom of trade** pledge—**owner** may be bound if **conditions** satisfied (buyer in ordinary course themes—read illustrations).

#### 🔹 Section 178A

Seller continues in possession—**pledge to** new buyer’s financier—**statutory conditions** must be exactly reproduced in exam.

#### 🔹 Section 179

Pledge by person with **limited interest**—pawnee’s rights **only to extent** of that interest.
