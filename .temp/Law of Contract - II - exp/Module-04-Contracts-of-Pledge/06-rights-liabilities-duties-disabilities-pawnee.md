## 📘 Rights, Liabilities, Duties, and Disabilities of the Pawnee

🎚️ Difficulty: 🔴 Hard

**Statute:** Sections 173–176, Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

**Pawnee’s** defining right is **retention** (Section 173) until payment; may pursue **debt** while retaining goods; on **default**, may bring **suit** while retaining pledge, or **sell** after **notice** as per **Section 176** (learn exact steps).

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Right of retainer (173)

For **principal**, **interest**, and **expenses** (as per statutory wording—memorise).

#### 🔹 Care duty

As **bailee**—ordinary care.

#### 🔹 Sale right (176)

**Default** + **notice** + **sale procedure** fairness; wrongful sale exposes pawnee to **conversion**.

#### 🔹 Disabilities

Cannot **sell without authority** of contract/statute; cannot **use** pledged goods for own purposes.

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

Problem: pawnee sells without notice → **damages**.
