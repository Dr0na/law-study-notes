## 📘 Creation of a Contract of Pledge

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Sections 172–173, 178–179, Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

Pledge is created by **agreement + delivery of possession** to pawnee (actual/constructive). In banking, **constructive delivery** (key to godown, documents of title) is exam-heavy.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Actual vs constructive delivery

Constructive delivery through **mercantile agent**, **documents of title**, or **attornment** (third-party acknowledges holding for pawnee)—follow textbook examples.

#### 🔹 Redelivery to pawnor after pledge

If goods redelivered **in continuance of pledge** (rare valid structures) vs **pledge destroyed**—fact-sensitive.

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

- **Possession must be** with pawnee (hypothecation differs).  
- **178–179** special creation routes.

---

### ⚖️ Case Laws

| Case | Principle |
|------|-----------|
| **Sethu Madhavan Rao v. Walter** | Constructive delivery—use per source |
| **Bank of Chittoor v. P. Sundara Ram** | Pledge of goods with keys/locker control facts |

---

### 🧾 Conclusion

Creation = **contract + possession shift**; commercial pledges often turn on **constructive** delivery proof.
