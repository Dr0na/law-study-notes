## 📘 Distinction Between Pledge, Bailment, and Hypothecation

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Sections 148, 172, Indian Contract Act, 1872 + banking practice

---

### 🧠 Introduction

**Bailment** is the genus; **pledge** is a **species** (bailment for **security**). **Hypothecation** is **charge** without giving **possession** to creditor (movable charge—e.g., vehicle hypothecation), governed often by **specific statutes** and **security interest** law beyond ICA text.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Pledge vs general bailment

| Basis | General bailment | Pledge |
|------|------------------|--------|
| Purpose | Many (repair, storage) | **Security for debt** |
| Pawnee sale | Not implied | **Statutory sale** route on default (176) |

#### 🔹 Pledge vs hypothecation

| Basis | Pledge | Hypothecation |
|------|--------|----------------|
| Possession | With pawnee | Borrower retains |
| Enforcement | Traditional pledge remedies | Seize/repossess per contract/statute |

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

Examiners reward **possession test** + **remedy test**.

---

### ⚖️ Case Laws

**Reeves v. Capper** (historical)—hypothecation coinage—optional.

---

### 🧾 Conclusion

Triad answer: **bailment ⊃ pledge**; **hypothecation** is **non-possessory** security.
