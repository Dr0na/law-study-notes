## 📘 Meaning and Definition of Contract of Pledge

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟢 Easy

**Statute:** Section 172, Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

The **bailment of goods** as **security** for payment of a debt or performance of a promise is called **pledge** (Section 172). The bailor is **pawnor**; the bailee is **pawnee**. Pledge is a **special kind of bailment** with **special remedy**: **right to sell on default** after notice (Section 176).

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Essentials from definition

1. **Bailment** (possession shift)  
2. **Security purpose**  
3. **Debt or promise** secured

#### 🔹 Possession

Pawnee must have **possession** (generally); contrast **hypothecation** (topic 4).

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

- **Special property** in goods to pawnee; **general property** often remains with pawnor (examiners like this line).

---

### 🧾 Conclusion

Pledge = **bailment + security + possession in pawnee** (typically).
