## 📘 Rights, Duties, Liabilities of Bailor and Bailee; Third Parties and True Owner

🎚️ Difficulty: 🔴 Hard

**Statute:** Sections 150–164, 166–171, Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

This topic bundles **bailor’s duties** (disclosure, reimbursement—Sections 150–157 cluster), **bailee’s duties** (care, unauthorised use, mixture, return—Sections 151–160), **rights** (lien, compensation), and conflicts when a **third party** or **true owner** claims goods.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Bailor duties (high frequency)

- **Disclose faults** (150)  
- **Bear extraordinary expenses** in certain bailments (156–157—read bare Act)

#### 🔹 Bailee duties

- **Reasonable care** (151)  
- **No unauthorised use** (154)  
- **Proper mixture** rules (155)  
- **Return** (160–161)

#### 🔹 Bailee’s **particular lien** (Section 170–171)

Bailee may retain goods until **charges due** for skill/labour (particular lien rules—memorise section limits).

#### 🔹 True owner claims

If bailor had **no title**, bailee may still have **defences** depending on facts; **tort** of conversion risk—examiners use **entrustment** fact patterns.

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

Problem structure: **identify bailment type → apply care standard → check lien → check title dispute**.

Rights/duties are **statutory grids**; third-party problems add **title + conversion** layer.
