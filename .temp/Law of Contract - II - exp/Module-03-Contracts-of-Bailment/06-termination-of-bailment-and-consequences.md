## 📘 Termination of Bailment and Consequences

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Sections 153, 162–163, 180–181, Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

Bailment ends by **expiry of time**, **achievement of purpose**, **bailee’s inconsistent act** (Section 153), **death**, **mutual agreement**, or **notice** where bailment is **at will** (Section 162). Consequences: **return of goods**, liability for **wrongful retention**, and **loss of lien** in many cases.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Section 153

If bailee does **inconsistent act** with conditions, bailment is **terminable** (learn exact effect in bare Act).

#### 🔹 Gratuitous bailment at will (162)

Either party may terminate **subject to notice** and **reasonable time** for return (statutory language).

#### 🔹 Consequences

- **Return** obligations  
- **Damages** for failure  
- **Lien** may survive until lawful payment (170–171 interplay)

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

Distinguish **lawful termination** vs **conversion**.
