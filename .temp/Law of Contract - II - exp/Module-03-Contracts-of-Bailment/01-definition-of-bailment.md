## 📘 Definition of a Contract of Bailment

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟢 Easy

**Statute:** Section 148, Indian Contract Act, 1872 (also **Section 71** quasi-bailment)

---

### 🧠 Introduction

**Bailment** is the delivery of goods by one person to another for some **purpose**, upon a contract that they shall, when the purpose is accomplished, be **returned** or otherwise **disposed of** according to the directions of the person delivering them (Section 148). The person delivering is **bailor**; the recipient is **bailee**.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Triple element test

1. **Delivery of possession** (actual or constructive)  
2. **Purpose** (repair, storage, hire, loan, pledge)  
3. **Return or disposal** as agreed

#### 🔹 Possession vs custody

**Servant** holding goods for master is **not** bailee; **independent** possession for a purpose creates bailment.

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

- **Contract** basis (distinguish **gratuitous** bailment where duties still exist but differ).  
- **Goods** only (not land/money generally—money special cases).

Definition turns on **delivery + purpose + redelivery/disposal** triad under **Section 148**.
