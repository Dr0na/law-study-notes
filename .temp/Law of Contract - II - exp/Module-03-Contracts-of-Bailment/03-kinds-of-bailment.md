### 🧠 Introduction

**Classifications** (exam tables):

1. **Gratuitous vs non-gratuitous** (for reward)  
2. **For sole benefit of bailor** / **sole benefit of bailee** / **mutual benefit**  
3. **Deposit, hire, loan for use, loan for consumption** (Roman-style labels used in Indian texts)

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Duty of care link

- **Sole benefit of bailor**: bailee must take **as much care as of own goods** (Section 151 second limb—verify exact limb in bare Act)  
- **Mutual/reward bailment**: **ordinary prudence** standard (Section 151 first part)  
- **Gratuitous for bailee**: bailor must **disclose faults** (Section 150)

Always **quote correct limb** from your bare Act for full marks.
