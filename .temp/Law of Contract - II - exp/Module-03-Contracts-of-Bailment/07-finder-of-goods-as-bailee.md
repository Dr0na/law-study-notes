## 📘 Finder of Goods as a Bailee

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Section 71 (finder of goods), Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

A person who finds goods **belonging to another** and takes them into **custody** is subject to the **same responsibility** as a bailee (Section 71). Finder is a **quasi-bailee** with specific **rights** (e.g., lien for expenses—read section) and **duties** (reasonable care, effort to find owner).

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Duties of finder

- **Care** as bailee of similar benefit class (link to 151–152 teaching)  
- **Try to find true owner**  
- **Not to use** goods improperly

#### 🔹 Rights of finder

Section 71 and related parts: **retain** against owner for **necessary expenses** / **reward** if offered (follow bare Act exactly); **sale** rights in **perishable** or **low-value** contexts if statute permits—**verify illustrations** in Act.

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

Problem pattern: wallet found—**advertisement**, **police**, **conversion** if appropriated.

---

### ⚖️ Case Laws

**Armory v. Delamirie** (English—title as against world except true owner) sometimes linked in notes.

---

### 🧾 Conclusion

Finder is **bailee by law** under **Section 71**—rights/duties are **statutory**.
