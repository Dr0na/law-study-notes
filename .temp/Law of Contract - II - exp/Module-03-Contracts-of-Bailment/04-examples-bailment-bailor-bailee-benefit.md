## 📘 Examples: Bailment for Benefit of Bailor, Bailee, or Both

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟢 Easy

**Statute:** Sections 148–152, Indian Contract Act, 1872

---

### 🧠 Introduction

Syllabus contrasts bailments **for bailor’s benefit** (bailee does a favour—e.g., safekeeping neighbour’s goods), **for bailee’s benefit** (owner lends article gratuitously to borrower), and **mutual** (paid warehouse, carrier, hire).

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 For benefit of bailor only

Examples: neighbour stores your seeds in their godown **without charge**—bailee’s duty still exists but standard differs.

#### 🔹 For benefit of bailee only

Examples: **gratuitous loan of book**—bailor must disclose latent defects (Section 150).

#### 🔹 For mutual benefit

Examples: **carrier**, **dry cleaner**, **paid parking**—bailee must show **ordinary diligence** (Section 151).

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

Always attach **duty standard** to each example.
