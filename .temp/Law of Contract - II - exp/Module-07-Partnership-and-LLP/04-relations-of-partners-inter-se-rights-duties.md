## 📘 Relations of Partners Inter Se: Rights, Liabilities, Duties, Immunities, Disabilities

🎚️ Difficulty: 🔴 Hard

**Statute:** Sections 9–22, Indian Partnership Act, 1932

---

### 🧠 Introduction

Partners owe **utmost good faith** and must carry on the business to the **greatest common advantage**, be **just and faithful**, and render **true accounts** (**Section 9**, Indian Partnership Act, 1932). **Sections 12–17** cover additional inter se rules (property, capital, profits, remuneration, expulsion, etc.—use your **bare Act** headings in examinations). **Section 11** allows partners to determine rights/duties by contract and contains the **restraint of trade** exception in **sub-section (2)** (partner may agree not to carry competing business while a partner).

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Management and internal governance

Unless the partnership deed provides otherwise, partners typically share **management** and **voting** per Act/deed (see **Sections 12–18** cluster in your bare Act for **conduct of business**, **majority decisions**, and related rules).

---

Internal disputes: **breach of fiduciary** duties.
