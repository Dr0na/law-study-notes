## 📘 Registration of Firms: Procedure and Effect of Non-Registration

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Sections 56–69, Indian Partnership Act, 1932 (Chapter VII)

---

### 🧠 Introduction

Registration is **optional** but carries **disabilities** if not registered (**Section 69**): partner cannot **sue firm** or other partners for **enforcing rights arising from contract** (subject to listed exceptions—memorise).

**Procedure**: application to **Registrar**, firm name rules, etc. (**Sections 58–59**—outline only).

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Effects of registration

Evidentiary clarity; ability to **sue** for partnership rights.

#### 🔹 Section 69 disabilities

Cannot enforce certain **contractual rights** in court if unregistered—**exceptions** (suit for dissolution, realising property, set-off) must be quoted.

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

Problem: unregistered firm sues on **partnership deed**—may fail under **69**.

---

### ⚖️ Case Laws

**Hurst v. Picture Theatres** not relevant—use Indian cases on **69** from textbook.

---

### 🧾 Conclusion

Registration is **strategic** though not mandatory for existence.
