## 📘 Kinds of Partnership

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟢 Easy

**Statute:** Sections 7–8, Indian Partnership Act, 1932 (partnership at will), **special partnerships** by agreement

---

### 🧠 Introduction

**Partnership at will**: no **fixed term**, dissolvable by **written notice** under **Section 43** (partnership at will). **Particular partnership**: for **single adventure**. **Limited partnership** not classic under old IPA 1932 except **LLP** separately.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 By duration

Fixed term vs at will.

#### 🔹 By object

Trading vs professional firm (liability similar unless LLP).

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

**Deed clauses** create “kinds” commercially: **sleeping partner**, **nominal partner** (holding out risk).

---

### ⚖️ Case Laws

**Cox v. Hickman** on undisclosed principals—optional.

---

### 🧾 Conclusion

Kinds are mostly **classification for dissolution** timing.
