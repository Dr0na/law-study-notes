## 📘 Change in Constitution of Firm: Admission, Retirement, Expulsion, Death, Insolvency, Public Notice

🎚️ Difficulty: 🔴 Hard

**Statute:** Sections 29–32, 40, 72, Indian Partnership Act, 1932

---

### 🧠 Introduction

**Incoming partner** liability for pre-admission debts is limited as per **Section 31** (read bare Act). **Retirement/expulsion** require **agreement** + often **public notice** under **Section 72** to cut **holding out** liability against retired partner for post-retirement acts.

**Death** or **insolvency** triggers default dissolution rules under **Chapter VI** (see **Section 42** contingencies and related provisions in your bare Act), **unless** the partnership deed provides for **continuation** of the firm (also read **Section 35** on liability of a deceased partner’s estate where the firm continues).

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Public notice (Section 72)

Without notice, retired partner may still be liable to third parties who **deal on appearance**.

#### 🔹 Expulsion

A partner may not be expelled by a **mere majority** unless the **partnership agreement** confers power in **good faith** (**Section 33**). **Sub-sections (2)–(4) of Section 32** apply to expelled partners as they apply to retired partners (read bare Act).
