## 📘 Limited Liability Partnership (LLP): Essential Features and Distinction from Partnership

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008

---

### 🧠 Introduction

**LLP** is a **body corporate** with **separate legal personality**, **perpetual succession**, and **limited liability** of partners for debts unless **fraud/wrongful conduct** triggers personal liability. Governed by **LLP Act 2008** + **LLP Agreement**.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Distinction table (score maximiser)

| Basis | Traditional partnership | LLP |
|------|-------------------------|-----|
| Legal personality | Firm not a company-like person | **Corporate body** |
| Liability | Unlimited (generally) | **Limited** to contribution |
| Registration | Optional (Partnership Act) | **Mandatory** incorporation |
| Management | Mutual agency default | **Designated partners** + compliance |

#### 🔹 Essential features

**Separate legal entity**, **LLP agreement**, **DPIN/Designated Partners**, **audit thresholds** (tax law crossover—mention only if taught).

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

LLP partners are **not agents of each other** in same default sense as partnership—**LLP is principal**.

---

### ⚖️ Case Laws

Recent cases on **designated partner liability** for statutory defaults—use faculty list.

### 🧾 Conclusion

LLP modernises **professional services** structures while limiting **personal ruin risk**.
