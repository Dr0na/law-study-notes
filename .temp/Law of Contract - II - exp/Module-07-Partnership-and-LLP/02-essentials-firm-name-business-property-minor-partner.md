## 📘 Essentials of Partnership: Firm, Firm Name, Business, Property, Minor as Partner

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟡 Medium

**Statute:** Sections 4, 5, 14–15, 30, Indian Partnership Act, 1932

---

### 🧠 Introduction

Essentials mirror **Section 4**. **Firm name** is the **business name**; **firm property** is partnership assets held for partnership purposes (**Sections 14–15**—partners’ property vs firm property distinction).

**Minor:** Section **30** allows minor to be **admitted to benefits** only—not full partner with unlimited liability (read exact limits).

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Firm vs partners

Firm is not a **legal person** like company, but has **procedural personality** for suits under Act.

#### 🔹 Partnership property

Acquired with **firm money** or for **business**—belongs to **partnership** (subject to agreement).

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

Minor’s rights/duties limited; **no personal liability** for firm debts beyond benefits scheme.
