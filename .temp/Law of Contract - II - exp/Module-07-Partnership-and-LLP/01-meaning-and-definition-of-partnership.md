## 📘 Meaning and Definition of Partnership

🎚️ Difficulty: 🟢 Easy

**Statute:** Section 4, Indian Partnership Act, 1932

---

### 🧠 Introduction

**Partnership** is the relation between persons who have **agreed** to **share profits** of a **business** carried on by **all or any of them acting for all** (Section 4). Persons who have entered into partnership are **individually partners**; collectively a **firm**.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Four pillars (exam mnemonic)

1. **Agreement** (contract)  
2. **Business**  
3. **Profit-sharing** (motive test—not wages)  
4. **Mutual agency** (each partner principal/agent for others)

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

**Co-ownership alone** is not partnership without **business + profit motive** (**Section 7** association tests may appear).

---

### ⚖️ Case Laws

**Cox v. Hickman** — profit sharing not conclusive—if cited.

---

### 🧾 Conclusion

Definition is **Section 4**; disputes turn on **profit sharing + mutual agency**.
