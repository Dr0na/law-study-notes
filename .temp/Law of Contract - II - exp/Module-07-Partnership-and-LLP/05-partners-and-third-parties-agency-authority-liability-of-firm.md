## 📘 Partners and Third Parties: Partner as Agent of Firm, Authority, Implied Authority, Liability

🎚️ Difficulty: 🔴 Hard

**Statute:** Sections 18–19, 22–25, Indian Partnership Act, 1932

---

### 🧠 Introduction

Each partner is an **agent of the firm** for purposes of **business** (**Section 18**). **Implied authority** (**Section 19**) lists acts a partner may bind firm—**bond, acknowledge debt, submit dispute**, etc.—memorise list headings.

**Liability**: partners are **jointly and severally** liable for **acts of the firm** (**Section 25**); the **firm** is liable for a partner’s **wrongful acts/omissions** in the **ordinary course** of business (**Section 26**). Pair with **Sections 18–19** on partner as agent and **implied authority**.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Holding out (Section 28)

Person represented as partner becomes **liable as partner** to third parties.

#### 🔹 Limitations on implied authority

**Section 19(2)** prohibitions: **no submit partnership to arbitration** etc.—quote Act.

---

### 🧩 Elements / Principles

Third party must show **apparent** partnership business connection.
