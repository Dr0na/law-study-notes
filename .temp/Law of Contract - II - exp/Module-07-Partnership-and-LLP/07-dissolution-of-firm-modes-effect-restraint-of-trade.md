## 📘 Dissolution of Firm: Modes, Effect, and Agreements in Restraint of Trade

🎚️ Difficulty: 🔴 Hard

**Statute:** Sections 33–44, Indian Partnership Act, 1932

---

### 🧠 Introduction

Dissolution may be **by court** (**Section 44** grounds), **by agreement**, **compulsory** (unlawful business), **on happening of contingencies** (**Section 42**), **partnership at will** notice (**Section 43**), or **insolvency/death** subject to deed.

**Effect**: partners’ authority continues for **winding up** acts (**Section 47**—confirm).

**Restraint**: **Section 11(2)** allows **reasonable** partnership restraints during term; post-dissolution restraints judged under **Contract Act Section 27** reasonableness.

---

### 📖 Main Content

#### 🔹 Settlement of accounts (Section 48)

Goodwill, assets, losses—**order of payment**.

#### 🔹 Continuing authority by notice

Third parties until **public notice** of dissolution is given in the manner prescribed by **Section 72** (Official Gazette + vernacular newspaper; additional **Registrar** intimation for **registered** firms where applicable—read bare Act).

---

Distinguish **dissolution of firm** vs **dissolution of partnership** (same in many textbooks).
