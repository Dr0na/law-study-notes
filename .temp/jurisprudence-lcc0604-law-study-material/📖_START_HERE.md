# 📖 START HERE — Jurisprudence LCC 0604

Welcome. This pack is designed so you can move from **zero knowledge → exam-ready** in roughly **14 days** of focused work.

## 🎯 Recommended order of reading

### Days 1-2 — Foundation

1. Read [Module 01 — Introduction](01-complete-notes/Module_01_Introduction_to_Jurisprudence.md).
2. Read [Module 02 — Sources](01-complete-notes/Module_02_Sources_of_Law.md).
3. Skim the condensed sheet for [Modules 01-03](02-condensed-notes/Modules_01_to_03_Foundation_and_Sources.md) and the comparison table for [Custom vs Legislation vs Precedent](03-visual-aids/Comparison_Tables/02_Custom_vs_Legislation_vs_Precedent.md).

### Days 3-5 — Schools of jurisprudence

1. [Module 03 — Natural Law](01-complete-notes/Module_03_Natural_Law_Theories.md).
2. [Module 04 — Legal Positivism](01-complete-notes/Module_04_Legal_Positivism.md).
3. [Module 05 — Sociological / Realist / Historical / CLS](01-complete-notes/Module_05_Sociological_Realist_Historical_CLS.md).
4. Practise the flowchart for [choosing the right positivist](03-visual-aids/Flowcharts/02_Module04_Positivism_Choice_Tree.md).

### Days 6-8 — Rights, property, liability

1. [Module 06 — Person, Rights, Duties](01-complete-notes/Module_06_Person_Rights_Duties.md).
2. [Module 07 — Ownership, Possession, Title](01-complete-notes/Module_07_Ownership_Possession_Title.md).
3. [Module 08 — Obligations and Liability](01-complete-notes/Module_08_Obligations_and_Liability.md).
4. Practise the flowchart for [routing a liability question](03-visual-aids/Flowcharts/03_Module08_Liability_Route.md) and the [Hohfeld skeleton](03-visual-aids/Comparison_Tables/03_Hohfeld_Correlatives_Skeleton.md).

### Days 9-11 — Question bank drill

1. Attempt all five questions in [`05-question-bank/15_Marks/`](05-question-bank/15_Marks).
2. Attempt all five questions in [`05-question-bank/10_Marks/`](05-question-bank/10_Marks).
3. Walk through every [Distinguish Between](05-question-bank/Distinguish_Between) file.

### Days 12-13 — Most probable + cases

1. Open [Most Probable Questions](05-question-bank/Most_Probable_Questions_Sem_II_2026.md) — memorise the **Tier S** answers.
2. Read the [important cases digest](04-case-laws/Module_Wise_Cases/Important_Cases_Jurisprudence.md) twice.

### Day 14 — Mock paper

1. Attempt the [Self-Assessment Test](09-practice-worksheets/Self_Assessment_Test.md) under timed conditions.
2. Score yourself against the rubric in the same file.
3. Re-read the [Answer Writing Template](08-exam-strategy/Answer_Writing_Template.md) before the exam.

## 🧠 What to remember every day

- Topper answers always include: **definition → mnemonic list → case → table or flowchart → Indian touch → critique → conclusion**.
- Master the mnemonics in [`07-quick-revision/Doctrines_and_Maxims.md`](07-quick-revision/Doctrines_and_Maxims.md) — they are your "list-on-demand" tools.
- **Verify** every section number, year, and citation against an authoritative source.
