# ⚡ Condensed — Modules 01–03 (Foundation, Sources, Natural Law)

> Quick-recall sheet. Pair each section with its complete-note file.

## Module 01 — Introduction

- **Definitions:** Salmond (*science of first principles of civil law*), Austin (*philosophy of positive law*), Holland (*formal science of positive law*).
- **Characteristics of law (5):** General, Normative, Sanction, Recognised authority, Conduct — **GNSRC**.
- **Purposes (4):** Justice, Order, Protection, Social engineering — **JOPS**.
- **Classification:** public/private; substantive/procedural; municipal/international.
- **Law and morality:** Overlap (natural law) vs Separation (positivism); Hart–Fuller (1958); Devlin–Hart (1959).

## Module 02 — Sources

- **Four sources — CLPJ:** Custom, Legislation, Precedent, Juristic writings.
- **Custom essentials (6) — ACPRCC:** Antiquity, Continuity, Peaceable, Reasonable, Certain, Consistent. *Nec vi, nec clam, nec precario.*
- **Legislation merits (4) — CSDR:** Clear, Speedy, Democratic, Repealable.
- **Precedent:**
  - **Article 141** — SC law binding on all courts.
  - **Ratio binds, obiter persuades.**
  - **Stare decisis** = stand by decided.
  - **Per incuriam** destroys binding force.
  - **Prospective overruling** — *Golak Nath, 1967*.
- **Juristic writings** — persuasive (Salmond, Pollock, Hart).

## Module 03 — Natural law

- **Aquinas’ fourfold law — EDNH:** Eternal, Divine, Natural, Human.
- **Social contract — GHLR:** Grotius (IL), Hobbes (Leviathan, absolute sovereign), Locke (life-liberty-property, right to resist), Rousseau (general will).
- **Fuller’s 8 desiderata — GPPC-CPCC:** Generality, Publicity, Prospectivity, Clarity, Consistency, Possibility, Constancy, Congruence.
- **Hart’s minimum content** — 5 truisms about humans → minimum rules on persons, property, promises.
- **Finnis’ 7 basic goods — LKPASPR:** Life, Knowledge, Play, Aesthetic, Sociability, Practical reasonableness, Religion.
