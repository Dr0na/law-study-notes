# 📊 Custom vs Legislation vs Precedent

| 🔍 Aspect | Custom | Legislation | Precedent |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Origin** | Community practice over time | Deliberate enactment by sovereign | Judicial pronouncement in a decided case |
| **Form** | Unwritten (until codified) | Written and promulgated | Found in law reports |
| **Speed** | Slow, organic | Fast, can keep up with change | Incremental, case-by-case |
| **Authority** | Ancient and accepted | Constitutional competence | Higher-court ratio (Article 141) |
| **Validity test** | 6 essentials — ACPRCC | Constitutional + procedural | Material facts + ratio decidendi |
| **Flexibility** | Hard to change | Easy to repeal/amend | Adapts through distinguishing/overruling |
| **Indian example** | Hindu / Muslim personal laws | Indian Penal Code, 1860 | *Maneka Gandhi v UoI* (1978) |
| **Limitations** | Must be reasonable, not against statute | Subject to constitutional review | Can be *per incuriam*, overruled, distinguished |
| **Best supports** | Local, cultural matters | Economy-wide, mass conduct | Filling statutory gaps, hard cases |

> 🧠 Use this table whenever the question asks **"compare the sources of law"** or **"which is the most important source today?"** Conclude with **legislation primary, precedent dominant in courts, custom residual, juristic writings persuasive.**
