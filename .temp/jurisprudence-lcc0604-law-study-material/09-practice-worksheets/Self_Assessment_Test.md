# 📝 Self-Assessment Test — Jurisprudence (LCC 0604)

> Time limit: **3 hours.** Total marks: **80.** Attempt **any five** questions from Section A and **any two** from Section B.

## Section A — Short and Medium (5 × 10 = 50)

1. Explain the relationship between law and morality with reference to Hart-Fuller debate.
2. Discuss the essentials of a valid custom and its place under Article 13(3)(a) of the Constitution.
3. State and illustrate Hart’s rule of recognition.
4. Examine Kelsen’s pure theory and the role of the Grundnorm.
5. Discuss Roscoe Pound’s theory of social engineering with one Indian illustration.
6. Distinguish ratio decidendi from obiter dicta with examples.

## Section B — Long answers (2 × 15 = 30)

1. Discuss in detail the sources of law and assess their relative importance in the Indian legal system.
2. Compare Austin, Hart, and Kelsen as legal positivists.
3. Critically examine American Realism with reference to Holmes, Llewellyn, and Frank.
4. Discuss the nature, characteristics, and Hohfeldian classification of legal rights.

## Self-scoring rubric

| Band | Marks per question (10) | Indicators |
|:---|:---:|:---|
| Excellent | 8.5–10 | All sub-heads + cases + Indian touch + criticism + clean structure |
| Good | 7–8.5 | Most sub-heads, at least 1 case, structured answer |
| Average | 5–7 | Definitions plus a couple of points; no case / no criticism |
| Needs work | <5 | Vague or off-topic |

## Reflection prompts (after attempting)

1. Which **two modules** did you under-cover? Plan a 30-minute revision tomorrow.
2. Did you use at least **two mnemonics** per answer?
3. Did you cite **Article 141** in the precedent answer?
4. Did the 15-mark answers contain a **table or flowchart**?
5. Identify **three mistakes** to fix in the next attempt.
