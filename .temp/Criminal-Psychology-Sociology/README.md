## Important Topics & Key Theories

### Must-Know Theories:

**Classical School:**
- Beccaria, Bentham
- Free will, deterrence, rational choice

**Positivist School:**
- Lombroso (biological)
- Ferri (social)
- Garofalo (psychological)

**Merton's Strain:**
- 5 modes: Conformity, Innovation, Ritualism, Retreatism, Rebellion
- Cultural goals vs. institutional means

**Differential Association (Sutherland):**
- 9 propositions
- Crime learned through interaction
- Excess of definitions favorable to crime

**Labelling Theory (Becker):**
- Primary vs. secondary deviance
- Master status
- Society creates criminals

**Social Control (Hirschi):**
- 4 bonds: Attachment, Commitment, Involvement, Belief (ACIB)

**Subcultural:**
- Cohen: Status frustration
- Miller: 6 focal concerns (TTSEFA)

## Key Mnemonics

**Schools:** "CNP" - Classical, Neo-Classical, Positivist
**Positivists:** "LFG" - Lombroso, Ferri, Garofalo
**5 Modes:** "CIRR-R" - Conformity, Innovation, Ritualism, Retreatism, Rebellion
**4 Bonds:** "ACIB" - Attachment, Commitment, Involvement, Belief
**Focal Concerns:** "TTSEFA" - Trouble, Toughness, Smartness, Excitement, Fate, Autonomy
**Neutralization:** "DDCAL" - Denial Responsibility, Denial Injury, Condemnation, Appeal, (Denial of) Victim
**Approaches:** "PPB" - Psychological, Psycho-analytical, Behaviourist
