# 📝 Question: Define Criminology and explain its scope. (5 Marks)

---

# ✍️ Answer:

## 🎯 Definition

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

**Criminology** is the scientific study of crime, criminals, criminal behavior, and the criminal justice system.

**Etymology:** "Crimen" (Latin - crime) + "Logos" (Greek - study) = Study of crime

</div>

---

## 📋 Scope of Criminology (6 Areas)

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

**1. Nature and Extent of Crime:** What constitutes crime, crime statistics, patterns

**2. Causation of Crime:** Why people commit crimes - biological, psychological, social factors

**3. Criminal Behavior:** Psychology of criminals, typology of offenders

**4. Victimology:** Study of victims - who becomes victim, impact of crime

**5. Criminal Justice System:** Police, courts, prisons - how system responds to crime

**6. Crime Prevention and Control:** Strategies to reduce crime, rehabilitation methods

</div>

---

## 🎯 Conclusion

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

Criminology is multidisciplinary science drawing from sociology, psychology, law, biology, and statistics to understand crime comprehensively. Its scope extends from studying why crime occurs to how society should respond to it.

</div>
