# 📝 Question: Discuss the relationship between human aggression, violence, and crime. (5 Marks)

---

# ✍️ Answer:

## 🎯 Definitions

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

**Aggression:** Behavior intended to harm another person who is motivated to avoid that harm

**Violence:** Physical aggression resulting in injury or death

**Crime:** Illegal acts punishable by law

</div>

---

## 🔗 Relationship

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

Not all aggression is criminal (sports), not all violence is crime (self-defense), but violent crime represents intersection of aggression, violence, and law violation.

### 3 Types of Aggression:

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

**1. Instrumental Aggression:** Goal-directed (robbery for money)

**2. Hostile/Reactive Aggression:** Emotional response to provocation (assault in anger)

**3. Relational Aggression:** Harm through relationships (bullying, social exclusion)

</div>

</div>

---

## 🧠 Causes of Aggression

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

**Biological:** Genetics, brain structure (amygdala), hormones (testosterone), neurotransmitters (serotonin)

**Psychological:** Frustration-aggression hypothesis, hostile attribution bias, poor anger control

**Social:** Learning through observation (Bandura), cultural norms valuing aggression, media violence exposure

</div>

---

## 🎯 Conclusion

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

Aggression and violence are complex behaviors resulting from biological predispositions, psychological processes, and social learning. Understanding these mechanisms helps develop interventions - anger management, cognitive therapy, social skills training - to reduce violent crime.

</div>
