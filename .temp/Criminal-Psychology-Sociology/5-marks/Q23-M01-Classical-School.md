# 📝 Question: Write a short note on the Classical School of Criminology. (5 Marks)

---

# ✍️ Answer:

## 🎯 Introduction

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

Classical School emerged in 18th century as reaction against arbitrary, cruel punishments of pre-classical era. Founded by Cesare Beccaria and Jeremy Bentham, it revolutionized criminal justice.

</div>

---

## 👨‍⚖️ Key Contributors

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

**Cesare Beccaria (1738-1794):**

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">
"On Crimes and Punishments" (1764) - punishment should be proportionate, certain, swift - prevention better than punishment
</div>

**Jeremy Bentham (1748-1832):**

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">
Utilitarian approach - "greatest happiness for greatest number" - hedonistic calculus (pleasure vs. pain)
</div>

</div>

---

## 🔑 Core Principles

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

**1. Free Will:** Humans have rational choice - choose to commit crimes

**2. Deterrence:** Swift, certain, proportionate punishment deters crime

**3. Equality:** All equal before law - same crime = same punishment

**4. Social Contract:** State has right to punish law violators

**5. Codified Laws:** Crimes and punishments defined by written laws

</div>

---

## ✅ Impact

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

- Abolished torture and arbitrary punishments
- Codification of criminal laws
- Proportionate sentencing
- Presumption of innocence
- Fair trial rights
- Foundation for modern criminal law

</div>

---

## 🎯 Conclusion

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

Classical School's emphasis on rationality, deterrence, and proportionality transformed criminal justice from arbitrary brutality to systematic, humane legal framework. Its principles continue to underlie modern criminal law worldwide.

</div>
