# 📝 Question: Write a short note on Enrico Ferri's contribution to criminology. (5 Marks)

---

# ✍️ Answer:

## 🎯 Introduction

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

Enrico Ferri (1856-1929) was Italian criminologist who expanded criminology beyond Lombroso's biological focus to include social and environmental factors. He developed sociological positivism.

</div>

---

## 📖 Key Contributions

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

### Multiple Causation Theory:

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

Crime has **3 types of causes:**

**1. Anthropological (Individual):** Age, sex, psychology, race, biological constitution

**2. Physical (Geographic):** Climate, temperature, geographic location, seasonal variations

**3. Social:** Poverty, unemployment, education, family structure, population density, religion, customs **(Most Important)**

</div>

### Law of Criminal Saturation:

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

Every society has "criminal saturation point" - given social conditions, certain amount of crime inevitable

</div>

### Penal Substitutes:

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

Instead of just punishment, use social reforms: improve economic conditions, better education, housing reforms, reduce poverty

</div>

### Classification of Criminals:

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

Born criminals, Insane criminals, Occasional criminals, Criminals by passion, Habitual criminals

</div>

</div>

---

## 🎯 Conclusion

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

Ferri broadened criminology from purely biological to sociological, emphasized social factors as primary causes, advocated social reform over punishment - influenced modern crime prevention policies.

</div>
