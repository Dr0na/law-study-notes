# 📝 Question: Write a short note on Labelling Theory. (5 Marks)

---

# ✍️ Answer:

## 🎯 Introduction

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

Labelling Theory, developed by Howard Becker (1963), argues that deviance is not inherent in behavior but is created when society labels certain individuals and acts as deviant.

</div>

---

## 📖 Core Concept

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

**"Deviance is not a quality of the act, but a consequence of application of rules and sanctions by others."**

Society creates criminals by labeling them. The deviant is one to whom label has successfully been applied.

</div>

---

## 🔑 Key Concepts

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

**Primary Deviance:**

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">
Initial minor rule-breaking - not part of self-concept - person doesn't see self as deviant
</div>

<br>

**Secondary Deviance:**

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">
Deviance after being labeled - person accepts deviant identity - organizes life around deviant role - criminal career begins
</div>

<br>

**Master Status:**

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">
"Criminal" label becomes master status - overrides all other statuses - leads to social exclusion, employment discrimination - self-fulfilling prophecy
</div>

</div>

---

## 💡 Policy Implications

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

- **Diversion programs:** Keep youth out of formal system to avoid labeling
- **Decriminalization:** Reduce behaviors creating criminal labels
- **Restorative justice:** Focus on repair, not punishment and labeling
- **Expungement:** Seal criminal records
- **Reintegration:** Help shed criminal label

</div>

---

## 🎯 Conclusion

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

Labelling Theory shows society plays role in creating criminals through labeling process. Once labeled, difficult to shed deviant identity. Criminal justice policy should minimize harmful labeling, especially for youth.

</div>
