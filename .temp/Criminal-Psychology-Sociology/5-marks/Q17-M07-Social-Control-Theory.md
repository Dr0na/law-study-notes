# 📝 Question: Explain Hirschi's Social Control Theory (Social Bond Theory). (5 Marks)

---

# ✍️ Answer:

## 🎯 Introduction

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

Travis Hirschi's Social Control Theory (1969) argues crime occurs when individual's bonds to society are weak or broken. Unlike other theories asking "why crime?", Hirschi asks "why NOT crime?"

</div>

---

> ## 🧠 Mnemonic: "ACIB"
> - **A** - Attachment | **C** - Commitment | **I** - Involvement | **B** - Belief

---

## 🔗 4 Elements of Social Bond

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

**Bond 1: Attachment**

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">
Emotional connection to others (family, friends, teachers) - care about their opinion - don't want to disappoint - attachment prevents crime
</div>

<br>

**Bond 2: Commitment**

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">
Investment in conventional activities (education, career, reputation) - "stake in conformity" - crime risks losing investments - commitment prevents crime
</div>

<br>

**Bond 3: Involvement**

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">
Time in conventional activities (school, work, sports) - too busy for crime - limits opportunities
</div>

<br>

**Bond 4: Belief**

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">
Acceptance of conventional values and moral validity of law - believe law is right - internalized morality prevents crime
</div>

</div>

---

## 🎯 Conclusion

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

**When bonds strong** → Individual constrained from crime
**When bonds weak** → Free to commit crime

**Policy:** Strengthen social bonds through family programs, school engagement, community involvement, moral education.

</div>
