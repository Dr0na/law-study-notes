# 📝 Question: Write a short note on Lombroso's "Born Criminal" theory. (5 Marks)

---

# ✍️ Answer:

## 🎯 Introduction

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

Cesare Lombroso (1835-1909), known as the "Father of Criminology," proposed the controversial "Born Criminal" theory suggesting criminals are evolutionary throwbacks identifiable by physical features.

</div>

---

## 📖 Core Theory

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

**Born Criminal (Atavistic):** Criminals are born, not made - biological throwbacks to primitive humans - can be identified by physical characteristics (criminal stigmata)

**Atavistic Features:** Large jaw, prominent cheekbones, excessive arm length, large ears, asymmetrical face, insensitivity to pain

</div>

---

## 🔑 Criminal Types

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

**1. Born Criminals:** Atavistic (most dangerous)

**2. Insane Criminals:** Mentally ill

**3. Occasional Criminals:** Circumstantial

**4. Criminals of Passion:** Emotional crimes

</div>

---

## ⚖️ Criticism & Legacy

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

**Criticisms:** Methodologically flawed, oversimplified, ignored social factors, deterministic

**Legacy:** First to use scientific methods in criminology, established criminology as science, shifted focus from crime to criminal

</div>

---

## 🎯 Conclusion

<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

Though Lombroso's specific theory discredited, his scientific approach revolutionized criminology and laid foundation for biological and psychological studies of crime.

</div>
