# Law of Contract II - Study Material

Welcome to the comprehensive, exam-oriented study material for **Law of Contract II** (LCC 0602) aligned with the SPPU syllabus.

## About the Subject
This course covers the special provisions of law that apply to specific contractual relationships, including Contracts of Indemnity, Guarantee, Bailment, Pledge, Agency, Sale of Goods, and Partnership. It builds upon general principles of contract law and emphasizes the rights, liabilities, and remedies associated with these special transactions.

## Repository Structure

The study material is structured into the following layers to facilitate progressive learning and revision:

- **`01-complete-notes/`**: In-depth explanations covering all 7 modules, including definitions, essentials, case laws, and statutory provisions. Ideal for first-time learning and long answers.
- **`02-condensed-notes/`**: Bulleted, keyword-focused summaries designed for rapid revision before exams.
- **`03-visual-aids/`**: Comparison tables, flowcharts, and mind maps for quick conceptual clarity (e.g., Indemnity vs. Guarantee).
- **`04-case-laws/`**: Module-wise digests of landmark and important case laws with concise facts, issues, and principles held.
- **`05-question-bank/`**: Categorized questions (5 Marks, 10 Marks, Distinguish Between) with structured answer frameworks.
- **`06-previous-papers/`**: Analysis and trends from past exam papers (if available).
- **`07-quick-revision/`**: One-liners, maxims, and quick reference sheets for last-minute recall.
- **`08-exam-strategy/`**: Guidelines for writing topper-quality, high-scoring answers.
- **`09-practice-worksheets/`**: Self-assessment tests to evaluate your preparation.
