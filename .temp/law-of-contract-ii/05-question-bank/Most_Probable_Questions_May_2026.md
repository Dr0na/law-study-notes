# 📕 15 Marks Questions (Long Answers) — 7 Model Answers

> **💡 Expect 2-3 long answer questions in the paper. These carry the most marks — prioritize them.**

### 🔴 1. Who is an unpaid seller? Discuss in detail the rights of an unpaid seller against the goods and against the buyer personally.
- **Module:** 6 (Sale of Goods Act)
- **Why likely:** This is the single most repeated 15-mark question across SPPU papers. Sale of Goods always carries a long answer, and "Unpaid Seller" is the #1 favorite.
- **Key Points:** Sec 45 (definition), Sec 46-54 (Lien, Stoppage in Transit, Resale), Sec 55-61 (suit for price, damages, interest).
- 📄 **Model Answer:** [Q01_Unpaid_Seller.md](15_Marks/Q01_Unpaid_Seller.md)

### 🔴 2. Discuss the rule "Nemo dat quod non habet." Explain its exceptions under the Sale of Goods Act.
- **Module:** 6 (Sale of Goods Act)
- **Why likely:** Transfer of title by non-owners is conceptually rich with 7-8 exceptions — perfect for a 15-mark essay.
- **Key Points:** Sec 27 (general rule), Exceptions: mercantile agent, joint owner, voidable contract, seller/buyer in possession, unpaid seller, finder of goods.
- 📄 **Model Answer:** [Q05_Transfer_Of_Title_By_Non_Owners.md](15_Marks/Q05_Transfer_Of_Title_By_Non_Owners.md)

### 🔴 3. What is a Contract of Guarantee? Discuss the rights of a surety against the principal debtor, the creditor, and the co-sureties.
- **Module:** 2 (Guarantee)
- **Why likely:** Guarantee is a heavyweight module. "Rights of surety" tests knowledge of Sec 140-147.
- **Key Points:** Subrogation (Sec 140), Indemnity (Sec 145), Securities (Sec 141), Contribution (Sec 146-147).
- 📄 **Model Answer:** [Q06_Rights_Of_Surety.md](15_Marks/Q06_Rights_Of_Surety.md)

### 🟠 4. Define Contract of Agency. Discuss the various modes of creation of agency. Explain *Delegatus non potest delegare*.
- **Module:** 5 (Agency)
- **Why likely:** Agency is the largest ICA module (Sec 182-238). "Modes of creation" covers 6 methods and combines naturally with the delegation maxim.
- **Key Points:** Sec 182, 6 modes (Express, Implied, Estoppel, Holding Out, Necessity, Ratification), Sec 190 (delegation).
- 📄 **Model Answer:** [Q02_Agency_Creation.md](15_Marks/Q02_Agency_Creation.md)

### 🟠 5. Define Contract of Indemnity. Discuss the rights of the indemnity holder when sued. When does the liability of the indemnifier commence?
- **Module:** 1 (Indemnity)
- **Why likely:** Indemnity (Sec 124-125) combined with the "commencement" issue and *Gajanan Moreshwar* case forms a rich 15-mark answer.
- **Key Points:** Sec 124, Sec 125 (3 rights), Commencement of liability, *Gajanan Moreshwar Parelkar vs. Moreshwar Madan Mantri (1942)*.
- 📄 **Model Answer:** [Q03_Indemnity_Rights_Commencement.md](15_Marks/Q03_Indemnity_Rights_Commencement.md)

### 🟠 6. Under what circumstances is an agent personally liable? Discuss the rights of third parties against an undisclosed principal.
- **Module:** 5 (Agency)
- **Why likely:** Personal liability of agent is nuanced. Combined with "undisclosed principal" it makes a comprehensive 15-marker.
- **Key Points:** Sec 230, Exceptions (foreign/undisclosed/incompetent principal, pretended agent Sec 235), Sec 231-233.
- 📄 **Model Answer:** [Q04_Personal_Liability_Agent.md](15_Marks/Q04_Personal_Liability_Agent.md)

### 🟡 7. Define Partnership. Discuss the implied authority of a partner. What acts fall outside the implied authority?
- **Module:** 7 (Partnership)
- **Why likely:** Implied authority (Sec 19) combined with restricted acts makes a structured, enumerable essay.
- **Key Points:** Sec 4 (definition), 5 essentials, Sec 19 (implied authority), 8 acts outside implied authority, Sec 22 (apparent authority).
- 📄 **Model Answer:** [Q07_Partnership_Essentials_Implied_Authority.md](15_Marks/Q07_Partnership_Essentials_Implied_Authority.md)
