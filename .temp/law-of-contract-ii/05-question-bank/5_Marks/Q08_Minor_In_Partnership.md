### Q8. Discuss the position of a Minor in a partnership firm.
**📖 Statutory Provision:** Section 30 of the Indian Partnership Act, 1932.
**1. General Rule:** A minor cannot enter into a contract, hence cannot be a partner.
**2. Admission to Benefits:** With the consent of ALL partners, a minor may be admitted to the *benefits* of the partnership.
**3. Rights & Liabilities of Minor:**
- Has a right to share property and profits.
- Can access and inspect accounts.
- 🛡️ **Liability:** The minor's share is liable for firm's acts, but the minor is **NOT personally liable**.
**4. Upon Attaining Majority:**
- The minor has **6 months** to decide whether to become a full partner or leave.
- If they do nothing, they automatically become a partner and become personally liable for firm debts retrospectively from the date of admission to benefits.
**🎯 Conclusion:** The law balances protecting the minor from unlimited liability while allowing them to reap the benefits of a firm.
