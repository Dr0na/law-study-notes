### Q6. Explain the maxim: *Delegatus non potest delegare*.
**📖 Statutory Provision:** Section 190 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872.
**1. Meaning:** "A delegated power cannot be further delegated."
**2. Principle:** When a principal appoints an agent, it is based on trust and confidence in that agent's personal skill. Therefore, the agent cannot employ a sub-agent to do the work.
**3. Exceptions (When delegation IS allowed):**
- 🏢 **Custom of Trade:** If the ordinary custom of that trade permits a sub-agent.
- 🚨 **Nature of Agency:** When the act is purely ministerial or clerical and requires no special skill.
- 🗣️ **Express/Implied Consent:** When the principal explicitly allows it.
- 🆘 **Emergency:** To protect the principal from loss.
**🎯 Conclusion:** While personal performance is the rule, commercial necessity has carved out practical exceptions.
