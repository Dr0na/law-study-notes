### Q2. Explain the nature of Surety's liability (Co-extensive).
**📖 Statutory Provision:** Section 128 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872.
**1. Meaning:** "Co-extensive" means the liability of the surety is exactly the same as the liability of the principal debtor (PD), neither more nor less.
**2. Scope:** If the PD is liable for the principal amount plus interest and penalties, the surety is equally liable for the same, unless the contract specifically limits the surety's liability.
**3. Example:** If A guarantees a loan of ₹50,000 taken by B. B defaults and accrues ₹5,000 in interest. A is liable for ₹55,000.
**4. Exceptions:** 
- If the original contract between creditor and PD is void (e.g., PD is a minor), the surety may still be held liable as a primary debtor in some cases.
**🎯 Conclusion:** The creditor has the right to sue the surety directly without exhausting remedies against the principal debtor first.
