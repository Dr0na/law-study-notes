## 🛡️ Q1. Define Contract of Guarantee. Discuss the various modes by which a surety is discharged from his liability.

### 1. Introduction & Definition
A Contract of Guarantee is primarily entered into to provide security to a creditor. 
**Section 126** of the Indian Contract Act defines it as: *"A contract to perform the promise, or discharge the liability, of a third person in case of his default."*
- **Parties (3):** Surety (who guarantees), Principal Debtor (who borrows), Creditor (who lends).

### 2. Discharge of Surety
A surety is said to be discharged when their liability under the guarantee comes to an end. The modes of discharge are categorized into three heads:

**A. By Revocation**
1. **Notice of Revocation (Sec 130):** A continuing guarantee may be revoked for *future transactions* by giving notice to the creditor.
2. **Death of Surety (Sec 131):** Automatically revokes a continuing guarantee for future transactions.
3. **Novation (Sec 62):** If a new contract is substituted for the old one.

**B. By Conduct of the Creditor (Very Important)**
4. **Variance in Terms (Sec 133):** Any variation made without the surety's consent in the terms of the contract between the principal debtor and creditor discharges the surety for transactions subsequent to the variance.
5. **Release of Principal Debtor (Sec 134):** If the creditor legally releases the principal debtor, the surety is also released.
6. **Compounding / Giving Time (Sec 135):** If the creditor makes a composition with, or promises to give time to, the principal debtor without the surety's consent.
7. **Impairing Surety's Remedy (Sec 139):** If the creditor does any act which is inconsistent with the rights of the surety.
8. **Loss of Security (Sec 141):** If the creditor loses or parts with any security given by the debtor, the surety is discharged to the extent of the value of the security. *(Case: Amrit Lal Goverdhan Lalan vs. SBI)*.

**C. By Invalidation of Contract**
9. **Misrepresentation (Sec 142) & Concealment (Sec 143):** Guarantee obtained by keeping silence on material facts is invalid.
10. **Failure of Co-surety to join (Sec 144).**

### 3. Conclusion
The law zealously protects the surety. Since the surety steps in to help the debtor, the creditor must not do anything that prejudices the surety's position.
