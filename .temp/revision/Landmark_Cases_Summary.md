# Landmark Cases Summary Digest

| Case Name & Citation | Module | Key Ratio / Principle Established | Exam Relevance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *Hanumant v. State of MP* (AIR 1952 SC 343) | Module 1 | Established the 5 golden principles of circumstantial evidence. | 15 Marks (Circumstantial) |
| *Sukhar v. State of UP* (1999) | Module 2 | Res Gestae: Statement must be spontaneous and contemporaneous. | 10 Marks (Res Gestae) |
| *Pulukuri Kottaya v. Emperor* (AIR 1947 PC 67) | Module 2 | Section 27 IEA (Sec 23(2) BSS): Only the distinct info leading to discovery is admissible. | 15 Marks (Confessions) |
| *Pakala Narayana Swami v. Emperor* (AIR 1939 PC 47) | Module 3 | Defined confession; and established rules for dying declaration circumstances. | 15 Marks (Confessions/DD) |
| *Kaushal Rao v. State of Bombay* (AIR 1958 SC 22) | Module 3 | Dying declaration can form the sole basis of conviction without corroboration if voluntary. | 10 Marks (Dying Declaration) |
| *Anvar P.V. v. P.K. Basheer* (2014) | Module 4 | Section 65B Certificate is mandatory for admissibility of secondary electronic records. | 15 Marks (Electronic Evidence) |
| *Arjun Panditrao v. Kailash* (2020) | Module 4 | Reaffirmed *Anvar P.V.*; certificate is mandatory unless original device is in court. | 15 Marks (Electronic Evidence) |
| *Selvi v. State of Karnataka* (2010) | Module 5 | Narco-analysis or polygraph tests conducted forcibly violate Article 20(3) and are inadmissible. | 15 Marks (Witnesses/Self-Incrim) |
| *KM Nanavati v. State of Maharashtra* (AIR 1962 SC 605) | Module 6 | Burden of proving general/special exceptions (plea of provocation) lies on the accused. | 15 Marks (Burden of Proof) |
| *Shambhu Nath Mehra v. State of Ajmer* (AIR 1956 SC 404) | Module 6 | Section 106 IEA (Sec 107 BSS) does not shift the primary burden of proof from prosecution. | 10 Marks (Burden of Proof) |
| *Pickard v. Sears* (1837) | Module 8 | Foundation of the Doctrine of Estoppel; willful representation binds the party. | 15 Marks (Estoppel) |
| *Motilal Padampat Sugar Mills v. State of UP* (1979) | Module 8 | Promissory Estoppel applies against the State acting in administrative capacity. | 15 Marks (Estoppel) |
