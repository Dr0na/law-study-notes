# SPPU Exam Answer Writing Template

This template provides the recommended structure for writing high-scoring answers in the SPPU LLB examinations.

## 1. 15 Marks Essay Questions (Target: 4-5 pages)
*   **Introduction**: Define the core concept. State its section numbers under the **Bharatiya Sakshya Sanhita, 2023** (BSS) and mention the historical equivalent under the Indian Evidence Act, 1872.
*   **Statutory Framework**: Quote the relevant sections or provision summaries. Discuss the legislative intent.
*   **Essential Ingredients**: List the essentials or conditions (e.g., res gestae, confessions, estoppel). Use a bulleted list or a diagram.
*   **Detailed Analysis**: Discuss the principles step-by-step.
*   **Exception/Provisos**: Explain when the rule does not apply (e.g., exceptions to hearsay, when confession is admissible).
*   **Judicial Interpretation**: Discuss key case laws. Format as:
    - *Case Name & Citation*
    - *Facts*
    - *Issue*
    - *Held / Principle*
*   **Conclusion**: Provide a brief summary of the modern position of law.

## 2. 10 Marks Descriptive Questions (Target: 2.5-3 pages)
*   **Introduction**: Brief definition of the term/concept.
*   **Key Section**: Cite section number and heading.
*   **Essentials**: List the core ingredients.
*   **Key Cases**: Cite 2-3 landmark Supreme Court rulings with their ratios.
*   **Conclusion**: Summary of the concept's importance in trials.

## 3. 5 Marks Short Notes (Target: 1-1.5 pages)
*   **Definition & Section**: Straight to the point definition with section number.
*   **Key Points**: 3-4 bullet points explaining the concept.
*   **Illustration / Mnemonic**: Include an illustration or a recall aid.
*   **Key Case**: Mention one landmark case.
